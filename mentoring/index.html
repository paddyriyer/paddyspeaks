Mentoring

Sometimes 30 minutes with someone who has already walked the road can save months of wandering.

After more than three decades in technology, data, architecture, cloud, BI and AI, I have accumulated plenty of experience — including a respectable collection of mistakes.

I would like to pass some of those lessons forward.

I am happy to spend time mentoring:

  • Students entering technology
  • Early-career professionals
  • Data engineers and analysts
  • People considering careers in Data, AI, Cloud or Architecture
  • Professionals wondering what to learn next
  • People navigating career changes, interviews or difficult career decisions

This is not a coaching business.
There is no sales pitch and no obligation.

Bring a question, a problem, a career crossroads, or even simply: “I don’t know what I should do next.”

That is enough.

Book 30 minutes with Paddy

If I can help, I will.

And if I don’t know the answer, I will tell you that too.
