Making petabyte-scale pipelines fast, cheap and reliable

Meta · Data Engineer (Consultant) · Oct 2015 – Dec 2021

Six years across Meta’s ads, integrity, commerce and marketing data — tuning what was slow, migrating what was stuck, and root-causing what kept breaking.

A

Performance & Cost Engineering

  • Cut a critical ads pipeline from 5 hours to under 1 hour by decomposing serial split tasks into 30 parallel threads — recovering ~4 hours of daily batch window on a revenue-reporting dependency.
  • Brought a pipeline blocked at 10× its CPU budget back within quota through query optimization, unblocking a stalled finance reporting flow.
  • Cut data scanned per run ~30× on a high-cardinality user dimension check by narrowing a full-month partition scan to a single day.
  • Rewrote a targeting pipeline from per-brand iteration to set-based aggregation — a loop over every brand replaced by a single GROUP BY, on optimized dimension variants.
  • Resolved repeated evictions from CPU overage by splitting monolithic jobs into bounded units, converting jobs the platform was killing into jobs that completed reliably.
  • Eliminated recurring out-of-memory failures on high-volume ad conversion fact tables via timestamp-partition parallelization with signal-based synchronization — jobs that could not finish now complete within SLA.

B

Query Engine Migration & Judgment

  • Migrated 89 production pipelines from Hive to Spark and Presto over 11 months — datelist, cube and funnel workloads across ads, integrity, commerce and marketing.
  • Benchmarked rather than assumed: on one workload Spark failed 40 times and ran over 50 minutes while Presto finished in under 30. Chose Presto and documented the evidence, avoiding a migration that would have shipped a slower, failure-prone pipeline.
  • Extended the funnel framework to support Spark as a query engine, making the migration path reusable for other teams rather than a one-off conversion.
  • Tuned Spark execution profiles — dynamic allocation, bucketing at 128, partition-strategy overrides, executor and driver memory — resolving conversions that failed on JVM heap limits rather than query logic.
  • Diagnosed Spark semantics gaps surfaced by migration, including map-type aggregation failures that broke cross-engine output parity.
  • Tuned Presto for high-volume commerce clickstream ingestion using batch-mode execution, recovering a consistently failing task.
