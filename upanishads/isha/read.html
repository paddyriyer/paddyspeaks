Īśāvāsya Upaniṣad

Read straight through

All 18 mantras: the Sanskrit, the transliteration and the readable English rendering. About ten minutes.

1

ईशा वास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्य स्विद्धनम् ॥

īśā vāsyamidaṃ sarvaṃ yatkiñca jagatyāṃ jagat |tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā mā gṛdhaḥ kasya sviddhanam ||

All this, whatever moves in this moving world, is to be seen as dwelt in by the Lord. Let go of it, and so enjoy it. Do not covet — for whose is wealth?

2

कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः ।एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥

kurvanneveha karmāṇi jijīviṣecchataṃ samāḥ |evaṃ tvayi nānyatheto'sti na karma lipyate nare ||

Doing your work in this world, you may wish to live a hundred years. For you there is no other way than this, and so action does not cling to you.

3

असुर्या नाम ते लोका अन्धेन तमसावृताः ।तांस्ते प्रेत्याभिगच्छन्ति ये के चात्महनो जनाः ॥

asuryā nāma te lokā andhena tamasāvṛtāḥ |tāṃste pretyābhigacchanti ye ke cātmahano janāḥ ||

There are worlds called the worlds of the asuras, wrapped in blind darkness. After death, all who slay the self go to them.

4

अनेजदेकं मनसो जवीयो नैनद्देवा आप्नुवन्पूर्वमर्षत् ।तद्धावतोऽन्यानत्येति तिष्ठत्तस्मिन्नपो मातरिश्वा दधाति ॥

anejadekaṃ manaso javīyo nainaddevā āpnuvanpūrvamarṣat |taddhāvato'nyānatyeti tiṣṭhattasminnapo mātariśvā dadhāti ||

It does not move, it is one, and it is swifter than the mind; the gods never reached it, for it had always gone ahead. Standing still, it outruns all who run. In it the wind sets the waters.

5

तदेजति तन्नैजति तद्दूरे तद्वन्तिके ।तदन्तरस्य सर्वस्य तदु सर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः ॥

tadejati tannaijati taddūre tadvantike |tadantarasya sarvasya tadu sarvasyāsya bāhyataḥ ||

It moves, and it does not move. It is far away, and it is near. It is inside all of this, and it is outside all of this.

6

यस्तु सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मन्येवानुपश्यति ।सर्वभूतेषु चात्मानं ततो न विजुगुप्सते ॥

yastu sarvāṇi bhūtānyātmanyevānupaśyati |sarvabhūteṣu cātmānaṃ tato na vijugupsate ||

Whoever sees all beings in the self, and the self in all beings, no longer shrinks from anything.

7

यस्मिन्सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मैवाभूद्विजानतः ।तत्र को मोहः कः शोक एकत्वमनुपश्यतः ॥

yasminsarvāṇi bhūtānyātmaivābhūdvijānataḥ |tatra ko mohaḥ kaḥ śoka ekatvamanupaśyataḥ ||

When, for one who knows, all beings have become the self, what delusion can there be, what grief, for one who sees the oneness?

8

स पर्यगाच्छुक्रमकायमव्रणमस्नाविरं शुद्धमपापविद्धम् ।कविर्मनीषी परिभूः स्वयम्भूर्याथातथ्यतोऽर्थान्व्यदधाच्छाश्वतीभ्यः समाभ्यः ॥

sa paryagācchukramakāyamavraṇamasnāviraṃ śuddhamapāpaviddham |kavirmanīṣī paribhūḥ svayambhūryāthātathyato'rthānvyadadhācchāśvatībhyaḥ samābhyaḥ ||

He has gone everywhere — bright, bodiless, without wound or sinew, pure, untouched by evil. The seer, the wise, the all-encompassing, the self-existent, he has ordered all things rightly for years without end.

9

अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ विद्यायां रताः ॥

andhaṃ tamaḥ praviśanti ye'vidyāmupāsate |tato bhūya iva te tamo ya u vidyāyāṃ ratāḥ ||

Those who devote themselves to avidyā enter blind darkness; into a darkness greater still go those who delight in vidyā alone.

10

अन्यदेवाहुर्विद्ययान्यदाहुरविद्यया ।इति शुश्रुम धीराणां ये नस्तद्विचचक्षिरे ॥

anyadevāhurvidyayānyadāhuravidyayā |iti śuśruma dhīrāṇāṃ ye nastadvicacakṣire ||

One thing, they say, comes from vidyā, and another from avidyā: so we have heard from the wise who explained it to us.

11

विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥

vidyāṃ cāvidyāṃ ca yastadvedobhayaṃ saha |avidyayā mṛtyuṃ tīrtvā vidyayāmṛtamaśnute ||

Whoever knows vidyā and avidyā together crosses death by avidyā and reaches immortality by vidyā.

12

अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽसम्भूतिमुपासते ।ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ सम्भूत्यां रताः ॥

andhaṃ tamaḥ praviśanti ye'sambhūtimupāsate |tato bhūya iva te tamo ya u sambhūtyāṃ ratāḥ ||

Those who worship the unmanifest enter blind darkness; those who delight in the manifest enter a darkness greater still.

13

अन्यदेवाहुः सम्भवादन्यदाहुरसम्भवात् ।इति शुश्रुम धीराणां ये नस्तद्विचचक्षिरे ॥

anyadevāhuḥ sambhavādanyadāhurasambhavāt |iti śuśruma dhīrāṇāṃ ye nastadvicacakṣire ||

One result, they say, comes from becoming, and another from non-becoming: so we have heard from the wise who taught us this.

14

सम्भूतिं च विनाशं च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।विनाशेन मृत्युं तीर्त्वा सम्भूत्यामृतमश्नुते ॥

sambhūtiṃ ca vināśaṃ ca yastadvedobhayaṃ saha |vināśena mṛtyuṃ tīrtvā sambhūtyāmṛtamaśnute ||

Whoever knows both together — becoming and perishing — crosses death through the one that perishes and reaches the deathless through becoming.

15

हिरण्मयेन पात्रेण सत्यस्यापिहितं मुखम् ।तत्त्वं पूषन्नपावृणु सत्यधर्माय दृष्टये ॥

hiraṇmayena pātreṇa satyasyāpihitaṃ mukham |tattvaṃ pūṣannapāvṛṇu satyadharmāya dṛṣṭaye ||

The face of the real is hidden by a golden vessel. Uncover it, Pūṣan, so that I, who hold to the truth, may see.

16

पूषन्नेकर्षे यम सूर्य प्राजापत्य व्यूह रश्मीन्समूह तेजः ।यत्ते रूपं कल्याणतमं तत्ते पश्यामि योऽसावसौ पुरुषः सोऽहमस्मि ॥

pūṣannekarṣe yama sūrya prājāpatya vyūha raśmīnsamūha tejaḥ |yatte rūpaṃ kalyāṇatamaṃ tatte paśyāmi yo'sāvasau puruṣaḥ so'hamasmi ||

Pūṣan, lone seer, controller, Sun, child of Prajāpati: draw back your rays, gather in your light. I see your most beautiful form. That person there — I am he.

17

वायुरनिलममृतमथेदं भस्मान्तं शरीरम् ।ॐ क्रतो स्मर कृतं स्मर क्रतो स्मर कृतं स्मर ॥

vāyuranilamamṛtamathedaṃ bhasmāntaṃ śarīram |om krato smara kṛtaṃ smara krato smara kṛtaṃ smara ||

Let my breath go to the immortal air, and this body end in ashes. Om. Mind, remember; remember what was done. Mind, remember; remember what was done.

18

अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान्विश्वानि देव वयुनानि विद्वान् ।युयोध्यस्मज्जुहुराणमेनो भूयिष्ठां ते नम उक्तिं विधेम ॥

agne naya supathā rāye asmānviśvāni deva vayunāni vidvān |yuyodhyasmajjuhurāṇameno bhūyiṣṭhāṃ te nama uktiṃ vidhema ||

Agni, you who know all the ways, lead us by a good path to what is truly rich. Keep from us the wrong that leads astray. We offer you our homage, again and again.
