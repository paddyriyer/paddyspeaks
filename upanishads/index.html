उपनिषदःThe Upaniṣads

From first questions to self-knowledge. A study edition for the curious beginner, the serious student and the scholar — the verified Sanskrit, its grammar, the traditional commentaries in their own words, and a modern voice that always says it is modern.

Further principal Upaniṣads will be added one at a time, each verified the same way before it is published. None is announced until it is ready.

Three reading paths

The Curious Reader

The Sanskrit, a readable English rendering, a plain explanation with one analogy, and one question to think about.

The Serious Student

Adds the words separated and translated one by one, a literal translation, the traditional commentary and the connections with other scriptures.

The Scholar

Adds the full grammar, sandhi and compounds, the textual witnesses and their variants, and the schools compared with their disagreements.

The paths only decide what is open when a page loads; everything is on every page. How to study.

The ten principal Upaniṣads

The ten on which the classical commentaries were written, named together in the Muktikā Upaniṣad. Why these ten, and how they relate to the hundred and eight and to the ‘early’ Upaniṣads of modern scholarship, is explained in the introduction.

Levels: beginner — Short, in verse or simple narrative; can be read with only this site's introduction. intermediate — Longer or more technical; assumes the basic vocabulary (Brahman, ātman, prāṇa, Om). advanced — Long prose with ritual background, or dense doctrine best read with a commentary.
