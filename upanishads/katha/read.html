प्रथमाध्याये प्रथमा वल्ली · Adhyāya 1 · Vallī 1

1.1.1

उशन्ह वै वाजश्रवसः सर्ववेदसं ददौ ।तस्य ह नचिकेता नाम पुत्र आस ॥ १ ॥

uśanha vai vājaśravasaḥ sarvavedasaṃ dadau |tasya ha naciketā nāma putra āsa || 1 ||

Once, hoping for heaven, the son of Vājaśravas gave away everything he owned. He had a son called Naciketas.

1.1.2

तं ह कुमारं सन्तं दक्षिणासु नीयमानासु श्रद्धाविवेश सोऽमन्यत ॥ २ ॥

taṃ ha kumāraṃ santaṃ dakṣiṇāsu nīyamānāsu śraddhāviveśa so'manyata || 2 ||

Though he was only a boy, as the cows were led away to the priests, faith took hold of him, and he thought:

1.1.3

पीतोदका जग्धतृणादुग्धदोहा निरिन्द्रियाः ।अनन्दा नाम ते लोकास्तान्स गच्छति ता ददत् ॥ ३ ॥

pītodakā jagdhatṛṇādugdhadohā nirindriyāḥ |anandā nāma te lokāstānsa gacchati tā dadat || 3 ||

‘Cows that have drunk their last water, eaten their last grass, given their last milk, and can no longer calve — whoever gives such gifts goes to worlds without joy.’

1.1.4

स होवाच पितरं तत कस्मै मां दास्यसीति द्वितीयं तृतीयं तं होवाच मृत्यवे त्वा ददामीति ॥ ४ ॥

sa hovāca pitaraṃ tata kasmai māṃ dāsyasīti dvitīyaṃ tṛtīyaṃ taṃ hovāca mṛtyave tvā dadāmīti || 4 ||

He said to his father, ‘Father, to whom will you give me?’ He asked a second time, and a third. His father said to him, ‘I give you to Death!’

1.1.5

बहूनामेमि प्रथमो बहूनामेमि मध्यमः ।किं स्विद्यमस्य कर्तव्यं यन्मयाद्य करिष्यति ॥ ५ ॥

bahūnāmemi prathamo bahūnāmemi madhyamaḥ |kiṃ svidyamasya kartavyaṃ yanmayādya kariṣyati || 5 ||

‘Among many I go first, among many in the middle. What could Yama want with me, that he will do today?’

1.1.6

अनुपश्य यथा पूर्वे प्रतिपश्य तथा परे ।सस्यमिव मर्त्यः पच्यते सस्यमिवाजायते पुनः ॥ ६ ॥

anupaśya yathā pūrve pratipaśya tathā pare |sasyamiva martyaḥ pacyate sasyamivājāyate punaḥ || 6 ||

‘Think how our forebears acted, and see how good people act now. A mortal ripens like grain, and like grain he springs up again.’

1.1.7

वैश्वानरः प्रविशति अतिथिर्ब्राह्मणो गृहान् ।तस्यैतां शान्तिं कुर्वन्ति हर वैवस्वतोदकम् ॥ ७ ॥

vaiśvānaraḥ praviśati atithirbrāhmaṇo gṛhān |tasyaitāṃ śāntiṃ kurvanti hara vaivasvatodakam || 7 ||

‘A brāhmaṇa who comes as a guest enters a house like fire itself; people calm him with this welcome. Bring water, son of Vivasvat!’

1.1.8

आशाप्रतीक्षे सङ्गतं सूनृतां च इष्टापूर्ते पुत्रपशूंश्च सर्वान् ।एतद्वृङ्क्ते पुरुषस्याल्पमेधसो यस्यानश्नन्वसति ब्राह्मणो गृहे ॥ ८ ॥

āśāpratīkṣe saṅgataṃ sūnṛtāṃ ca iṣṭāpūrte putrapaśūṃśca sarvān |etadvṛṅkte puruṣasyālpamedhaso yasyānaśnanvasati brāhmaṇo gṛhe || 8 ||

‘Hope and expectation, the good of friendship and kind words, the merit of rites and good works, sons and cattle — all of it is lost to the foolish man in whose house a brāhmaṇa stays unfed.’

1.1.9

तिस्रो रात्रीर्यदवात्सीर्गृहे मे अनश्नन्ब्रह्मन्नतिथिर्नमस्यः ।नमस्तेऽस्तु ब्रह्मन्स्वस्ति मेऽस्तु तस्मात्प्रति त्रीन्वरान्वृणीष्व ॥ ९ ॥

tisro rātrīryadavātsīrgṛhe me anaśnanbrahmannatithirnamasyaḥ |namaste'stu brahmansvasti me'stu tasmātprati trīnvarānvṛṇīṣva || 9 ||

‘Brāhmaṇa, you are a guest who deserves honour, and you have stayed three nights in my house without food. Reverence to you! Let it be well with me. So choose three boons, one for each night.’

1.1.10

शान्तसङ्कल्पः सुमना यथा स्याद्वीतमन्युर्गौतमो माभिमृत्यो ।त्वत्प्रसृष्टं माभिवदेत्प्रतीत एतत्त्रयाणां प्रथमं वरं वृणे ॥ १० ॥

śāntasaṅkalpaḥ sumanā yathā syādvītamanyurgautamo mābhimṛtyo |tvatprasṛṣṭaṃ mābhivadetpratīta etattrayāṇāṃ prathamaṃ varaṃ vṛṇe || 10 ||

‘Death, let my father Gautama be at peace about me, glad at heart and no longer angry with me; and when you send me back, let him know me and greet me. That is the first of my three boons.’

1.1.11

यथा पुरस्ताद्भविता प्रतीत औद्दालकिरारुणिर्मत्प्रसृष्टः ।सुखं रात्रीः शयिता वीतमन्युस्त्वां ददृशिवान्मृत्युमुखात्प्रमुक्तम् ॥ ११ ॥

yathā purastādbhavitā pratīta auddālakirāruṇirmatprasṛṣṭaḥ |sukhaṃ rātrīḥ śayitā vītamanyustvāṃ dadṛśivānmṛtyumukhātpramuktam || 11 ||

‘Your father will know you as before. With my leave, Auddālaki Āruṇi will sleep peacefully through the nights, his anger gone, once he has seen you freed from the jaws of death.’

1.1.12

स्वर्गे लोके न भयं किञ्चनास्ति न तत्र त्वं न जरया बिभेति ।उभे तीर्त्वा अशनायापिपासे शोकातिगो मोदते स्वर्गलोके ॥ १२ ॥

svarge loke na bhayaṃ kiñcanāsti na tatra tvaṃ na jarayā bibheti |ubhe tīrtvā aśanāyāpipāse śokātigo modate svargaloke || 12 ||

‘In heaven there is nothing to fear. You are not there, and no one is afraid of growing old. Beyond hunger and thirst, beyond sorrow, one rejoices in heaven.’

1.1.13

स त्वमग्निं स्वर्ग्यमध्येषि मृत्यो प्रब्रूहि तं श्रद्दधानाय मह्यम् ।स्वर्गलोका अमृतत्वं भजन्त एतद्द्वितीयेन वृणे वरेण ॥ १३ ॥

sa tvamagniṃ svargyamadhyeṣi mṛtyo prabrūhi taṃ śraddadhānāya mahyam |svargalokā amṛtatvaṃ bhajanta etaddvitīyena vṛṇe vareṇa || 13 ||

‘You know the fire that leads to heaven, Death. Teach it to me, for I have faith. Those who reach heaven gain immortality — this is my second wish.’

1.1.14

प्र ते ब्रवीमि तदु मे निबोध स्वर्ग्यमग्निं नचिकेतः प्रजानन् ।अनन्तलोकाप्तिमथो प्रतिष्ठां विद्धि त्वमेतं निहितं गुहायाम् ॥ १४ ॥

pra te bravīmi tadu me nibodha svargyamagniṃ naciketaḥ prajānan |anantalokāptimatho pratiṣṭhāṃ viddhi tvametaṃ nihitaṃ guhāyām || 14 ||

‘I will tell you — listen and learn from me, Naciketas, for I know that heavenly fire well. Know that it wins the endless world, that it is the ground of all, and that it lies hidden in the cave of the heart.’

1.1.15

लोकादिमग्निं तमुवाच तस्मै या इष्टका यावतीर्वा यथा वा ।स चापि तत्प्रत्यवदद्यथोक्तमथास्य मृत्युः पुनरेवाह तुष्टः ॥ १५ ॥

lokādimagniṃ tamuvāca tasmai yā iṣṭakā yāvatīrvā yathā vā |sa cāpi tatpratyavadadyathoktamathāsya mṛtyuḥ punarevāha tuṣṭaḥ || 15 ||

‘So Death taught him the fire that is the origin of the worlds — what bricks it takes, how many, and how they are laid. Naciketas repeated it back exactly. Pleased, Death spoke again.’

1.1.16

तमब्रवीत्प्रीयमाणो महात्मा वरं तवेहाद्य ददामि भूयः ।तवैव नाम्ना भवितायमग्निः सृङ्कां चेमामनेकरूपां गृहाण ॥ १६ ॥

tamabravītprīyamāṇo mahātmā varaṃ tavehādya dadāmi bhūyaḥ |tavaiva nāmnā bhavitāyamagniḥ sṛṅkāṃ cemāmanekarūpāṃ gṛhāṇa || 16 ||

‘Delighted, the great-hearted Death said to him: “I give you one more gift here and now. This fire will be called by your name. And take this chain of many colours.”’

1.1.17

त्रिणाचिकेतस्त्रिभिरेत्य सन्धिं त्रिकर्मकृत्तरति जन्ममृत्यू ।ब्रह्मजज्ञं देवमीड्यं विदित्वा निचाय्येमां शान्तिमत्यन्तमेति ॥ १७ ॥

triṇāciketastribhiretya sandhiṃ trikarmakṛttarati janmamṛtyū |brahmajajñaṃ devamīḍyaṃ viditvā nicāyyemāṃ śāntimatyantameti || 17 ||

‘Whoever has built the Nāciketa fire three times, joined with the three and doing the three works, crosses beyond birth and death. Knowing the all-knowing god born of Brahman, the one to be praised, and seeing him, he reaches this peace for ever.’

1.1.18

त्रिणाचिकेतस्त्रयमेतद्विदित्वा य एवं विद्वांश्चिनुते नाचिकेतम् ।स मृत्युपाशान्पुरतः प्रणोद्य शोकातिगो मोदते स्वर्गलोके ॥ १८ ॥

triṇāciketastrayametadviditvā ya evaṃ vidvāṃścinute nāciketam |sa mṛtyupāśānpurataḥ praṇodya śokātigo modate svargaloke || 18 ||

‘Whoever has built the Nāciketa fire three times, knowing these three things, and builds it in that knowledge, throws off the nooses of death while still alive and, past all sorrow, rejoices in heaven.’

1.1.19

एष तेऽग्निर्नचिकेतः स्वर्ग्यो यमवृणीथा द्वितीयेन वरेण ।एतमग्निं तवैव प्रवक्ष्यन्ति जनासस्तृतीयं वरं नचिकेतो वृणीष्व ॥ १९ ॥

eṣa te'gnirnaciketaḥ svargyo yamavṛṇīthā dvitīyena vareṇa |etamagniṃ tavaiva pravakṣyanti janāsastṛtīyaṃ varaṃ naciketo vṛṇīṣva || 19 ||

‘This, Naciketas, is the fire that leads to heaven, the one you chose as your second gift. People will call it by your name. Now, Naciketas, choose your third gift.’

1.1.20

येयं प्रेते विचिकित्सा मनुष्ये अस्तीत्येके नायमस्तीति चैके ।एतद्विद्यामनुशिष्टस्त्वयाहं वराणामेष वरस्तृतीयः ॥ २० ॥

yeyaṃ prete vicikitsā manuṣye astītyeke nāyamastīti caike |etadvidyāmanuśiṣṭastvayāhaṃ varāṇāmeṣa varastṛtīyaḥ || 20 ||

‘When a man dies there is this doubt: some say he still exists, others say he does not. Teach me, and let me know the truth of it. That is my third wish.’

1.1.21

देवैरत्रापि विचिकित्सितं पुरा न हि सुविज्ञेयमणुरेष धर्मः ।अन्यं वरं नचिकेतो वृणीष्व मा मोपरोत्सीरति मा सृजैनम् ॥ २१ ॥

devairatrāpi vicikitsitaṃ purā na hi suvijñeyamaṇureṣa dharmaḥ |anyaṃ varaṃ naciketo vṛṇīṣva mā moparotsīrati mā sṛjainam || 21 ||

Even the gods once doubted about this. It is not easy to know; it is a subtle thing. Choose some other boon, Naciketas. Do not hold me to it — let me off this one.

1.1.22

देवैरत्रापि विचिकित्सितं किल त्वं च मृत्यो यन्न सुज्ञेयमात्थ ।वक्ता चास्य त्वादृगन्यो न लभ्यो नान्यो वरस्तुल्य एतस्य कश्चित् ॥ २२ ॥

devairatrāpi vicikitsitaṃ kila tvaṃ ca mṛtyo yanna sujñeyamāttha |vaktā cāsya tvādṛganyo na labhyo nānyo varastulya etasya kaścit || 22 ||

You say even the gods doubted about this, and you yourself, Death, call it hard to know. Then where would I find another teacher like you? No other boon is equal to this one.

1.1.23

शतायुषः पुत्रपौत्रान्वृणीष्व बहून्पशून्हस्तिहिरण्यमश्वान् ।भूमेर्महदायतनं वृणीष्व स्वयं च जीव शरदो यावदिच्छसि ॥ २३ ॥

śatāyuṣaḥ putrapautrānvṛṇīṣva bahūnpaśūnhastihiraṇyamaśvān |bhūmermahadāyatanaṃ vṛṇīṣva svayaṃ ca jīva śarado yāvadicchasi || 23 ||

Choose sons and grandsons who will live a hundred years, cattle in plenty, elephants, gold and horses. Choose a great kingdom on the earth, and live yourself for as many years as you like.

1.1.24

एतत्तुल्यं यदि मन्यसे वरं वृणीष्व वित्तं चिरजीविकां च ।महाभूमौ नचिकेतस्त्वमेधि कामानां त्वा कामभाजं करोमि ॥ २४ ॥

etattulyaṃ yadi manyase varaṃ vṛṇīṣva vittaṃ cirajīvikāṃ ca |mahābhūmau naciketastvamedhi kāmānāṃ tvā kāmabhājaṃ karomi || 24 ||

If you think any boon equal to this, choose it, and wealth and long life with it. Be great on the wide earth, Naciketas; I will make you the enjoyer of every desire.

1.1.25

ये ये कामा दुर्लभा मर्त्यलोके सर्वान्कामांश्छन्दतः प्रार्थयस्व ।इमा रामाः सरथाः सतूर्या न हीदृशा लम्भनीया मनुष्यैः ।आभिर्मत्प्रत्ताभिः परिचारयस्व नचिकेतो मरणं मानुप्राक्षीः ॥ २५ ॥

ye ye kāmā durlabhā martyaloke sarvānkāmāṃśchandataḥ prārthayasva |imā rāmāḥ sarathāḥ satūryā na hīdṛśā lambhanīyā manuṣyaiḥ |ābhirmatprattābhiḥ paricārayasva naciketo maraṇaṃ mānuprākṣīḥ || 25 ||

Whatever pleasures are hard to come by in the world of mortals, ask for them all as you please. Here are these lovely women, with their chariots and their music — no man could win the like. Let them, my gift to you, wait upon you. Only, Naciketas, do not ask me about death.

1.1.26

श्वोभावा मर्त्यस्य यदन्तकैतत्सर्वेन्द्रियाणां जरयन्ति तेजः ।अपि सर्वं जीवितमल्पमेव तवैव वाहास्तव नृत्यगीते ॥ २६ ॥

śvobhāvā martyasya yadantakaitatsarvendriyāṇāṃ jarayanti tejaḥ |api sarvaṃ jīvitamalpameva tavaiva vāhāstava nṛtyagīte || 26 ||

These things last only till tomorrow, Death, and they wear away the strength of every sense a mortal has. Even the longest life is short. Keep your chariots; keep your dancing and your song.

1.1.27

न वित्तेन तर्पणीयो मनुष्यो लप्स्यामहे वित्तमद्राक्ष्म चेत्त्वा ।जीविष्यामो यावदीशिष्यसि त्वं वरस्तु मे वरणीयः स एव ॥ २७ ॥

na vittena tarpaṇīyo manuṣyo lapsyāmahe vittamadrākṣma cettvā |jīviṣyāmo yāvadīśiṣyasi tvaṃ varastu me varaṇīyaḥ sa eva || 27 ||

No one is ever satisfied by wealth. And since I have seen you, wealth will come to me; I shall live as long as you rule. The only boon I choose is the one I asked.

1.1.28

अजीर्यताममृतानामुपेत्य जीर्यन्मर्त्यः क्वधःस्थः प्रजानन् ।अभिध्यायन्वर्णरतिप्रमोदानतिदीर्घे जीविते को रमेत ॥ २८ ॥

ajīryatāmamṛtānāmupetya jīryanmartyaḥ kvadhaḥsthaḥ prajānan |abhidhyāyanvarṇaratipramodānatidīrghe jīvite ko rameta || 28 ||

Who, having come near the undying who never grow old, would — himself a mortal who grows old, here on the earth below, and knowing better — still dwell on the pleasures of beauty and love, and delight in a very long life?

1.1.29

यस्मिन्निदं विचिकित्सन्ति मृत्यो यत्साम्पराये महति ब्रूहि नस्तत् ।योऽयं वरो गूढमनुप्रविष्टो नान्यं तस्मान्नचिकेता वृणीते ॥ २९ ॥

yasminnidaṃ vicikitsanti mṛtyo yatsāmparāye mahati brūhi nastat |yo'yaṃ varo gūḍhamanupraviṣṭo nānyaṃ tasmānnaciketā vṛṇīte || 29 ||

Tell us, Death, that about which people are in doubt — what happens in the great passing-on. This boon goes into what is hidden; Naciketas chooses no other.

प्रथमाध्याये द्वितीया वल्ली · Adhyāya 1 · Vallī 2

1.2.1

अन्यच्छ्रेयोऽन्यदुतैव प्रेयस्ते उभे नानार्थे पुरुषं सिनीतः ।तयोः श्रेय आददानस्य साधु भवति हीयतेऽर्थाद्य उ प्रेयो वृणीते ॥ १ ॥

anyacchreyo'nyadutaiva preyaste ubhe nānārthe puruṣaṃ sinītaḥ |tayoḥ śreya ādadānasya sādhu bhavati hīyate'rthādya u preyo vṛṇīte || 1 ||

‘The good is one thing, the pleasant quite another. Both, with their different ends, take hold of a person. It goes well with the one who takes the good; whoever chooses the pleasant misses the goal.’

1.2.2

श्रेयश्च प्रेयश्च मनुष्यमेतस्तौ सम्परीत्य विविनक्ति धीरः ।श्रेयो हि धीरोऽभि प्रेयसो वृणीते प्रेयो मन्दो योगक्षेमाद्वृणीते ॥ २ ॥

śreyaśca preyaśca manuṣyametastau samparītya vivinakti dhīraḥ |śreyo hi dhīro'bhi preyaso vṛṇīte preyo mando yogakṣemādvṛṇīte || 2 ||

‘The good and the pleasant both come to a person. The wise look at them from every side and tell them apart. The wise choose the good over the pleasant; the dull choose the pleasant, for the sake of getting and keeping.’

1.2.3

स त्वं प्रियान्प्रियरूपांश्च कामानभिध्यायन्नचिकेतोऽत्यस्राक्षीः ।नैतां सृङ्कां वित्तमयीमवाप्तो यस्यां मज्जन्ति बहवो मनुष्याः ॥ ३ ॥

sa tvaṃ priyānpriyarūpāṃśca kāmānabhidhyāyannaciketo'tyasrākṣīḥ |naitāṃ sṛṅkāṃ vittamayīmavāpto yasyāṃ majjanti bahavo manuṣyāḥ || 3 ||

‘You have looked hard at desires, Naciketas, the dear ones and the lovely ones, and you have let them go. You did not take this chain of wealth, in which so many people drown.’

1.2.4

दूरमेते विपरीते विषूची अविद्या या च विद्येति ज्ञाता ।विद्याभीप्सिनं नचिकेतसं मन्ये न त्वा कामा बहवोऽलोलुपन्त ॥ ४ ॥

dūramete viparīte viṣūcī avidyā yā ca vidyeti jñātā |vidyābhīpsinaṃ naciketasaṃ manye na tvā kāmā bahavo'lolupanta || 4 ||

‘Ignorance and what is called knowledge are far apart; they are opposites and lead in opposite directions. I see that Naciketas longs for knowledge: all those desires could not pull you away.’

1.2.5

अविद्यायामन्तरे वर्तमानाः स्वयं धीराः पण्डितम्मन्यमानाः ।दन्द्रम्यमाणाः परियन्ति मूढा अन्धेनैव नीयमाना यथान्धाः ॥ ५ ॥

avidyāyāmantare vartamānāḥ svayaṃ dhīrāḥ paṇḍitammanyamānāḥ |dandramyamāṇāḥ pariyanti mūḍhā andhenaiva nīyamānā yathāndhāḥ || 5 ||

‘Living in the thick of ignorance, wise in their own eyes and sure of their learning, the deluded go round and round, stumbling this way and that, like blind people led by someone who is blind.’

1.2.6

न साम्परायः प्रतिभाति बालं प्रमाद्यन्तं वित्तमोहेन मूढम् ।अयं लोको नास्ति पर इति मानी पुनः पुनर्वशमापद्यते मे ॥ ६ ॥

na sāmparāyaḥ pratibhāti bālaṃ pramādyantaṃ vittamohena mūḍham |ayaṃ loko nāsti para iti mānī punaḥ punarvaśamāpadyate me || 6 ||

‘What lies beyond never dawns on the childish, careless and dazed by wealth. “This world is all there is,” he thinks, “there is no other” — and so he falls into my hands again and again.’

1.2.7

श्रवणायापि बहुभिर्यो न लभ्यः शृण्वन्तोऽपि बहवो यं न विद्युः ।आश्चर्यो वक्ता कुशलोऽस्य लब्धा आश्चर्यो ज्ञाता कुशलानुशिष्टः ॥ ७ ॥

śravaṇāyāpi bahubhiryo na labhyaḥ śṛṇvanto'pi bahavo yaṃ na vidyuḥ |āścaryo vaktā kuśalo'sya labdhā āścaryo jñātā kuśalānuśiṣṭaḥ || 7 ||

‘Many never even get to hear of it; many who hear of it do not understand it. Rare is the one who can teach it, and gifted the one who receives it; rare is the one who knows it, having been taught by someone gifted.’

1.2.8

न नरेणावरेण प्रोक्त एष सुविज्ञेयो बहुधा चिन्त्यमानः ।अनन्यप्रोक्ते गतिरत्र नास्ति अणीयान्ह्यतर्क्यमणुप्रमाणात् ॥ ८ ॥

na nareṇāvareṇa prokta eṣa suvijñeyo bahudhā cintyamānaḥ |ananyaprokte gatiratra nāsti aṇīyānhyatarkyamaṇupramāṇāt || 8 ||

‘Taught by an ordinary person, this self is not easily understood, for people think of it in so many ways. But when someone who is one with it teaches it, there is no more wandering about it; for it is subtler than the subtlest thing, and beyond the reach of argument.’

1.2.9

नैषा तर्केण मतिरापनेया प्रोक्तान्येनैव सुज्ञानाय प्रेष्ठ ।यां त्वमापः सत्यधृतिर्बतासि त्वादृङ् नो भूयान्नचिकेतः प्रष्टा ॥ ९ ॥

naiṣā tarkeṇa matirāpaneyā proktānyenaiva sujñānāya preṣṭha |yāṃ tvamāpaḥ satyadhṛtirbatāsi tvādṛṅ no bhūyānnaciketaḥ praṣṭā || 9 ||

‘This understanding cannot be reached by argument. It is when another teaches it, my dear, that it is truly understood. You have found it — and how steadfast you are! May we always have a questioner like you, Naciketas.’

1.2.10

जानाम्यहं शेवधिरित्यनित्यं न ह्यध्रुवैः प्राप्यते हि ध्रुवं तत् ।ततो मया नाचिकेतश्चितोऽग्निरनित्यैर्द्रव्यैः प्राप्तवानस्मि नित्यम् ॥ १० ॥

jānāmyahaṃ śevadhirityanityaṃ na hyadhruvaiḥ prāpyate hi dhruvaṃ tat |tato mayā nāciketaścito'gniranityairdravyaiḥ prāptavānasmi nityam || 10 ||

I know that what people call treasure does not last, and that what is lasting is not reached through things that pass. That is why I built the Nāciketa fire out of perishable things — and through it I have reached what lasts.

1.2.11

कामस्याप्तिं जगतः प्रतिष्ठां क्रतोरनन्त्यमभयस्य पारम् ।स्तोममहदुरुगायं प्रतिष्ठां दृष्ट्वा धृत्या धीरो नचिकेतोऽत्यस्राक्षीः ॥ ११ ॥

kāmasyāptiṃ jagataḥ pratiṣṭhāṃ kratoranantyamabhayasya pāram |stomamahadurugāyaṃ pratiṣṭhāṃ dṛṣṭvā dhṛtyā dhīro naciketo'tyasrākṣīḥ || 11 ||

You saw the fulfilment of every desire, the ground on which the world stands, the endless reward of ritual, the far shore where there is no fear, a station great and praised, wide-reaching and secure — and, Naciketas, wise and steady, you let it all go.

1.2.12

तं दुर्दर्शं गूढमनुप्रविष्टं गुहाहितं गह्वरेष्ठं पुराणम् ।अध्यात्मयोगाधिगमेन देवं मत्वा धीरो हर्षशोकौ जहाति ॥ १२ ॥

taṃ durdarśaṃ gūḍhamanupraviṣṭaṃ guhāhitaṃ gahvareṣṭhaṃ purāṇam |adhyātmayogādhigamena devaṃ matvā dhīro harṣaśokau jahāti || 12 ||

That ancient God is hard to see: hidden, entered deep within, set in the cave of the heart, dwelling in the depths. The wise one who comes to know him by turning inward leaves joy and sorrow behind.

1.2.13

एतच्छ्रुत्वा सम्परिगृह्य मर्त्यः प्रवृह्य धर्म्यमणुमेतमाप्य ।स मोदते मोदनीयं हि लब्ध्वा विवृतं सद्म नचिकेतसं मन्ये ॥ १३ ॥

etacchrutvā samparigṛhya martyaḥ pravṛhya dharmyamaṇumetamāpya |sa modate modanīyaṃ hi labdhvā vivṛtaṃ sadma naciketasaṃ manye || 13 ||

A mortal who hears this and takes it fully to heart, who draws out this subtle being, true to dharma, and reaches it, rejoices, for he has found what is truly worth rejoicing in. For Naciketas, I think, the house stands open.

1.2.14

अन्यत्र धर्मादन्यत्राधर्मादन्यत्रास्मात्कृताकृतात् ।अन्यत्र भूताच्च भव्याच्च यत्तत्पश्यसि तद्वद ॥ १४ ॥

anyatra dharmādanyatrādharmādanyatrāsmātkṛtākṛtāt |anyatra bhūtācca bhavyācca yattatpaśyasi tadvada || 14 ||

Tell me what you see that is beyond right and wrong, beyond what is made and unmade, beyond what has been and what is still to be.

1.2.15

सर्वे वेदा यत्पदमामनन्ति तपांसि सर्वाणि च यद्वदन्ति ।यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति तत्ते पदं सङ्ग्रहेण ब्रवीम्योमित्येतत् ॥ १५ ॥

sarve vedā yatpadamāmananti tapāṃsi sarvāṇi ca yadvadanti |yadicchanto brahmacaryaṃ caranti tatte padaṃ saṅgraheṇa bravīmyomityetat || 15 ||

The goal that all the Vedas proclaim, that every austerity speaks of, for whose sake people live the disciplined life of students — I will tell it to you in a word. It is Om.

1.2.16

एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ब्रह्म एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं परम् ।एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ज्ञात्वा यो यदिच्छति तस्य तत् ॥ १६ ॥

etaddhyevākṣaraṃ brahma etaddhyevākṣaraṃ param |etaddhyevākṣaraṃ jñātvā yo yadicchati tasya tat || 16 ||

This syllable is Brahman; this syllable is the highest. Whoever knows this syllable will have whatever they desire.

1.2.17

एतदालम्बनं श्रेष्ठमेतदालम्बनं परम् ।एतदालम्बनं ज्ञात्वा ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ १७ ॥

etadālambanaṃ śreṣṭhametadālambanaṃ param |etadālambanaṃ jñātvā brahmaloke mahīyate || 17 ||

This is the best support, this the highest support. Whoever knows this support is honoured in the world of Brahman.

1.2.18

न जायते म्रियते वा विपश्चिन्नायं कुतश्चिन्न बभूव कश्चित् ।अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥ १८ ॥

na jāyate mriyate vā vipaścinnāyaṃ kutaścinna babhūva kaścit |ajo nityaḥ śāśvato'yaṃ purāṇo na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre || 18 ||

‘The knowing self is never born and never dies. It did not come from anything, and nothing came from it. Unborn, eternal, lasting, ancient, it is not killed when the body is killed.’

1.2.19

हन्ता चेन्मन्यते हन्तुं हतश्चेन्मन्यते हतम् ।उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं हन्ति न हन्यते ॥ १९ ॥

hantā cenmanyate hantuṃ hataścenmanyate hatam |ubhau tau na vijānīto nāyaṃ hanti na hanyate || 19 ||

‘If the killer thinks he kills, and the killed thinks he is killed, neither of them understands. The self does not kill, and it is not killed.’

1.2.20

अणोरणीयान्महतो महीयानात्मास्य जन्तोर्निहितो गुहायाम् ।तमक्रतुः पश्यति वीतशोको धातुप्रसादान्महिमानमात्मनः ॥ २० ॥

aṇoraṇīyānmahato mahīyānātmāsya jantornihito guhāyām |tamakratuḥ paśyati vītaśoko dhātuprasādānmahimānamātmanaḥ || 20 ||

‘Smaller than the smallest, greater than the greatest, the self lies hidden in the heart of every creature. A person free of craving, whose mind and senses have grown calm, sees that greatness of the self and leaves sorrow behind.’

1.2.21

आसीनो दूरं व्रजति शयानो याति सर्वतः ।कस्तं मदामदं देवं मदन्यो ज्ञातुमर्हति ॥ २१ ॥

āsīno dūraṃ vrajati śayāno yāti sarvataḥ |kastaṃ madāmadaṃ devaṃ madanyo jñātumarhati || 21 ||

‘Though seated, he travels far; though lying still, he goes everywhere. Who but I can know this god, in whom joy and its absence meet?’

1.2.22

अशरीरं शरीरेषु अनवस्थेष्ववस्थितम् ।महान्तं विभुमात्मानं मत्वा धीरो न शोचति ॥ २२ ॥

aśarīraṃ śarīreṣu anavastheṣvavasthitam |mahāntaṃ vibhumātmānaṃ matvā dhīro na śocati || 22 ||

‘The wise, who know the self — without a body among bodies, steady among things that never stay, vast and everywhere — do not grieve.’

1.2.23

नायमात्मा प्रवचनेन लभ्यो न मेधया न बहुना श्रुतेन ।यमेवैष वृणुते तेन लभ्यस्तस्यैष आत्मा विवृणुते तनूंस्वाम् ॥ २३ ॥

nāyamātmā pravacanena labhyo na medhayā na bahunā śrutena |yamevaiṣa vṛṇute tena labhyastasyaiṣa ātmā vivṛṇute tanūṃsvām || 23 ||

‘This self is not won by teaching, nor by cleverness, nor by much learning. It is won by the one it chooses; to that one the self reveals its own true form.’

1.2.24

नाविरतो दुश्चरितान्नाशान्तो नासमाहितः ।नाशान्तमानसो वापि प्रज्ञानेनैनमाप्नुयात् ॥ २४ ॥

nāvirato duścaritānnāśānto nāsamāhitaḥ |nāśāntamānaso vāpi prajñānenainamāpnuyāt || 24 ||

‘No one who has not turned away from wrongdoing, who is restless, who is scattered, or whose mind is not at peace, can reach the self through knowledge.’

1.2.25

यस्य ब्रह्म च क्षत्रं च उभे भवत ओदनः ।मृत्युर्यस्योपसेचनं क इत्था वेद यत्र सः ॥ २५ ॥

yasya brahma ca kṣatraṃ ca ubhe bhavata odanaḥ |mṛtyuryasyopasecanaṃ ka itthā veda yatra saḥ || 25 ||

‘He for whom priests and kings alike are only rice, and death itself the sauce poured over it — who can truly know where he is?’

प्रथमाध्याये तृतीया वल्ली · Adhyāya 1 · Vallī 3

1.3.1

ऋतं पिबन्तौ सुकृतस्य लोके गुहां प्रविष्टौ परमे परार्धे ।छायातपौ ब्रह्मविदो वदन्ति पञ्चाग्नयो ये च त्रिणाचिकेताः ॥ १ ॥

ṛtaṃ pibantau sukṛtasya loke guhāṃ praviṣṭau parame parārdhe |chāyātapau brahmavido vadanti pañcāgnayo ye ca triṇāciketāḥ || 1 ||

‘There are two who drink the truth of what has been done, hidden in the cave of the heart, in its highest place. Those who know Brahman call them shade and sunlight — and so do householders who tend the five fires and those who have built the Nāciketa fire three times.’

1.3.2

यः सेतुरीजानानामक्षरं ब्रह्म यत्परम् ।अभयं तितीर्षतां पारं नाचिकेतं शकेमहि ॥ २ ॥

yaḥ seturījānānāmakṣaraṃ brahma yatparam |abhayaṃ titīrṣatāṃ pāraṃ nāciketaṃ śakemahi || 2 ||

‘May we be able to know the Nāciketa fire, the bridge for those who sacrifice, and the imperishable, highest Brahman, the shore beyond fear for those who long to cross.’

1.3.3

आत्मानं रथिनं विद्धि शरीरं रथमेव तु ।बुद्धिं तु सारथिं विद्धि मनः प्रग्रहमेव च ॥ ३ ॥

ātmānaṃ rathinaṃ viddhi śarīraṃ rathameva tu |buddhiṃ tu sārathiṃ viddhi manaḥ pragrahameva ca || 3 ||

‘Know the self as the one who rides in the chariot, and the body as the chariot. Know the intellect as the driver, and the mind as the reins.’

1.3.4

इन्द्रियाणि हयानाहुर्विषयांस्तेषु गोचरान् ।आत्मेन्द्रियमनोयुक्तं भोक्तेत्याहुर्मनीषिणः ॥ ४ ॥

indriyāṇi hayānāhurviṣayāṃsteṣu gocarān |ātmendriyamanoyuktaṃ bhoktetyāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ || 4 ||

‘The senses, they say, are the horses, and the things of the senses are the roads they run on. The self joined with body, senses and mind is what the wise call “the one who experiences”.’

1.3.5

यस्त्वविज्ञानवान्भवत्ययुक्तेन मनसा सदा ।तस्येन्द्रियाण्यवश्यानि दुष्टाश्वा इव सारथेः ॥ ५ ॥

yastvavijñānavānbhavatyayuktena manasā sadā |tasyendriyāṇyavaśyāni duṣṭāśvā iva sāratheḥ || 5 ||

‘When the driver lacks understanding and never holds the mind steady, the senses run out of control, like a poor driver’s unbroken horses.’

1.3.6

यस्तु विज्ञानवान्भवति युक्तेन मनसा सदा ।तस्येन्द्रियाणि वश्यानि सदश्वा इव सारथेः ॥ ६ ॥

yastu vijñānavānbhavati yuktena manasā sadā |tasyendriyāṇi vaśyāni sadaśvā iva sāratheḥ || 6 ||

‘When the driver has understanding and always holds the mind steady, the senses obey, like a good driver’s well-trained horses.’

1.3.7

यस्त्वविज्ञानवान्भवत्यमनस्कः सदाशुचिः ।न स तत्पदमाप्नोति संसारं चाधिगच्छति ॥ ७ ॥

yastvavijñānavānbhavatyamanaskaḥ sadāśuciḥ |na sa tatpadamāpnoti saṃsāraṃ cādhigacchati || 7 ||

‘One who lacks understanding, whose mind is never held and who is never pure, does not reach that goal, but comes back again into the round of births.’

1.3.8

यस्तु विज्ञानवान्भवति समनस्कः सदा शुचिः ।स तु तत्पदमाप्नोति यस्माद्भूयो न जायते ॥ ८ ॥

yastu vijñānavānbhavati samanaskaḥ sadā śuciḥ |sa tu tatpadamāpnoti yasmādbhūyo na jāyate || 8 ||

‘One who has understanding, whose mind is held and who is always pure, reaches that goal, from which there is no being born again.’

1.3.9

विज्ञानसारथिर्यस्तु मनःप्रग्रहवान्नरः ।सोऽध्वनः पारमाप्नोति तद्विष्णोः परमं पदम् ॥ ९ ॥

vijñānasārathiryastu manaḥpragrahavānnaraḥ |so'dhvanaḥ pāramāpnoti tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam || 9 ||

But the one whose charioteer is understanding, who holds the reins of the mind, reaches the end of the road: that is the highest place of Viṣṇu.

1.3.10

इन्द्रियेभ्यः परा ह्यर्था अर्थेभ्यश्च परं मनः ।मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्बुद्धेरात्मा महान्परः ॥ १० ॥

indriyebhyaḥ parā hyarthā arthebhyaśca paraṃ manaḥ |manasastu parā buddhirbuddherātmā mahānparaḥ || 10 ||

Beyond the senses are the objects, and beyond the objects the mind; beyond the mind is the intellect, and beyond the intellect the great self.

1.3.11

महतः परमव्यक्तमव्यक्तात्पुरुषः परः ।पुरुषान्न परं किञ्चित्सा काष्ठा सा परा गतिः ॥ ११ ॥

mahataḥ paramavyaktamavyaktātpuruṣaḥ paraḥ |puruṣānna paraṃ kiñcitsā kāṣṭhā sā parā gatiḥ || 11 ||

Beyond the great self is the unmanifest, and beyond the unmanifest the Person. Beyond the Person there is nothing at all: that is the end, that is the highest goal.

1.3.12

एष सर्वेषु भूतेषु गूढोऽत्मा न प्रकाशते ।दृश्यते त्वग्र्यया बुद्ध्या सूक्ष्मया सूक्ष्मदर्शिभिः ॥ १२ ॥

eṣa sarveṣu bhūteṣu gūḍho'tmā na prakāśate |dṛśyate tvagryayā buddhyā sūkṣmayā sūkṣmadarśibhiḥ || 12 ||

Hidden in every being, this self does not show itself; but those who see what is subtle see it, with an intellect made sharp and fine.

1.3.13

यच्छेद्वाङ्मनसी प्राज्ञस्तद्यच्छेज्ज्ञान आत्मनि ।ज्ञानमात्मनि महति नियच्छेत्तद्यच्छेच्छान्त आत्मनि ॥ १३ ॥

yacchedvāṅmanasī prājñastadyacchejjñāna ātmani |jñānamātmani mahati niyacchettadyacchecchānta ātmani || 13 ||

Let the wise one draw speech into the mind, the mind into the knowing self, the knowing self into the great self, and that into the self at peace.

1.3.14

उत्तिष्ठत जाग्रत प्राप्य वरान्निबोधत ।क्षुरस्य धारा निशिता दुरत्यया दुर्गं पथस्तत्कवयो वदन्ति ॥ १४ ॥

uttiṣṭhata jāgrata prāpya varānnibodhata |kṣurasya dhārā niśitā duratyayā durgaṃ pathastatkavayo vadanti || 14 ||

Arise, awake; go to those who are excellent and learn. The wise say this path is hard to tread, like the sharpened edge of a razor, hard to cross.

1.3.15

अशब्दमस्पर्शमरूपमव्ययं तथारसं नित्यमगन्धवच्च यत् ।अनाद्यनन्तं महतः परं ध्रुवं निचाय्य तं मृत्युमुखात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ १५ ॥

aśabdamasparśamarūpamavyayaṃ tathārasaṃ nityamagandhavacca yat |anādyanantaṃ mahataḥ paraṃ dhruvaṃ nicāyya taṃ mṛtyumukhātpramucyate || 15 ||

What has no sound, no touch, no form, no taste and no smell, what does not decay and is eternal, without beginning or end, beyond the great and unmoving: one who discerns it is freed from the jaws of death.

1.3.16

नाचिकेतमुपाख्यानं मृत्युप्रोक्तं सनातनम् ।उक्त्वा श्रुत्वा च मेधावी ब्रह्मलोके महीयते ॥ १६ ॥

nāciketamupākhyānaṃ mṛtyuproktaṃ sanātanam |uktvā śrutvā ca medhāvī brahmaloke mahīyate || 16 ||

The wise one who tells, or hears, this ancient story of Naciketas as Death told it is honoured in the world of Brahman.

1.3.17

य इमं परमं गुह्यं श्रावयेद्ब्रह्मसंसदि ।प्रयतः श्राद्धकाले वा तदानन्त्याय कल्पते ॥ तदानन्त्याय कल्पत इति ॥ १७ ॥

ya imaṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ śrāvayedbrahmasaṃsadi |prayataḥ śrāddhakāle vā tadānantyāya kalpate || tadānantyāya kalpata iti || 17 ||

Whoever, with a pure mind, recites this supreme secret to an assembly of brāhmaṇas, or at the time of the rite for the ancestors — that brings endless fruit. That brings endless fruit.

द्वितीयाध्याये प्रथमा वल्ली · Adhyāya 2 · Vallī 1

2.1.1

पराञ्चि खानि व्यतृणत्स्वयम्भूस्तस्मात्पराङ् पश्यति नान्तरात्मन् ।कश्चिद्धीरः प्रत्यगात्मानमैक्षदावृत्तचक्षुरमृतत्वमिच्छन् ॥ १ ॥

parāñci khāni vyatṛṇatsvayambhūstasmātparāṅ paśyati nāntarātman |kaściddhīraḥ pratyagātmānamaikṣadāvṛttacakṣuramṛtatvamicchan || 1 ||

‘The self-existent made the senses open outward, so people look outward and not at the self within. Now and then a wise person, longing for what does not die, turns the gaze back and sees the self within.’

2.1.2

पराचः कामाननुयन्ति बालास्ते मृत्योर्यन्ति विततस्य पाशम् ।अथ धीरा अमृतत्वं विदित्वा ध्रुवमध्रुवेष्विह न प्रार्थयन्ते ॥ २ ॥

parācaḥ kāmānanuyanti bālāste mṛtyoryanti vitatasya pāśam |atha dhīrā amṛtatvaṃ viditvā dhruvamadhruveṣviha na prārthayante || 2 ||

‘The immature chase what lies outside and walk into the wide net of death. The wise, who know the immortal that does not change, ask for nothing among the things here that do.’

2.1.3

येन रूपं रसं गन्धं शब्दान्स्पर्शांश्च मैथुनान् ।एतेनैव विजानाति किमत्र परिशिष्यते ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ३ ॥

yena rūpaṃ rasaṃ gandhaṃ śabdānsparśāṃśca maithunān |etenaiva vijānāti kimatra pariśiṣyate |etadvai tat || 3 ||

‘It is by this that one knows colour and taste and smell, sounds and touch and the joys of love. What is there that it does not know? This is what you asked about.’

2.1.4

स्वप्नान्तं जागरितान्तं चोभौ येनानुपश्यति ।महान्तं विभुमात्मानं मत्वा धीरो न शोचति ॥ ४ ॥

svapnāntaṃ jāgaritāntaṃ cobhau yenānupaśyati |mahāntaṃ vibhumātmānaṃ matvā dhīro na śocati || 4 ||

‘The one by which we see both our dreams and our waking days — knowing that as the great self that pervades all, the wise do not grieve.’

2.1.5

य इमं मध्वदं वेद आत्मानं जीवमन्तिकात् ।ईशानं भूतभव्यस्य न ततो विजुगुप्सते ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ५ ॥

ya imaṃ madhvadaṃ veda ātmānaṃ jīvamantikāt |īśānaṃ bhūtabhavyasya na tato vijugupsate |etadvai tat || 5 ||

‘Whoever knows this enjoyer of life’s honey, the living self, as close at hand — the lord of what has been and what will be — no longer shrinks from anything. This is that.’

2.1.6

यः पूर्वं तपसो जातमद्भ्यः पूर्वमजायत ।गुहां प्रविश्य तिष्ठन्तं यो भूतेभिर्व्यपश्यत ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ६ ॥

yaḥ pūrvaṃ tapaso jātamadbhyaḥ pūrvamajāyata |guhāṃ praviśya tiṣṭhantaṃ yo bhūtebhirvyapaśyata |etadvai tat || 6 ||

‘Whoever sees the first-born — born of tapas before the waters came to be, who has entered the cave of the heart and dwells there with the elements — sees this. This is that.’

2.1.7

या प्राणेन सम्भवति अदितिर्देवतामयी ।गुहां प्रविश्य तिष्ठन्तीं या भूतेभिर्व्यजायत ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ७ ॥

yā prāṇena sambhavati aditirdevatāmayī |guhāṃ praviśya tiṣṭhantīṃ yā bhūtebhirvyajāyata |etadvai tat || 7 ||

‘Aditi, the eater, made of all the gods, who comes into being as breath, who has entered the cave of the heart and dwells there, and who was born together with the elements: this is that.’

2.1.8

अरण्योर्निहितो जातवेदा गर्भ इव सुभृतो गर्भिणीभिः ।दिवे दिव ईड्यो जागृवद्भिर्हविष्मद्भिर्मनुष्येभिरग्निः ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ८ ॥

araṇyornihito jātavedā garbha iva subhṛto garbhiṇībhiḥ |dive diva īḍyo jāgṛvadbhirhaviṣmadbhirmanuṣyebhiragniḥ |etadvai tat || 8 ||

‘The fire hidden in the two fire-sticks, carefully kept like a child in the womb, to be praised every day by watchful people who bring offerings: this fire is that.’

2.1.9

यतश्चोदेति सूर्यः अस्तं यत्र च गच्छति ।तं देवाः सर्वे अर्पितास्तदु नात्येति कश्चन ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ९ ॥

yataścodeti sūryaḥ astaṃ yatra ca gacchati |taṃ devāḥ sarve arpitāstadu nātyeti kaścana |etadvai tat || 9 ||

That from which the sun rises and into which it sets, on which all the gods are fixed, that no one passes beyond. This is that.

2.1.10

यदेवेह तदमुत्र यदमुत्र तदन्विह ।मृत्योः स मृत्युमाप्नोति य इह नानेव पश्यति ॥ १० ॥

yadeveha tadamutra yadamutra tadanviha |mṛtyoḥ sa mṛtyumāpnoti ya iha nāneva paśyati || 10 ||

What is here is there; what is there is also here. Whoever sees anything like difference here goes from death to death.

2.1.11

मनसैवेदमाप्तव्यं नेह नानास्ति किञ्चन ।मृत्योः स मृत्युं गच्छति य इह नानेव पश्यति ॥ ११ ॥

manasaivedamāptavyaṃ neha nānāsti kiñcana |mṛtyoḥ sa mṛtyuṃ gacchati ya iha nāneva paśyati || 11 ||

It is by the mind alone that this is to be reached; here there is no diversity at all. Whoever sees anything like difference here goes from death to death.

2.1.12

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषो मध्य आत्मनि तिष्ठति ।ईशानं भूतभव्यस्य न ततो विजुगुप्सते ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ १२ ॥

aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo madhya ātmani tiṣṭhati |īśānaṃ bhūtabhavyasya na tato vijugupsate |etadvai tat || 12 ||

A person no bigger than a thumb dwells in the middle of the body. Whoever knows him as lord of past and future no longer shrinks from anything. This is that.

2.1.13

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषो ज्योतिरिवाधूमकः ।ईशानो भूतभव्यस्य स एवाद्य स उ श्वः ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ १३ ॥

aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo jyotirivādhūmakaḥ |īśāno bhūtabhavyasya sa evādya sa u śvaḥ |etadvai tat || 13 ||

The person no bigger than a thumb is like a flame without smoke, lord of past and future. He is the same today and the same tomorrow. This is that.

2.1.14

यथोदकं दुर्गे वृष्टं पर्वतेषु विधावति ।एवं धर्मान्पृथक्पश्यंस्तानेवानुविधावति ॥ १४ ॥

yathodakaṃ durge vṛṣṭaṃ parvateṣu vidhāvati |evaṃ dharmānpṛthakpaśyaṃstānevānuvidhāvati || 14 ||

As rain that falls on a peak runs away down the slopes in every direction, so one who sees things as separate runs after them, scattered.

2.1.15

यथोदकं शुद्धे शुद्धमासिक्तं तादृगेव भवति ।एवं मुनेर्विजानत आत्मा भवति गौतम ॥ १५ ॥

yathodakaṃ śuddhe śuddhamāsiktaṃ tādṛgeva bhavati |evaṃ munervijānata ātmā bhavati gautama || 15 ||

As clear water poured into clear water becomes just the same, so, Gautama, becomes the self of the sage who knows.

द्वितीयाध्याये द्वितीया वल्ली · Adhyāya 2 · Vallī 2

2.2.1

पुरमेकादशद्वारमजस्यावक्रचेतसः ।अनुष्ठाय न शोचति विमुक्तश्च विमुच्यते ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ १ ॥

puramekādaśadvāramajasyāvakracetasaḥ |anuṣṭhāya na śocati vimuktaśca vimucyate |etadvai tat || 1 ||

The body is a city of eleven gates belonging to the unborn one, whose awareness never bends. Whoever attends to him grieves no more; already free, he is set free. This is that.

2.2.2

हंसः शुचिषद्वसुरन्तरिक्षसद्धोता वेदिषदतिथिर्दुरोणसत् ।नृषद्वरसदृतसद्व्योमसदब्जा गोजा ऋतजा अद्रिजा ऋतं बृहत् ॥ २ ॥

haṃsaḥ śuciṣadvasurantarikṣasaddhotā vediṣadatithirduroṇasat |nṛṣadvarasadṛtasadvyomasadabjā gojā ṛtajā adrijā ṛtaṃ bṛhat || 2 ||

He is the swan in the shining sky, the giver of dwelling in the air between, the priest at the altar, the guest in the house; he dwells in men, in the gods, in what is true, in the sky; he is born from the waters, the earth, the rite and the mountains: the true, the great.

2.2.3

ऊर्ध्वं प्राणमुन्नयति अपानं प्रत्यगस्यति ।मध्ये वामनमासीनं विश्वे देवा उपासते ॥ ३ ॥

ūrdhvaṃ prāṇamunnayati apānaṃ pratyagasyati |madhye vāmanamāsīnaṃ viśve devā upāsate || 3 ||

He sends the breath upward and drives the downward breath back. He sits in the middle, small and worthy of love, and all the gods wait upon him.

2.2.4

अस्य विस्रंसमानस्य शरीरस्थस्य देहिनः ।देहाद्विमुच्यमानस्य किमत्र परिशिष्यते ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ४ ॥

asya visraṃsamānasya śarīrasthasya dehinaḥ |dehādvimucyamānasya kimatra pariśiṣyate |etadvai tat || 4 ||

When the embodied one who dwells in the body slips away and is released from it, what is left here? This is that.

2.2.5

न प्राणेन नापानेन मर्त्यो जीवति कश्चन ।इतरेण तु जीवन्ति यस्मिन्नेतावुपाश्रितौ ॥ ५ ॥

na prāṇena nāpānena martyo jīvati kaścana |itareṇa tu jīvanti yasminnetāvupāśritau || 5 ||

No mortal lives by the out-breath or the in-breath. All live by something else, on which these two breaths themselves depend.

2.2.6

हन्त त इदं प्रवक्ष्यामि गुह्यं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।यथा च मरणं प्राप्य आत्मा भवति गौतम ॥ ६ ॥

hanta ta idaṃ pravakṣyāmi guhyaṃ brahma sanātanam |yathā ca maraṇaṃ prāpya ātmā bhavati gautama || 6 ||

Now I will tell you this hidden, ancient Brahman, Gautama, and what becomes of the self when it meets death.

2.2.7

योनिमन्ये प्रपद्यन्ते शरीरत्वाय देहिनः ।स्थाणुमन्येऽनुसंयन्ति यथाकर्म यथाश्रुतम् ॥ ७ ॥

yonimanye prapadyante śarīratvāya dehinaḥ |sthāṇumanye'nusaṃyanti yathākarma yathāśrutam || 7 ||

Some embodied beings go to a womb to take a body again; others pass into things that do not move, such as trees — each according to what they have done and what they have come to know.

2.2.8

य एष सुप्तेषु जागर्ति कामं कामं पुरुषो निर्मिमाणः ।तदेव शुक्रं तद्ब्रह्म तदेवामृतमुच्यते ।तस्मिंल्लोकाः श्रिताः सर्वे तदु नात्येति कश्चन ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ ८ ॥

ya eṣa supteṣu jāgarti kāmaṃ kāmaṃ puruṣo nirmimāṇaḥ |tadeva śukraṃ tadbrahma tadevāmṛtamucyate |tasmiṃllokāḥ śritāḥ sarve tadu nātyeti kaścana |etadvai tat || 8 ||

The one who stays awake while all else sleeps, shaping one wish after another — that is the bright, that is Brahman, that is called the immortal. All the worlds rest in it, and no one goes beyond it. This is that.

2.2.9

अग्निर्यथैको भुवनं प्रविष्टो रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव ।एकस्तथा सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बहिश्च ॥ ९ ॥

agniryathaiko bhuvanaṃ praviṣṭo rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo babhūva |ekastathā sarvabhūtāntarātmā rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo bahiśca || 9 ||

As the one fire, entering the world, takes the shape of whatever it burns, so the one self within all beings takes the shape of each form, and yet remains outside them.

2.2.10

वायुर्यथैको भुवनं प्रविष्टो रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव ।एकस्तथा सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बहिश्च ॥ १० ॥

vāyuryathaiko bhuvanaṃ praviṣṭo rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo babhūva |ekastathā sarvabhūtāntarātmā rūpaṃ rūpaṃ pratirūpo bahiśca || 10 ||

As the one wind, entering the world, takes the shape of everything it fills, so the one self within all beings takes the shape of each form, and yet remains outside them.

2.2.11

सूर्यो यथा सर्वलोकस्य चक्षुर्न लिप्यते चाक्षुषैर्बाह्यदोषैः ।एकस्तथा सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा न लिप्यते लोकदुःखेन बाह्यः ॥ ११ ॥

sūryo yathā sarvalokasya cakṣurna lipyate cākṣuṣairbāhyadoṣaiḥ |ekastathā sarvabhūtāntarātmā na lipyate lokaduḥkhena bāhyaḥ || 11 ||

The sun is the eye of the whole world, yet it is not stained by what is wrong with anyone’s eyes. So the one self within all beings is not stained by the world’s sorrow, for it stands outside it.

2.2.12

एको वशी सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा एकं रूपं बहुधा यः करोति ।तमात्मस्थं येऽनुपश्यन्ति धीरास्तेषां सुखं शाश्वतं नेतरेषाम् ॥ १२ ॥

eko vaśī sarvabhūtāntarātmā ekaṃ rūpaṃ bahudhā yaḥ karoti |tamātmasthaṃ ye'nupaśyanti dhīrāsteṣāṃ sukhaṃ śāśvataṃ netareṣām || 12 ||

There is one who rules all, the self within every being, who makes the one form many. The wise who see him dwelling within themselves have lasting happiness; others do not.

2.2.13

नित्यो नित्यानां चेतनश्चेतनानामेको बहूनां यो विदधाति कामान् ।तमात्मस्थं येऽनुपश्यन्ति धीरास्तेषां शान्तिः शाश्वती नेतरेषाम् ॥ १३ ॥

nityo nityānāṃ cetanaścetanānāmeko bahūnāṃ yo vidadhāti kāmān |tamātmasthaṃ ye'nupaśyanti dhīrāsteṣāṃ śāntiḥ śāśvatī netareṣām || 13 ||

He is the eternal among things eternal, the conscious among the conscious, the one who grants what the many desire. The wise who see him dwelling within themselves have lasting peace; others do not.

2.2.14

तदेतदिति मन्यन्तेऽनिर्देश्यं परमं सुखम् ।कथं नु तद्विजानीयां किमु भाति विभाति वा ॥ १४ ॥

tadetaditi manyante'nirdeśyaṃ paramaṃ sukham |kathaṃ nu tadvijānīyāṃ kimu bhāti vibhāti vā || 14 ||

‘This is it,’ the wise say of that highest happiness, which no words can point to. But how am I to know it? Does it shine, does it shine out clearly?

2.2.15

न तत्र सूर्यो भाति न चन्द्रतारकं नेमा विद्युतो भान्ति कुतोऽयमग्निः ।तमेव भान्तमनुभाति सर्वं तस्य भासा सर्वमिदं विभाति ॥ १५ ॥

na tatra sūryo bhāti na candratārakaṃ nemā vidyuto bhānti kuto'yamagniḥ |tameva bhāntamanubhāti sarvaṃ tasya bhāsā sarvamidaṃ vibhāti || 15 ||

There the sun does not shine, nor the moon and stars; these lightnings do not shine there, much less this fire. Everything shines only after him, as he shines; by his light all this is lit.

द्वितीयाध्याये तृतीया वल्ली · Adhyāya 2 · Vallī 3

2.3.1

ऊर्ध्वमूलोऽवाक्शाख एषोऽश्वत्थः सनातनः ।तदेव शुक्रं तद्ब्रह्म तदेवामृतमुच्यते ।तस्मिंल्लोकाः श्रिताः सर्वे तदु नात्येति कश्चन ।एतद्वै तत् ॥ १ ॥

ūrdhvamūlo'vākśākha eṣo'śvatthaḥ sanātanaḥ |tadeva śukraṃ tadbrahma tadevāmṛtamucyate |tasmiṃllokāḥ śritāḥ sarve tadu nātyeti kaścana |etadvai tat || 1 ||

2.3.2

यदिदं किञ्च जगत्सर्वं प्राण एजति निःसृतम् ।महद्भयं वज्रमुद्यतं य एतद्विदुरमृतास्ते भवन्ति ॥ २ ॥

yadidaṃ kiñca jagatsarvaṃ prāṇa ejati niḥsṛtam |mahadbhayaṃ vajramudyataṃ ya etadviduramṛtāste bhavanti || 2 ||

2.3.3

भयादस्याग्निस्तपति भयात्तपति सूर्यः ।भयादिन्द्रश्च वायुश्च मृत्युर्धावति पञ्चमः ॥ ३ ॥

bhayādasyāgnistapati bhayāttapati sūryaḥ |bhayādindraśca vāyuśca mṛtyurdhāvati pañcamaḥ || 3 ||

2.3.4

इह चेदशकद्बोद्धुं प्राक्शरीरस्य विस्रसः ।ततः सर्गेषु लोकेषु शरीरत्वाय कल्पते ॥ ४ ॥

iha cedaśakadboddhuṃ prākśarīrasya visrasaḥ |tataḥ sargeṣu lokeṣu śarīratvāya kalpate || 4 ||

2.3.5

यथादर्शे तथात्मनि यथा स्वप्ने तथा पितृलोके ।यथाप्सु परीव ददृशे तथा गन्धर्वलोके च्छायातपयोरिव ब्रह्मलोके ॥ ५ ॥

yathādarśe tathātmani yathā svapne tathā pitṛloke |yathāpsu parīva dadṛśe tathā gandharvaloke cchāyātapayoriva brahmaloke || 5 ||

2.3.6

इन्द्रियाणां पृथग्भावमुदयास्तमयौ च यत् ।पृथगुत्पद्यमानानां मत्वा धीरो न शोचति ॥ ६ ॥

indriyāṇāṃ pṛthagbhāvamudayāstamayau ca yat |pṛthagutpadyamānānāṃ matvā dhīro na śocati || 6 ||

2.3.7

इन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनो मनसः सत्त्वमुत्तमम् ।सत्त्वादधि महानात्मा महतोऽव्यक्तमुत्तमम् ॥ ७ ॥

indriyebhyaḥ paraṃ mano manasaḥ sattvamuttamam |sattvādadhi mahānātmā mahato'vyaktamuttamam || 7 ||

2.3.8

अव्यक्तात्तु परः पुरुषो व्यापकोऽलिङ्ग एव च ।यं ज्ञात्वा मुच्यते जन्तुरमृतत्वं च गच्छति ॥ ८ ॥

avyaktāttu paraḥ puruṣo vyāpako'liṅga eva ca |yaṃ jñātvā mucyate janturamṛtatvaṃ ca gacchati || 8 ||

2.3.9

न सन्दृशे तिष्ठति रूपमस्य न चक्षुषा पश्यति कश्चनैनम् ।हृदा मनीषा मनसाभिक्लृप्तो य एतद्विदुरमृतास्ते भवन्ति ॥ ९ ॥

na sandṛśe tiṣṭhati rūpamasya na cakṣuṣā paśyati kaścanainam |hṛdā manīṣā manasābhiklṛpto ya etadviduramṛtāste bhavanti || 9 ||

2.3.10

यदा पञ्चावतिष्ठन्ते ज्ञानानि मनसा सह ।बुद्धिश्च न विचेष्टति तामाहुः परमां गतिम् ॥ १० ॥

yadā pañcāvatiṣṭhante jñānāni manasā saha |buddhiśca na viceṣṭati tāmāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim || 10 ||

2.3.11

तां योगमिति मन्यन्ते स्थिरामिन्द्रियधारणाम् ।अप्रमत्तस्तदा भवति योगो हि प्रभवाप्ययौ ॥ ११ ॥

tāṃ yogamiti manyante sthirāmindriyadhāraṇām |apramattastadā bhavati yogo hi prabhavāpyayau || 11 ||

2.3.12

नैव वाचा न मनसा प्राप्तुं शक्यो न चक्षुषा ।अस्तीति ब्रुवतोऽन्यत्र कथं तदुपलभ्यते ॥ १२ ॥

naiva vācā na manasā prāptuṃ śakyo na cakṣuṣā |astīti bruvato'nyatra kathaṃ tadupalabhyate || 12 ||

2.3.13

अस्तीत्येवोपलब्धव्यस्तत्त्वभावेन चोभयोः ।अस्तीत्येवोपलब्धस्य तत्त्वभावः प्रसीदति ॥ १३ ॥

astītyevopalabdhavyastattvabhāvena cobhayoḥ |astītyevopalabdhasya tattvabhāvaḥ prasīdati || 13 ||

2.3.14

यदा सर्वे प्रमुच्यन्ते कामा येऽस्य हृदि श्रिताः ।अथ मर्त्योऽमृतो भवत्यत्र ब्रह्म समश्नुते ॥ १४ ॥

yadā sarve pramucyante kāmā ye'sya hṛdi śritāḥ |atha martyo'mṛto bhavatyatra brahma samaśnute || 14 ||

2.3.15

यदा सर्वे प्रभिद्यन्ते हृदयस्येह ग्रन्थयः ।अथ मर्त्योऽमृतो भवत्येतावद्ध्यनुशासनम् ॥ १५ ॥

yadā sarve prabhidyante hṛdayasyeha granthayaḥ |atha martyo'mṛto bhavatyetāvaddhyanuśāsanam || 15 ||

2.3.16

शतं चैका च हृदयस्य नाड्यस्तासां मूर्धानमभिनिःसृतैका ।तयोर्ध्वमायन्नमृतत्वमेति विष्वङ्ङन्या उत्क्रमणे भवन्ति ॥ १६ ॥

śataṃ caikā ca hṛdayasya nāḍyastāsāṃ mūrdhānamabhiniḥsṛtaikā |tayordhvamāyannamṛtatvameti viṣvaṅṅanyā utkramaṇe bhavanti || 16 ||

2.3.17

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषोऽन्तरात्मा सदा जनानां हृदये सन्निविष्टः ।तं स्वाच्छरीरात्प्रवृहेन्मुञ्जादिवेषीकां धैर्येण ।तं विद्याच्छुक्रममृतं तं विद्याच्छुक्रममृतमिति ॥ १७ ॥

aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo'ntarātmā sadā janānāṃ hṛdaye sanniviṣṭaḥ |taṃ svāccharīrātpravṛhenmuñjādiveṣīkāṃ dhairyeṇa |taṃ vidyācchukramamṛtaṃ taṃ vidyācchukramamṛtamiti || 17 ||

2.3.18

मृत्युप्रोक्तां नचिकेतोऽथ लब्ध्वा विद्यामेतां योगविधिं च कृत्स्नम् ।ब्रह्म प्राप्तो विरजोऽभूद्विमृत्युरन्योऽप्येवं यो विदध्यात्ममेव ॥ १८ ॥

mṛtyuproktāṃ naciketo'tha labdhvā vidyāmetāṃ yogavidhiṃ ca kṛtsnam |brahma prāpto virajo'bhūdvimṛtyuranyo'pyevaṃ yo vidadhyātmameva || 18 ||
