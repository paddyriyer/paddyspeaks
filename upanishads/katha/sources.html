Corrections to the base witness

  • Mantra 1.1.17: निचाय्य मां → निचाय्येमां (typo) — The base file reads निचाय्य मां (‘having perceived me’) where GRETIL, the vishvAsa mūla and Madhva’s text all read निचाय्येमां (nicāyya imāṃ, ‘having perceived this [peace]’); Śaṅkara’s gloss in the same file comments on imām: निचाय्य दृष्ट्वा चात्मभावेन इमां स्वबुद्धिप्रत्यक्षां शान्तिम्.
  • Mantra 1.3.12: त्वग्न्यया → त्वग्र्यया (typo) — The base file reads अग्न्यया; GRETIL, the vishvAsa mūla and Madhva’s text read अग्र्यया (agryayā, ‘sharp, foremost’), and Śaṅkara derives the word from agra, ‘point’ (अग्रमिव), which gives agryā — the base file’s bhāṣya repeats the same slip of न्य for र्य in its quotation.
  • Mantra 2.1.8: अरण्योर्निर्हितो → अरण्योर्निहितो (typo) — The base file reads निर्हितो, an extra र्; GRETIL, the vishvAsa mūla and Madhva’s text read निहितो (nihitaḥ, ‘placed’), and Śaṅkara’s gloss in the same file reads उत्तराधरारण्योर्निहितः.

The corrections restore readings the base file has mistyped or spaced wrongly; each is confirmed by the other witnesses, and none adopts a variant reading.

    Uncertainties recorded in the analysis

    • Mantra 1.1.2 variant Madhva’s text lacks ‘so ’manyata’ and counts mantras 1–2 as its verse 1 (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.4 variant Madhva’s text reads tāta for tata and dadāni for dadāmi (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.5 interpretation Two readings of the first line: the boy’s worth among pupils and sons (Śaṅkara) or his place among those who go to Death (Raṅgarāmānuja).
    • Mantra 1.1.6 variant Madhva’s text reads sasyam iva jāyate punaḥ for sasyam ivājāyate punaḥ (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.7 variant The base prints praviśati atithiḥ unjoined; the vishvAsa mūla and Madhva’s text join it (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.8 variant The base prints ca iṣṭāpūrte unjoined; the vishvAsa mūla joins ceṣṭāpūrte (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.9 variant The base prints me anaśnan unjoined; GRETIL reads avatsīḥ with a short a (a slip) (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.11 variant Raṅgarāmānuja reports a reading matprasṛṣṭam (accusative) and reads dṛśivān for dadṛśivān; the witnesses of this edition read matprasṛṣṭaḥ and dadṛśivān. The vishvAsa mūla writes vītamanyuḥ tvāṃ with the visarga (spelling only).
    • Mantra 1.1.12 interpretation Whether svarga here is heaven (Śaṅkara) or release (Raṅgarāmānuja, following the Śrībhāṣya) is disputed and shapes the reading of the second boon.
    • Mantra 1.1.13 variant The vishvAsa mūla reads tvaṃ for taṃ (‘you’ for ‘it’); Madhva’s text writes bhajante etad without the sandhi. Both listed in corrections.json.
    • Mantra 1.1.15 variant punar evāha (base, vishvAsa mūla, Śaṅkara) against punar āha (GRETIL, Madhva, Raṅgarāmānuja’s text); the shorter reading regularises the metre.
    • Mantra 1.1.16 variant dadāmi (base) against dadāni (Madhva’s text, Raṅgarāmānuja); Madhva’s text spells the chain śṛṅkā (spelling only).
    • Mantra 1.1.17 variant निचाय्येमां restores the reading of GRETIL, the vishvAsa mūla, Madhva’s text and Śaṅkara’s gloss for the base file’s निचाय्य मां (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.17 interpretation brahmajajña is read as Hiraṇyagarbha’s all-knowing offspring (Śaṅkara), the individual self (Raṅgarāmānuja, Śrībhāṣya) or Hari (Madhva, citing the Nāmanirukta).

    • Mantra 1.1.18 review The extra verse in Raṅgarāmānuja’s text (after 1.1.18) should be checked against the printed Prakāśikā and other manuscripts; the edition follows its four witnesses, which lack it.
    • Mantra 1.1.19 variant Madhva’s text reads nāciketaḥ (the fire’s name) for the vocative naciketaḥ (corrections.json).
    • Mantra 1.1.20 interpretation Whether the doubt concerns the self’s survival of death (Śaṅkara), the nature of release (Śrībhāṣya, via Raṅgarāmānuja) or the Lord in the released (Madhva) shapes how the rest of the Upaniṣad is read.

    Open questions of the studies

    • Naciketas: the story and its frame: Who speaks 1.1.7 — Death’s ministers or wife (Śaṅkara), the elders at the door (Raṅgarāmānuja), or Yama’s wife (Madhva)? The text does not say.
    • Naciketas: the story and its frame: Whether ‘first of many, in the middle of many’ (1.1.5) concerns the boy’s conduct among pupils (Śaṅkara) or his place among those who go to Death (Raṅgarāmānuja).
    • The three boons: What svarga means in 1.1.12–1.1.13: the heaven of the fire-rite (Śaṅkara), the place of release (Raṅgarāmānuja), or heaven as a stage on the way to release (the verses Madhva cites).
    • The three boons: The extra mantra after 1.1.18 in the text printed with Raṅgarāmānuja: its manuscript basis needs a printed critical edition.
    • The three boons: Whether ‘prete’ in 1.1.20 means a man who has died or a self that has been released.
    • The refusal: tests of the seeker: Whether the ‘subtle dharma’ of 1.1.21 is the self (Śaṅkara) or the Lord (Madhva) — the word allows both and the context does not settle it.
    • The refusal: tests of the seeker: Which reading of 1.1.28 is older: kvadhaḥsthaḥ (the base, with Śaṅkara), kva tadāsthaḥ (Raṅgarāmānuja; noted by Śaṅkara), or Madhva’s kvādhasthaḥ.
    • The refusal: tests of the seeker: Whether Death’s reluctance tests the boy’s worth, his steadiness, or both: the text gives no reason of its own.
