शीक्षावल्ली · Śīkṣāvallī

1.1

ॐ शं नो मित्रः शं वरुणः ।शं नो भवत्वर्यमा ।शं न इन्द्रो बृहस्पतिः ।शं नो विष्णुरुरुक्रमः ।नमो ब्रह्मणे ।नमस्ते वायो ।त्वमेव प्रत्यक्षं ब्रह्मासि ।त्वामेव प्रत्यक्षं ब्रह्म वदिष्यामि ।ऋतं वदिष्यामि ।सत्यं वदिष्यामि ।तन्मामवतु ।तद्वक्तारमवतु ।अवतु माम् ।अवतु वक्तारम् ॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥ १ ॥

om śaṃ no mitraḥ śaṃ varuṇaḥ |śaṃ no bhavatvaryamā |śaṃ na indro bṛhaspatiḥ |śaṃ no viṣṇururukramaḥ |namo brahmaṇe |namaste vāyo |tvameva pratyakṣaṃ brahmāsi |tvāmeva pratyakṣaṃ brahma vadiṣyāmi |ṛtaṃ vadiṣyāmi |satyaṃ vadiṣyāmi |tanmāmavatu |tadvaktāramavatu |avatu mām |avatu vaktāram || om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ || 1 ||

Om. May Mitra be kind to us, and Varuṇa; may Aryaman be kind to us; may Indra and Bṛhaspati be kind to us, and Viṣṇu of the wide strides. Homage to Brahman. Homage to you, Vāyu: you are Brahman that can be seen. I will call you, and you alone, Brahman that can be seen; I will speak what is right; I will speak what is true. May it protect me; may it protect my teacher. May it protect me; may it protect my teacher. Om, peace, peace, peace.

1.2

शीक्षां व्याख्यास्यामः ।वर्णः स्वरः ।मात्रा बलम् ।साम सन्तानः ।इत्युक्तः शीक्षाध्यायः ॥ १ ॥

śīkṣāṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ |varṇaḥ svaraḥ |mātrā balam |sāma santānaḥ |ityuktaḥ śīkṣādhyāyaḥ || 1 ||

We will now explain the science of pronunciation: the sounds, the accent, the length, the effort, the evenness, the joining. That is the lesson on pronunciation.

1.3

सह नौ यशः ।सह नौ ब्रह्मवर्चसम् ।अथातः संहिताया उपनिषदं व्याख्यास्यामः ।पञ्चस्वधिकरणेषु ।अधिलोकमधिज्यौतिषमधिविद्यमधिप्रजमध्यात्मम् ।ता महासंहिता इत्याचक्षते ।अथाधिलोकम् ।पृथिवी पूर्वरूपम् ।द्यौरुत्तररूपम् ।आकाशः सन्धिः ॥ १ ॥

saha nau yaśaḥ |saha nau brahmavarcasam |athātaḥ saṃhitāyā upaniṣadaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ |pañcasvadhikaraṇeṣu |adhilokamadhijyautiṣamadhividyamadhiprajamadhyātmam |tā mahāsaṃhitā ityācakṣate |athādhilokam |pṛthivī pūrvarūpam |dyauruttararūpam |ākāśaḥ sandhiḥ || 1 ||

May renown come to both of us together; may the lustre of sacred learning come to both of us together. Now, then, we will explain the hidden teaching of the joining, in five areas: the worlds, the lights, learning, offspring and the person. These are called the great joinings. First, the worlds: the earth is the first sound, the sky the second, the space between them the junction,

वायुः सन्धानम् ।इत्यधिलोकम् ।अथाधिज्यौतिषम् ।अग्निः पूर्वरूपम् ।आदित्य उत्तररूपम् ।आपः सन्धिः ।वैद्युतः सन्धानम् ।इत्यधिज्यौतिषम् ।अथाधिविद्यम् ।आचार्यः पूर्वरूपम् ॥ २ ॥

vāyuḥ sandhānam |ityadhilokam |athādhijyautiṣam |agniḥ pūrvarūpam |āditya uttararūpam |āpaḥ sandhiḥ |vaidyutaḥ sandhānam |ityadhijyautiṣam |athādhividyam |ācāryaḥ pūrvarūpam || 2 ||

and the wind is what joins them. So much for the worlds. Next, the lights: fire is the first sound, the sun the second, the waters the junction, lightning what joins them. So much for the lights. Next, learning: the teacher is the first sound,

अन्तेवास्युत्तररूपम् ।विद्या सन्धिः ।प्रवचनं सन्धानम् ।इत्यधिविद्यम् ।अथाधिप्रजम् ।माता पूर्वरूपम् ।पितोत्तररूपम् ।प्रजा सन्धिः ।प्रजननं सन्धानम् ।इत्यधिप्रजम् ॥ ३ ॥

antevāsyuttararūpam |vidyā sandhiḥ |pravacanaṃ sandhānam |ityadhividyam |athādhiprajam |mātā pūrvarūpam |pitottararūpam |prajā sandhiḥ |prajananaṃ sandhānam |ityadhiprajam || 3 ||

the pupil the second, learning the junction, teaching what joins them. So much for learning. Next, offspring: the mother is the first sound, the father the second, the child the junction, procreation what joins them. So much for offspring.

अथाध्यात्मम् ।अधरा हनुः पूर्वरूपम् ।उत्तरा हनुरुत्तररूपम् ।वाक्सन्धिः ।जिह्वा सन्धानम् ।इत्यध्यात्मम् ।इतीमा महासंहिताः ।य एवमेता महासंहिता व्याख्याता वेद ।सन्धीयते प्रजया पशुभिः ।ब्रह्मवर्चसेनान्नाद्येन सुवर्गेण लोकेन ॥ ४ ॥

athādhyātmam |adharā hanuḥ pūrvarūpam |uttarā hanuruttararūpam |vāksandhiḥ |jihvā sandhānam |ityadhyātmam |itīmā mahāsaṃhitāḥ |ya evametā mahāsaṃhitā vyākhyātā veda |sandhīyate prajayā paśubhiḥ |brahmavarcasenānnādyena suvargeṇa lokena || 4 ||

Last, the person: the lower jaw is the first sound, the upper jaw the second, speech the junction, the tongue what joins them. These are the great joinings. Whoever meditates on them as they have been explained is joined with children and cattle, with the radiance of learning, with food, and with the heavenly world.

1.4

यश्छन्दसामृषभो विश्वरूपः ।छन्दोभ्योऽध्यमृतात्सम्बभूव ।स मेन्द्रो मेधया स्पृणोतु ।अमृतस्य देव धारणो भूयासम् ।शरीरं मे विचर्षणम् ।जिह्वा मे मधुमत्तमा ।कर्णाभ्यां भूरि विश्रुवम् ।ब्रह्मणः कोशोऽसि मेधया पिहितः ।श्रुतं मे गोपाय ।आवहन्ती वितन्वाना ॥ १ ॥

yaśchandasāmṛṣabho viśvarūpaḥ |chandobhyo'dhyamṛtātsambabhūva |sa mendro medhayā spṛṇotu |amṛtasya deva dhāraṇo bhūyāsam |śarīraṃ me vicarṣaṇam |jihvā me madhumattamā |karṇābhyāṃ bhūri viśruvam |brahmaṇaḥ kośo'si medhayā pihitaḥ |śrutaṃ me gopāya |āvahantī vitanvānā || 1 ||

May the one who is chief of the Vedic hymns, who takes every form, who arose from the immortal hymns — may that lord strengthen me with understanding. O god, may I hold what leads to immortality. May my body be able, my tongue speak sweetly, my ears hear much. You are the sheath in which Brahman is kept, hidden by ordinary understanding: guard what I have learned. Bringing and increasing,

कुर्वाणाचीरमात्मनः ।वासां सि मम गावश्च ।अन्नपाने च सर्वदा ।ततो मे श्रियमावह ।लोमशां पशुभिः सह स्वाहा ।आमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।विमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।प्रमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।दमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ।शमायन्तु ब्रह्मचारिणः स्वाहा ॥ २ ॥

kurvāṇācīramātmanaḥ |vāsāṃ si mama gāvaśca |annapāne ca sarvadā |tato me śriyamāvaha |lomaśāṃ paśubhiḥ saha svāhā |āmāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā |vimāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā |pramāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā |damāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā |śamāyantu brahmacāriṇaḥ svāhā || 2 ||

and quickly providing me with clothes and cattle, food and drink at all times — bring me, after that, such prosperity, rich in sheep and other animals. Svāhā. May students come to me; may they come from everywhere; may they come forward; may they come restrained; may they come at peace. Svāhā.

यशो जनेऽसानि स्वाहा ।श्रेयान् वस्यसोऽसानि स्वाहा ।तं त्वा भग प्रविशानि स्वाहा ।स मा भग प्रविश स्वाहा ।तस्मिन्सहस्रशाखे ।निभगाहं त्वयि मृजे स्वाहा ।यथापः प्रवता यन्ति ।यथा मासा अहर्जरम् ।एवं मां ब्रह्मचारिणः ।धातरायन्तु सर्वतः स्वाहा ।प्रतिवेशोऽसि प्र मा भाहि प्र मा पद्यस्व ॥ ३ ॥

yaśo jane'sāni svāhā |śreyān vasyaso'sāni svāhā |taṃ tvā bhaga praviśāni svāhā |sa mā bhaga praviśa svāhā |tasminsahasraśākhe |nibhagāhaṃ tvayi mṛje svāhā |yathāpaḥ pravatā yanti |yathā māsā aharjaram |evaṃ māṃ brahmacāriṇaḥ |dhātarāyantu sarvataḥ svāhā |prativeśo'si pra mā bhāhi pra mā padyasva || 3 ||

May I be known among people. Svāhā. May I be better than the wealthy. Svāhā. May I enter you, gracious one. Svāhā. Gracious one, enter me. Svāhā. In you, the one with a thousand branches, I wash myself clean. Svāhā. As water runs downhill, as the months run into the year, so may students come to me from every side, Ordainer. Svāhā. You are a shelter close at hand: shine on me; come to me.

1.5

भूर्भुवः सुवरिति वा एतास्तिस्रो व्याहृतयः ।तासामु ह स्मैतां चतुर्थीम् ।माहाचमस्यः प्रवेदयते ।मह इति ।तद्ब्रह्म ।स आत्मा ।अङ्गान्यन्या देवताः ।भूरिति वा अयं लोकः ।भुव इत्यन्तरिक्षम् ।सुवरित्यसौ लोकः ॥ १ ॥

bhūrbhuvaḥ suvariti vā etāstisro vyāhṛtayaḥ |tāsāmu ha smaitāṃ caturthīm |māhācamasyaḥ pravedayate |maha iti |tadbrahma |sa ātmā |aṅgānyanyā devatāḥ |bhūriti vā ayaṃ lokaḥ |bhuva ityantarikṣam |suvarityasau lokaḥ || 1 ||

Bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, suvar: these are the three well-known calls. To them Māhācamasya added a fourth, mahaḥ. That is Brahman, the body’s trunk; the other powers are its limbs. Bhūḥ is this world, bhuvaḥ the space between, suvar the world beyond.

मह इत्यादित्यः ।आदित्येन वाव सर्वे लोका महीयन्ते ।भूरिति वा अग्निः ।भुव इति वायुः ।सुवरित्यादित्यः ।मह इति चन्द्रमाः ।चन्द्रमसा वाव सर्वाणि ज्योतींषि महीयन्ते ।भूरिति वा ऋचः ।भुव इति सामानि ।सुवरिति यजूंषि ॥ २ ॥

maha ityādityaḥ |ādityena vāva sarve lokā mahīyante |bhūriti vā agniḥ |bhuva iti vāyuḥ |suvarityādityaḥ |maha iti candramāḥ |candramasā vāva sarvāṇi jyotīṃṣi mahīyante |bhūriti vā ṛcaḥ |bhuva iti sāmāni |suvariti yajūṃṣi || 2 ||

Mahaḥ is the sun, for it is the sun that makes all the worlds great. Bhūḥ is fire, bhuvaḥ wind, suvar the sun, mahaḥ the moon, for it is the moon that makes all the lights great. Bhūḥ is the verses of the Ṛgveda, bhuvaḥ the chants of the Sāmaveda, suvar the formulas of the Yajurveda.

मह इति ब्रह्म ।ब्रह्मणा वाव सर्वे वेदा महीयन्ते ।भूरिति वै प्राणः ।भुव इत्यपानः ।सुवरिति व्यानः ।मह इत्यन्नम् ।अन्नेन वाव सर्वे प्राणा महीयन्ते ।ता वा एताश्चतस्रश्चतुर्धा ।चतस्रश्चतस्रो व्याहृतयः ।ता यो वेद ।स वेद ब्रह्म ।सर्वेऽस्मै देवा बलिमावहन्ति ॥ ३ ॥

maha iti brahma |brahmaṇā vāva sarve vedā mahīyante |bhūriti vai prāṇaḥ |bhuva ityapānaḥ |suvariti vyānaḥ |maha ityannam |annena vāva sarve prāṇā mahīyante |tā vā etāścatasraścaturdhā |catasraścatasro vyāhṛtayaḥ |tā yo veda |sa veda brahma |sarve'smai devā balimāvahanti || 3 ||

Mahaḥ is brahman, the syllable Om, for it is Om that makes all the Vedas great. Bhūḥ is the out-breath, bhuvaḥ the in-breath, suvar the breath that pervades the body, mahaḥ food, for it is food that makes all the breaths great. So these four calls are each fourfold: four sets of four. Whoever knows them knows Brahman; to such a person all the gods bring offerings.

1.6

स य एषोऽन्तर्हृदय आकाशः ।तस्मिन्नयं पुरुषो मनोमयः ।अमृतो हिरण्मयः ।अन्तरेण तालुके ।य एष स्तन इवावलम्बते ।सेन्द्रयोनिः ।यत्रासौ केशान्तो विवर्तते ।व्यपोह्य शीर्षकपाले ।भूरित्यग्नौ प्रतितिष्ठति ।भुव इति वायौ ॥ १ ॥

sa ya eṣo'ntarhṛdaya ākāśaḥ |tasminnayaṃ puruṣo manomayaḥ |amṛto hiraṇmayaḥ |antareṇa tāluke |ya eṣa stana ivāvalambate |sendrayoniḥ |yatrāsau keśānto vivartate |vyapohya śīrṣakapāle |bhūrityagnau pratitiṣṭhati |bhuva iti vāyau || 1 ||

There is a space within the heart. In it is this person, made of mind, immortal, made of light. Between the two sides of the palate hangs a piece of flesh like a teat; beyond it is the passage of Indra, which goes up to where the hair parts on the crown and passes out through the skull. Through it, with the call bhūḥ, a person comes to rest in fire; with bhuvaḥ, in wind;

सुवरित्यादित्ये ।मह इति ब्रह्मणि ।आप्नोति स्वाराज्यम् ।आप्नोति मनसस्पतिम् ।वाक्पतिश्चक्षुष्पतिः ।श्रोत्रपतिर्विज्ञानपतिः ।एतत्ततो भवति ।आकाशशरीरं ब्रह्म ।सत्यात्म प्राणारामं मन आनन्दम् ।शान्तिसमृद्धममृतम् ।इति प्राचीनयोग्योपास्स्व ॥ २ ॥

suvarityāditye |maha iti brahmaṇi |āpnoti svārājyam |āpnoti manasaspatim |vākpatiścakṣuṣpatiḥ |śrotrapatirvijñānapatiḥ |etattato bhavati |ākāśaśarīraṃ brahma |satyātma prāṇārāmaṃ mana ānandam |śāntisamṛddhamamṛtam |iti prācīnayogyopāssva || 2 ||

with suvar, in the sun; with mahaḥ, in Brahman. Such a person attains self-rule and reaches the lord of the mind, becoming lord of speech, of sight, of hearing, of understanding. Then this follows: Brahman, whose body is space, whose nature is truth, whose delight is in life’s breaths, whose mind is joy, full of peace, deathless. Meditate on it so, Prācīnayogya.

1.7

पृथिव्यन्तरिक्षं द्यौर्दिशोऽवान्तरदिशः ।अग्निर्वायुरादित्यश्चन्द्रमा नक्षत्राणि ।आप ओषधयो वनस्पतय आकाश आत्मा ।इत्यधिभूतम् ।अथाध्यात्मम् ।प्राणो व्यानोऽपान उदानः समानः ।चक्षुः श्रोत्रं मनो वाक् त्वक् ।चर्म मांसंस्नावास्थि मज्जा ।एतदधिविधाय ऋषिरवोचत् ।पाङ्क्तं वा इदं सर्वम् ।पाङ्क्तेनैव पाङ्क्तं स्पृणोतीति ॥ १ ॥

pṛthivyantarikṣaṃ dyaurdiśo'vāntaradiśaḥ |agnirvāyurādityaścandramā nakṣatrāṇi |āpa oṣadhayo vanaspataya ākāśa ātmā |ityadhibhūtam |athādhyātmam |prāṇo vyāno'pāna udānaḥ samānaḥ |cakṣuḥ śrotraṃ mano vāk tvak |carma māṃsaṃsnāvāsthi majjā |etadadhividhāya ṛṣiravocat |pāṅktaṃ vā idaṃ sarvam |pāṅktenaiva pāṅktaṃ spṛṇotīti || 1 ||

Earth, the space between, the sky, the four quarters and the quarters between them; fire, wind, sun, moon and stars; water, plants, trees, space and the body of the whole — that is the outer world. Now the inner: the five breaths; eye, ear, mind, speech and touch; skin, flesh, sinew, bone and marrow. Having arranged all this, the seer said: ‘All this is in fives. With the fives within, one fills out the fives without.’

1.8

ओमिति ब्रह्म ।ओमितीदं सर्वम् ।ओमित्येतदनुकृतिर्ह स्म वा अप्यो श्रावयेत्याश्रावयन्ति ।ओमिति सामानि गायन्ति ।ॐ शोमिति शस्त्राणि शंसन्ति ।ओमित्यध्वर्युः प्रतिगरं प्रतिगृणाति ।ओमिति ब्रह्मा प्रसौति ।ओमित्यग्निहोत्रमनुजानाति ।ओमिति ब्राह्मणः प्रवक्ष्यन्नाह ब्रह्मोपाप्नवानीति ।ब्रह्मैवोपाप्नोति ॥ १ ॥

Om is Brahman. Om is all this. Om is the word of assent, as everyone knows. With ‘O, make heard’ the call is made; with Om the chants are sung; with ‘Om śom’ the hymns are recited; with Om the adhvaryu responds; with Om the brahman priest gives leave; with Om the fire-offering is permitted. With Om a brāhmaṇa about to recite says, ‘May I reach brahman’ — and he does reach it.

Practise what is right — and study and teach. Practise truth — and study and teach. Austerity, self-restraint, calm of mind — and with each, study and teach. Keep the sacred fires and the daily offering, honour guests, attend to the dealings of human life, raise children and grandchildren — and with all of these, study and teach. Rāthītara, called Truthful, says truth is what matters. Pauruśiṣṭi, ever austere, says austerity. Nāka Maudgalya says study and teaching alone: for that is austerity — that is austerity.

ahaṃ vṛkṣasya rerivā |kīrtiḥ pṛṣṭhaṃ gireriva |ūrdhvapavitro vājinīva svamṛtamasmi |draviṇaṃ savarcasam |sumedhā amṛtokṣitaḥ |iti triśaṅkorvedānuvacanam || 1 ||

I am the one who shakes the tree. My name stands high as a mountain’s ridge. Made pure from on high, I am the pure immortal, as it shines in the sun. I am treasure, full of light. I am wise, immortal, untouched by decay. — So spoke Triśaṅku when he had come to know.

1.11

वेदमनूच्याचार्योऽन्तेवासिनमनुशास्ति ।सत्यं वद ।धर्मं चर ।स्वाध्यायान्मा प्रमदः ।आचार्याय प्रियं धनमाहृत्य प्रजातन्तुं मा व्यवच्छेत्सीः ।सत्यान्न प्रमदितव्यम् ।धर्मान्न प्रमदितव्यम् ।कुशलान्न प्रमदितव्यम् ।भूत्यै न प्रमदितव्यम् ।स्वाध्यायप्रवचनाभ्यां न प्रमदितव्यम् ॥ १ ॥

vedamanūcyācāryo'ntevāsinamanuśāsti |satyaṃ vada |dharmaṃ cara |svādhyāyānmā pramadaḥ |ācāryāya priyaṃ dhanamāhṛtya prajātantuṃ mā vyavacchetsīḥ |satyānna pramaditavyam |dharmānna pramaditavyam |kuśalānna pramaditavyam |bhūtyai na pramaditavyam |svādhyāyapravacanābhyāṃ na pramaditavyam || 1 ||

When he has taught the Veda, the teacher gives his pupil these instructions. Speak the truth. Live by dharma. Never neglect your study. Bring your teacher the gift he values, and do not let your family line break off. Do not drift from truth. Do not drift from dharma. Do not neglect what keeps you well. Do not neglect what brings prosperity. Do not neglect study and teaching.

देवपितृकार्याभ्यां न प्रमदितव्यम् ।मातृदेवो भव ।पितृदेवो भव ।आचार्यदेवो भव ।अतिथिदेवो भव ।यान्यनवद्यानि कर्माणि ।तानि सेवितव्यानि ।नो इतराणि ।यान्यस्माकं सुचरितानि ।तानि त्वयोपास्यानि ॥ २ ॥

Do not neglect what you owe to the gods and to the ancestors. Let your mother be a god to you. Let your father be a god to you. Let your teacher be a god to you. Let your guest be a god to you. Do the deeds that are blameless, and no others. Follow what we have done well —

and nothing else. When people wiser than we are come, give them a seat and let them rest. Give with faith; never give without it. Give generously, give modestly, give with awe, give with understanding. And if you are ever unsure what to do — about a rite, or about how to behave —

ये तत्र ब्राह्मणाः सम्मर्शिनः ।युक्ता आयुक्ताः ।अलूक्षा धर्मकामाः स्युः ।यथा ते तत्र वर्तेरन् ।तथा तत्र वर्तेथाः ।अथाभ्याख्यातेषु ।ये तत्र ब्राह्मणाः सम्मर्शिनः ।युक्ता आयुक्ताः ।अलूक्षा धर्मकामाः स्युः ।यथा ते तेषु वर्तेरन् ।तथा तेषु वर्तेथाः ।एष आदेशः ।एष उपदेशः ।एषा वेदोपनिषत् ।एतदनुशासनम् ।एवमुपासितव्यम् ।एवमु चैतदुपास्यम् ॥ ४ ॥

then look to the wise people around you — thoughtful, committed, independent, gentle, and wanting only what is right — and do as they would do. And if it is a matter of people accused of some fault, look again to such people, and treat the accused as they would. This is the command. This is the counsel. This is the heart of the Veda. This is the teaching. Live this way. Truly, live this way.

ब्रह्मानन्दवल्ली · Brahmānandavallī

ॐ सह नाववतु । सह नौ भुनक्तु । सह वीर्यं करवावहै ।तेजस्वि नावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै ॥ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

2.1

ब्रह्मविदाप्नोति परम् ।तदेषाभ्युक्ता ।सत्यं ज्ञानमनन्तं ब्रह्म ।यो वेद निहितं गुहायां परमे व्योमन् ।सोऽश्नुते सर्वान् कामान् सह ।ब्रह्मणा विपश्चितेति ।तस्माद्वा एतस्मादात्मन आकाशः सम्भूतः ।आकाशाद्वायुः ।वायोरग्निः ।अग्नेरापः ।अद्भ्यः पृथिवी ।पृथिव्या ओषधयः ।ओषधीभ्योऽन्नम् ।अन्नात्पुरुषः ।स वा एष पुरुषोऽन्नरसमयः ।तस्येदमेव शिरः ।अयं दक्षिणः पक्षः ।अयमुत्तरः पक्षः ।अयमात्मा ।इदं पुच्छं प्रतिष्ठा ।तदप्येष श्लोको भवति ॥ १ ॥

brahmavidāpnoti param |tadeṣābhyuktā |satyaṃ jñānamanantaṃ brahma |yo veda nihitaṃ guhāyāṃ parame vyoman |so'śnute sarvān kāmān saha |brahmaṇā vipaściteti |tasmādvā etasmādātmana ākāśaḥ sambhūtaḥ |ākāśādvāyuḥ |vāyoragniḥ |agnerāpaḥ |adbhyaḥ pṛthivī |pṛthivyā oṣadhayaḥ |oṣadhībhyo'nnam |annātpuruṣaḥ |sa vā eṣa puruṣo'nnarasamayaḥ |tasyedameva śiraḥ |ayaṃ dakṣiṇaḥ pakṣaḥ |ayamuttaraḥ pakṣaḥ |ayamātmā |idaṃ pucchaṃ pratiṣṭhā |tadapyeṣa śloko bhavati || 1 ||

Whoever knows Brahman reaches the highest. A verse says: Brahman is the real, is knowing, is without limit. One who knows it as hidden in the cave of the heart, in the highest space, has all that is desired, all at once, as or with Brahman the all-knowing. From this Self came space; from space, air; from air, fire; from fire, water; from water, earth; from earth, plants; from plants, food; from food, the human being. This human being is made of the essence of food. Its head is this head, its right side this right arm, its left side this left arm, its body this trunk, its tail and support this lower part. There is a verse on this too.

2.2

अन्नाद्वै प्रजाः प्रजायन्ते ।याः काश्च पृथिवीं श्रिताः ।अथो अन्नेनैव जीवन्ति ।अथैनदपि यन्त्यन्ततः ।अन्नं हि भूतानां ज्येष्ठम् ।तस्मात्सर्वौषधमुच्यते ।सर्वं वै तेऽन्नमाप्नुवन्ति ।येऽन्नं ब्रह्मोपासते ।अन्नं हि भूतानां ज्येष्ठम् ।तस्मात्सर्वौषधमुच्यते ।अन्नाद्भूतानि जायन्ते ।जातान्यन्नेन वर्धन्ते ।अद्यतेऽत्ति च भूतानि ।तस्मादन्नं तदुच्यत इति ।तस्माद्वा एतस्मादन्नरसमयात् ।अन्योऽन्तर आत्मा प्राणमयः ।तेनैष पूर्णः ।स वा एष पुरुषविध एव ।तस्य पुरुषविधताम् ।अन्वयं पुरुषविधः ।तस्य प्राण एव शिरः ।व्यानो दक्षिणः पक्षः ।अपान उत्तरः पक्षः ।आकाश आत्मा ।पृथिवी पुच्छं प्रतिष्ठा ।तदप्येष श्लोको भवति ॥ १ ॥

annādvai prajāḥ prajāyante |yāḥ kāśca pṛthivīṃ śritāḥ |atho annenaiva jīvanti |athainadapi yantyantataḥ |annaṃ hi bhūtānāṃ jyeṣṭham |tasmātsarvauṣadhamucyate |sarvaṃ vai te'nnamāpnuvanti |ye'nnaṃ brahmopāsate |annaṃ hi bhūtānāṃ jyeṣṭham |tasmātsarvauṣadhamucyate |annādbhūtāni jāyante |jātānyannena vardhante |adyate'tti ca bhūtāni |tasmādannaṃ taducyata iti |tasmādvā etasmādannarasamayāt |anyo'ntara ātmā prāṇamayaḥ |tenaiṣa pūrṇaḥ |sa vā eṣa puruṣavidha eva |tasya puruṣavidhatām |anvayaṃ puruṣavidhaḥ |tasya prāṇa eva śiraḥ |vyāno dakṣiṇaḥ pakṣaḥ |apāna uttaraḥ pakṣaḥ |ākāśa ātmā |pṛthivī pucchaṃ pratiṣṭhā |tadapyeṣa śloko bhavati || 1 ||

All creatures that live on the earth are born of food, live by food, and return to food at the end. Food came first, before the beings; so it is called the medicine for all. Those who revere food as Brahman never lack food. Beings are born of food and grow by food; food is eaten by them and in the end eats them, and that is why it is called anna. Within the self made of food there is another self, made of breath, which fills it. It too has a person’s shape, following the shape of the first: the out-breath is its head, the diffused breath its right side, the down-breath its left, space its body, earth its tail and support. There is a verse on this too.

2.3

प्राणं देवा अनु प्राणन्ति ।मनुष्याः पशवश्च ये ।प्राणो हि भूतानामायुः ।तस्मात्सर्वायुषमुच्यते ।सर्वमेव त आयुर्यन्ति ।ये प्राणं ब्रह्मोपासते ।प्राणो हि भूतानामायुः ।तस्मात्सर्वायुषमुच्यत इति ।तस्यैष एव शारीर आत्मा ।यः पूर्वस्य ।तस्माद्वा एतस्मात्प्राणमयात् ।अन्योऽन्तर आत्मा मनोमयः ।तेनैष पूर्णः ।स वा एष पुरुषविध एव ।तस्य पुरुषविधताम् ।अन्वयं पुरुषविधः ।तस्य यजुरेव शिरः ।ऋग्दक्षिणः पक्षः ।सामोत्तरः पक्षः ।आदेश आत्मा ।अथर्वाङ्गिरसः पुच्छं प्रतिष्ठा ।तदप्येष श्लोको भवति ॥ १ ॥

prāṇaṃ devā anu prāṇanti |manuṣyāḥ paśavaśca ye |prāṇo hi bhūtānāmāyuḥ |tasmātsarvāyuṣamucyate |sarvameva ta āyuryanti |ye prāṇaṃ brahmopāsate |prāṇo hi bhūtānāmāyuḥ |tasmātsarvāyuṣamucyata iti |tasyaiṣa eva śārīra ātmā |yaḥ pūrvasya |tasmādvā etasmātprāṇamayāt |anyo'ntara ātmā manomayaḥ |tenaiṣa pūrṇaḥ |sa vā eṣa puruṣavidha eva |tasya puruṣavidhatām |anvayaṃ puruṣavidhaḥ |tasya yajureva śiraḥ |ṛgdakṣiṇaḥ pakṣaḥ |sāmottaraḥ pakṣaḥ |ādeśa ātmā |atharvāṅgirasaḥ pucchaṃ pratiṣṭhā |tadapyeṣa śloko bhavati || 1 ||

The gods breathe by breath, and so do humans and animals. Breath is the life of every being, so it is called the life of all. Those who revere breath as Brahman live out their full span. This self of breath is the embodied self of the one before it. Within the self of breath there is another self, made of mind, which fills it. It too has a person’s shape, following the shape of the self of breath: the Yajus is its head, the Ṛc its right side, the Sāman its left, instruction its body, the hymns of Atharvan and Aṅgiras its tail and support. There is a verse on this too.

‘Words turn back from it, with the mind, without reaching it. One who knows the bliss of Brahman is never afraid.’ This is the embodied self of the one before. Within the self made of mind there is another self, made of understanding, and it fills the mind-self. It too has the shape of a person, following the shape of the one before: faith is its head, the right is its right wing, truth its left wing, concentration its body, and the great its tail and foundation. There is a verse about this too.

‘Understanding carries out the sacrifice, and all our acts. All the gods revere understanding as Brahman, the first. One who knows understanding as Brahman, and never strays from it, leaves every evil behind in the body and attains all that is desired.’ This is the embodied self of the one before. Within the self made of understanding there is another self, made of bliss, and it fills the one before. It too has the shape of a person: what is dear is its head, delight its right wing, great delight its left wing, bliss its body, and Brahman its tail and foundation. There is a verse about this too.

‘One who thinks Brahman does not exist becomes as if he did not exist himself. One who knows that Brahman is — him they know as truly being.’ This is the embodied self of the one before. Now the questions that follow: Does one who does not know go to that world after death? Or does one who knows reach it? He wished: ‘Let me be many; let me be born.’ He brooded in heat, and from that brooding he brought forth all this, everything there is. Having brought it forth, he entered into it. Having entered it, he became both the formed and the formless, the defined and the undefined, what rests on something and what rests on nothing, the conscious and the unconscious, the true and the false. As the true he became all that is; and so they call it ‘the true’. There is a verse about this too.

‘In the beginning this was non-being; from it being was born. It made itself by itself — and so it is called the self-made.’ That self-made is the essence, the very savour of things: whoever tastes it is filled with joy. Who could breathe out, who could breathe in, if this bliss were not there in the space of the heart? It is this that gives joy. When a person finds a fearless footing in this invisible, bodiless, indefinable, unsupported one, he has reached fearlessness. But the moment he makes the slightest gap in it, fear comes. It is that very reality that becomes fear for one who knows but does not reflect. There is a verse about this too.

That is Brahman.’ Bhṛgu practised austerity, and when he had practised it —

3.2

अन्नं ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् ।अन्नाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते ।अन्नेन जातानि जीवन्ति ।अन्नं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति ।तद्विज्ञाय ।पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार ।अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति ।तं होवाच ।तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व ।तपो ब्रह्मेति ।स तपोऽतप्यत ।स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ १ ॥

annaṃ brahmeti vyajānāt |annāddhyeva khalvimāni bhūtāni jāyante |annena jātāni jīvanti |annaṃ prayantyabhisaṃviśantīti |tadvijñāya |punareva varuṇaṃ pitaramupasasāra |adhīhi bhagavo brahmeti |taṃ hovāca |tapasā brahma vijijñāsasva |tapo brahmeti |sa tapo'tapyata |sa tapastaptvā || 1 ||

Having practised austerity, he understood that food is Brahman: for it is from food that these beings are born, by food that they live once born, and into food that they pass and merge at the end. Having understood this, he went to his father Varuṇa again and said, ‘Sir, teach me Brahman.’ Varuṇa told him: ‘Seek to know Brahman by austerity. Austerity is Brahman.’ He practised austerity, and when he had practised it —

3.3

प्राणो ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् ।प्राणाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते ।प्राणेन जातानि जीवन्ति ।प्राणं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति ।तद्विज्ञाय ।पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार ।अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति ।तं होवाच ।तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व ।तपो ब्रह्मेति ।स तपोऽतप्यत ।स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ १ ॥

prāṇo brahmeti vyajānāt |prāṇāddhyeva khalvimāni bhūtāni jāyante |prāṇena jātāni jīvanti |prāṇaṃ prayantyabhisaṃviśantīti |tadvijñāya |punareva varuṇaṃ pitaramupasasāra |adhīhi bhagavo brahmeti |taṃ hovāca |tapasā brahma vijijñāsasva |tapo brahmeti |sa tapo'tapyata |sa tapastaptvā || 1 ||

Having practised austerity, he understood that breath is Brahman: for it is from breath that these beings are born, by breath that they live once born, and into breath that they pass and merge at the end. Having understood this, he went to his father Varuṇa again and said, ‘Sir, teach me Brahman.’ Varuṇa told him: ‘Seek to know Brahman by austerity. Austerity is Brahman.’ He practised austerity, and when he had practised it —

3.4

मनो ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् ।मनसो ह्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते ।मनसा जातानि जीवन्ति ।मनः प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति ।तद्विज्ञाय ।पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार ।अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति ।तं होवाच ।तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व ।तपो ब्रह्मेति ।स तपोऽतप्यत ।स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ १ ॥

mano brahmeti vyajānāt |manaso hyeva khalvimāni bhūtāni jāyante |manasā jātāni jīvanti |manaḥ prayantyabhisaṃviśantīti |tadvijñāya |punareva varuṇaṃ pitaramupasasāra |adhīhi bhagavo brahmeti |taṃ hovāca |tapasā brahma vijijñāsasva |tapo brahmeti |sa tapo'tapyata |sa tapastaptvā || 1 ||

Having practised austerity, he understood that mind is Brahman: for it is from mind that these beings are born, by mind that they live once born, and into mind that they pass and merge at the end. Having understood this, he went to his father Varuṇa again and said, ‘Sir, teach me Brahman.’ Varuṇa told him: ‘Seek to know Brahman by austerity. Austerity is Brahman.’ He practised austerity, and when he had practised it —

3.5

विज्ञानं ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् ।विज्ञानाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते ।विज्ञानेन जातानि जीवन्ति ।विज्ञानं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति ।तद्विज्ञाय ।पुनरेव वरुणं पितरमुपससार ।अधीहि भगवो ब्रह्मेति ।तं होवाच ।तपसा ब्रह्म विजिज्ञासस्व ।तपो ब्रह्मेति ।स तपोऽतप्यत ।स तपस्तप्त्वा ॥ १ ॥

vijñānaṃ brahmeti vyajānāt |vijñānāddhyeva khalvimāni bhūtāni jāyante |vijñānena jātāni jīvanti |vijñānaṃ prayantyabhisaṃviśantīti |tadvijñāya |punareva varuṇaṃ pitaramupasasāra |adhīhi bhagavo brahmeti |taṃ hovāca |tapasā brahma vijijñāsasva |tapo brahmeti |sa tapo'tapyata |sa tapastaptvā || 1 ||

Having practised austerity, he understood that understanding is Brahman: for it is from understanding that these beings are born, by understanding that they live once born, and into understanding that they pass and merge at the end. Having understood this, he went to his father Varuṇa again and said, ‘Sir, teach me Brahman.’ Varuṇa told him: ‘Seek to know Brahman by austerity. Austerity is Brahman.’ He practised austerity, and when he had practised it —

3.6

आनन्दो ब्रह्मेति व्यजानात् ।आनन्दाद्ध्येव खल्विमानि भूतानि जायन्ते ।आनन्देन जातानि जीवन्ति ।आनन्दं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति ।सैषा भार्गवी वारुणी विद्या ।परमे व्योमन् प्रतिष्ठिता ।स य एवं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति ।अन्नवानन्नादो भवति ।महान् भवति ।प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन ।महान् कीर्त्या ॥ १ ॥

ānando brahmeti vyajānāt |ānandāddhyeva khalvimāni bhūtāni jāyante |ānandena jātāni jīvanti |ānandaṃ prayantyabhisaṃviśantīti |saiṣā bhārgavī vāruṇī vidyā |parame vyoman pratiṣṭhitā |sa ya evaṃ veda pratitiṣṭhati |annavānannādo bhavati |mahān bhavati |prajayā paśubhirbrahmavarcasena |mahān kīrtyā || 1 ||

At last Bhṛgu understood: bliss is Brahman. For it is from bliss that all beings are born, by bliss that they live once born, and into bliss that they pass and return. This is the wisdom Bhṛgu found and Varuṇa taught, and it rests in the highest space. Whoever knows this stands firm. Such a person has food and enjoys it, and grows great in family, in wealth, in the light of learning, and in good name.

3.7

अन्नं न निन्द्यात् ।तद्व्रतम् ।प्राणो वा अन्नम् ।शरीरमन्नादम् ।प्राणे शरीरं प्रतिष्ठितम् ।शरीरे प्राणः प्रतिष्ठितः ।तदेतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् ।स य एतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति ।अन्नवानन्नादो भवति ।महान् भवति ।प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन ।महान् कीर्त्या ॥ १ ॥

annaṃ na nindyāt |tadvratam |prāṇo vā annam |śarīramannādam |prāṇe śarīraṃ pratiṣṭhitam |śarīre prāṇaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ |tadetadannamanne pratiṣṭhitam |sa ya etadannamanne pratiṣṭhitaṃ veda pratitiṣṭhati |annavānannādo bhavati |mahān bhavati |prajayā paśubhirbrahmavarcasena |mahān kīrtyā || 1 ||

Do not speak ill of food: that is the vow. Breath is food, and the body eats it; the body rests on breath, and breath rests in the body. So food rests in food. Whoever knows that food rests in food stands firm, has food and enjoys it, and grows great in family, wealth, learning and good name.

3.8

अन्नं न परिचक्षीत ।तद्व्रतम् ।आपो वा अन्नम् ।ज्योतिरन्नादम् ।अप्सु ज्योतिः प्रतिष्ठितम् ।ज्योतिष्यापः प्रतिष्ठिताः ।तदेतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् ।स य एतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति ।अन्नवानन्नादो भवति ।महान्भवति ।प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन ।महान्कीर्त्या ॥ १ ॥

annaṃ na paricakṣīta |tadvratam |āpo vā annam |jyotirannādam |apsu jyotiḥ pratiṣṭhitam |jyotiṣyāpaḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ |tadetadannamanne pratiṣṭhitam |sa ya etadannamanne pratiṣṭhitaṃ veda pratitiṣṭhati |annavānannādo bhavati |mahānbhavati |prajayā paśubhirbrahmavarcasena |mahānkīrtyā || 1 ||

Do not refuse food: that is the vow. Water is food, and light, or fire, eats it; light rests in water, and water rests in light. So food rests in food. Whoever knows that food rests in food stands firm, has food and enjoys it, and grows great in family, wealth, learning and good name.

3.9

अन्नं बहु कुर्वीत ।तद्व्रतम् ।पृथिवी वा अन्नम् ।आकाशोऽन्नादः ।पृथिव्यामाकाशः प्रतिष्ठितः ।आकाशे पृथिवी प्रतिष्ठिता ।तदेतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् ।स य एतदन्नमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितं वेद प्रतितिष्ठति ।अन्नवानन्नादो भवति ।महान्भवति ।प्रजया पशुभिर्ब्रह्मवर्चसेन ।महान्कीर्त्या ॥ १ ॥

annaṃ bahu kurvīta |tadvratam |pṛthivī vā annam |ākāśo'nnādaḥ |pṛthivyāmākāśaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ |ākāśe pṛthivī pratiṣṭhitā |tadetadannamanne pratiṣṭhitam |sa ya etadannamanne pratiṣṭhitaṃ veda pratitiṣṭhati |annavānannādo bhavati |mahānbhavati |prajayā paśubhirbrahmavarcasena |mahānkīrtyā || 1 ||

Make much of food: that is the vow. The earth is food, and space eats it; space rests in the earth, and the earth rests in space. So food rests in food. Whoever knows that food rests in food stands firm, has food and enjoys it, and grows great in family, wealth, learning and good name.

3.10

न कञ्चन वसतौ प्रत्याचक्षीत ।तद्व्रतम् ।तस्माद्यया कया च विधया बह्वन्नं प्राप्नुयात् ।अराध्यस्मा अन्नमित्याचक्षते ।एतद्वै मुखतोऽन्नं राद्धम् ।मुखतोऽस्मा अन्नं राध्यते ।एतद्वै मध्यतोऽन्नं राद्धम् ।मध्यतोऽस्मा अन्नं राध्यते ।एतद्वा अन्ततोऽन्नं राद्धम् ।अन्ततोऽस्मा अन्नं राध्यते ॥ १ ॥

na kañcana vasatau pratyācakṣīta |tadvratam |tasmādyayā kayā ca vidhayā bahvannaṃ prāpnuyāt |arādhyasmā annamityācakṣate |etadvai mukhato'nnaṃ rāddham |mukhato'smā annaṃ rādhyate |etadvai madhyato'nnaṃ rāddham |madhyato'smā annaṃ rādhyate |etadvā antato'nnaṃ rāddham |antato'smā annaṃ rādhyate || 1 ||

Never turn away anyone who asks to stay; that is the vow. So get plenty of food by whatever means you can: the wise say to the guest, “Food is ready for you.” Food given in the first and best way returns to the giver in the first and best way; given in the middling way, it returns in the middling way; given in the last and meanest way, it returns in the last way.

य एवं वेद ।क्षेम इति वाचि ।योगक्षेम इति प्राणापानयोः ।कर्मेति हस्तयोः ।गतिरिति पादयोः ।विमुक्तिरिति पायौ ।इति मानुषीः समाज्ञाः ।अथ दैवीः ।तृप्तिरिति वृष्टौ ।बलमिति विद्युति ॥ २ ॥

ya evaṃ veda |kṣema iti vāci |yogakṣema iti prāṇāpānayoḥ |karmeti hastayoḥ |gatiriti pādayoḥ |vimuktiriti pāyau |iti mānuṣīḥ samājñāḥ |atha daivīḥ |tṛptiriti vṛṣṭau |balamiti vidyuti || 2 ||

For one who knows this, it is so. [Meditate on Brahman] as keeping, in speech; as gaining and keeping, in the breaths; as action, in the hands; as movement, in the feet; as release, in the organ of excretion. These are the meditations within the person. Now those among the powers of nature: as satisfaction, in rain; as strength, in lightning —

यश इति पशुषु ।ज्योतिरिति नक्षत्रेषु ।प्रजातिरमृतमानन्द इत्युपस्थे ।सर्वमित्याकाशे ।तत्प्रतिष्ठेत्युपासीत ।प्रतिष्ठावान् भवति ।तन्मह इत्युपासीत ।महान् भवति ।तन्मन इत्युपासीत ।मानवान् भवति ॥ ३ ॥

yaśa iti paśuṣu |jyotiriti nakṣatreṣu |prajātiramṛtamānanda ityupasthe |sarvamityākāśe |tatpratiṣṭhetyupāsīta |pratiṣṭhāvān bhavati |tanmaha ityupāsīta |mahān bhavati |tanmana ityupāsīta |mānavān bhavati || 3 ||

— as fame, in cattle; as light, in the stars; as procreation, immortality and joy, in the organ of generation; as the whole, in space. Meditate on it as the foundation, and you are well founded; as greatness, and you become great; as mind, and you become able to think.

तन्नम इत्युपासीत ।नम्यन्तेऽस्मै कामाः ।तद्ब्रह्मेत्युपासीत ।ब्रह्मवान् भवति ।तद्ब्रह्मणः परिमर इत्युपासीत ।पर्येणं म्रियन्ते द्विषन्तः सपत्नाः ।परि येऽप्रिया भ्रातृव्याः ।स यश्चायं पुरुषे ।यश्चासावादित्ये ।स एकः ॥ ४ ॥

tannama ityupāsīta |namyante'smai kāmāḥ |tadbrahmetyupāsīta |brahmavān bhavati |tadbrahmaṇaḥ parimara ityupāsīta |paryeṇaṃ mriyante dviṣantaḥ sapatnāḥ |pari ye'priyā bhrātṛvyāḥ |sa yaścāyaṃ puruṣe |yaścāsāvāditye |sa ekaḥ || 4 ||

Meditate on it as homage, and what you desire bows to you; as Brahman, the vast, and you become vast. Meditate on it as that in which all dies, and around you your hating rivals die, and the kinsmen who wish you ill. The one in the person and the one in the sun are one.

स य एवंवित् ।अस्माल्लोकात्प्रेत्य ।एतमन्नमयमात्मानमुपसङ्क्रम्य ।एतं प्राणमयमात्मानमुपसङ्क्रम्य ।एतं मनोमयमात्मानमुपसङ्क्रम्य ।एतं विज्ञानमयमात्मानमुपसङ्क्रम्य ।एतमानन्दमयमात्मानमुपसङ्क्रम्य ।इमांल्लोकान्कामान्नी कामरूप्यनुसञ्चरन् ।एतत्साम गायन्नास्ते ।हा३वु हा३वु हा३वु ॥ ५ ॥

sa ya evaṃvit |asmāllokātpretya |etamannamayamātmānamupasaṅkramya |etaṃ prāṇamayamātmānamupasaṅkramya |etaṃ manomayamātmānamupasaṅkramya |etaṃ vijñānamayamātmānamupasaṅkramya |etamānandamayamātmānamupasaṅkramya |imāṃllokānkāmānnī kāmarūpyanusañcaran |etatsāma gāyannāste |hā3vu hā3vu hā3vu || 5 ||

One who knows this, leaving this world, passes into the self made of food, into the self made of breath, of mind, of understanding, and into the self made of bliss. Then, moving through these worlds, with food at will and form at will, he sits and sings this chant: “Ah! Ah! Ah!”

अहमन्नमहमन्नमहमन्नम् ।अहमन्नादो३ऽहमन्नादो३ऽहमन्नादः ।अहं श्लोककृदहं श्लोककृदहं श्लोककृत् ।अहमस्मि प्रथमजा ऋता३स्य ।पूर्वं देवेभ्योऽमृतस्य ना३भायि ।यो मा ददाति स इदेव मा३वाः ।अहमन्नमन्नमदन्तमा३द्मि ।अहं विश्वं भुवनमभ्यभवा३म् ।सुवर्न ज्योतीः ।य एवं वेद ।इत्युपनिषत् ॥ ६ ॥

ahamannamahamannamahamannam |ahamannādo3'hamannādo3'hamannādaḥ |ahaṃ ślokakṛdahaṃ ślokakṛdahaṃ ślokakṛt |ahamasmi prathamajā ṛtā3sya |pūrvaṃ devebhyo'mṛtasya nā3bhāyi |yo mā dadāti sa ideva mā3vāḥ |ahamannamannamadantamā3dmi |ahaṃ viśvaṃ bhuvanamabhyabhavā3m |suvarna jyotīḥ |ya evaṃ veda |ityupaniṣat || 6 ||

“I am food! I am food! I am food! I am the eater of food! I am the eater of food! I am the eater of food! I am the one who joins them! I am the one who joins them! I am the one who joins them! I am the first-born of the true order, older than the gods, the centre of immortality. Whoever gives me away preserves me; whoever eats food without giving, I, food, eat him. I have overcome the whole world; my light is like the sun.” So it is for one who knows this. This is the Upaniṣad.
