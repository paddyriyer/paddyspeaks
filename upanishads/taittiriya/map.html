Prayer before learning · 1.1, 1.12

‘May Mitra be kind to us … I shall speak the right, I shall speak the true.’ The prayer that opens the Śīkṣāvallī and, in the past tense, closes it.

  • then → The lesson on sound — The prayer of 1.1 is followed by the lesson on sound in 1.2.

The lesson on sound · 1.2

Six things a reciter must get right: sound, accent, length, effort, evenness and joining.

  • Prayer before learning → then — The prayer of 1.1 is followed by the lesson on sound in 1.2.
  • joining of sounds → The great joinings — 1.3 takes the joining (saṃhitā) of the lesson on sound and makes it a meditation.
  • study and teaching → Study, teaching, conduct — 1.9 joins svādhyāya and pravacana, study and teaching, to every other duty.

The great joinings · 1.3

The joining of two sounds becomes a way of looking at the worlds, the lights, learning, offspring and the self.

  • The lesson on sound → joining of sounds — 1.3 takes the joining (saṃhitā) of the lesson on sound and makes it a meditation.
  • meditations → Mahaḥ, the fourth call — The meditations continue: the teacher’s prayer (1.4), then the four calls (1.5–1.6).

Mahaḥ, the fourth call · 1.5, 1.6

To bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ and suvaḥ the Upaniṣad adds a fourth, mahaḥ, and calls it Brahman; then the person in the space within the heart.

  • The great joinings → meditations — The meditations continue: the teacher’s prayer (1.4), then the four calls (1.5–1.6).
  • then Om → Om, the syllable — After the fivefold world (1.7), 1.8 opens ‘om iti brahma’.

Study: Bhūr bhuvaḥ suvaḥ, the fivefold and Om

Om, the syllable · 1.8

‘Om is Brahman; Om is all this.’ The syllable of assent, of the chant and of the teacher’s beginning.

  • Mahaḥ, the fourth call → then Om — After the fivefold world (1.7), 1.8 opens ‘om iti brahma’.
  • Om is Brahman → Truth, knowledge, infinite — 1.8 names Om as Brahman; 2.1 defines Brahman.

Study: Bhūr bhuvaḥ suvaḥ, the fivefold and Om

Study, teaching, conduct · 1.9, 1.11

Every duty is to be joined with study and teaching; the teacher’s last words to the departing student: speak the truth, practise dharma.

  • The lesson on sound → study and teaching — 1.9 joins svādhyāya and pravacana, study and teaching, to every other duty.
  • to knowledge → Truth, knowledge, infinite — Śaṅkara (on 2.1): the first vallī gave meditations that do not conflict with rites; the second teaches the knowledge of Brahman. Kūranārāyaṇa (on 3.1) reads all three vallīs as one teaching of the Lord.

Study: The teacher’s last instruction: satyaṃ vada, dharmaṃ cara

Truth, knowledge, infinite · 2.1

‘The knower of Brahman reaches the highest’, and Brahman is truth, knowledge, the infinite, hidden in the cave in the highest space.

  • Study, teaching, conduct → to knowledge — Śaṅkara (on 2.1): the first vallī gave meditations that do not conflict with rites; the second teaches the knowledge of Brahman. Kūranārāyaṇa (on 3.1) reads all three vallīs as one teaching of the Lord.
  • Om, the syllable → Om is Brahman — 1.8 names Om as Brahman; 2.1 defines Brahman.
  • from that self → Creation from the self — 2.1: from that very self space came into being, and so on to the person.
  • The self made of bliss → is it Brahman? — Śaṅkara (on 2.5): the self made of bliss is a product, with Brahman as its tail. Kūranārāyaṇa, Raṅgarāmānuja, Govindarāja and Rāmānanda: it is the supreme self. Madhva: Viṣṇu in each of the five.

Study: Satyaṃ jñānam anantaṃ brahma: defining what cannot be defined

Creation from the self · 2.1

From the self, space; from space, wind; then fire, water, earth, plants, food, and the person made of the essence of food.

  • Truth, knowledge, infinite → from that self — 2.1: from that very self space came into being, and so on to the person.
  • within the person → The five selves — 2.1–2.5: the person made of food, and the selves within it.

Study: The five selves: from food to bliss

The five selves · 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.5

Within the self made of food, a self made of breath; within that, of mind; then of understanding; then of bliss — each shaped like a bird.

  • Creation from the self → within the person — 2.1–2.5: the person made of food, and the selves within it.
  • innermost → The self made of bliss — 2.5: the fifth and innermost self.
  • Bhṛgu’s tapas → the same five — The five answers are the five selves in order. Kūranārāyaṇa (on 3.1): the five meditations and the order of attainment in 3.10 are one order.

Study: The five selves: from food to bliss

The self made of bliss · 2.5

The innermost of the five, with Brahman as its tail and foundation; the vallī’s great dispute is whether it is a product or the supreme self.

  • The five selves → innermost — 2.5: the fifth and innermost self.
  • is it Brahman? → Truth, knowledge, infinite — Śaṅkara (on 2.5): the self made of bliss is a product, with Brahman as its tail. Kūranārāyaṇa, Raṅgarāmānuja, Govindarāja and Rāmānanda: it is the supreme self. Madhva: Viṣṇu in each of the five.
  • he is flavour → Raso vai saḥ: flavour — 2.7: ‘he is flavour’ — the bliss the vallī has reached, named again.
  • ‘I am food’: the song → having reached — 3.10.5: passing on to the self made of bliss, he sits singing this sāman.

Study: The five selves: from food to bliss

Raso vai saḥ: flavour · 2.6, 2.7

The self desired to be many, created and entered what it made; ‘he is flavour; obtaining flavour, one is glad’.

  • The self made of bliss → he is flavour — 2.7: ‘he is flavour’ — the bliss the vallī has reached, named again.
  • bliss measured → The measure of bliss — 2.8 measures bliss, from a human being to Brahman.

Study: Bliss, its measure and its limit

The measure of bliss · 2.8, 2.9

A ladder of bliss in steps of a hundred, from a happy human life to Brahman; and the verse ‘from which words turn back, with the mind’.

  • Raso vai saḥ: flavour → bliss measured — 2.8 measures bliss, from a human being to Brahman.

Study: Bliss, its measure and its limit

Bhṛgu’s tapas · 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6

Sent away with a definition instead of an answer, Bhṛgu finds Brahman by tapas, five times over: food, breath, mind, understanding, bliss.

  • the same five → The five selves — The five answers are the five selves in order. Kūranārāyaṇa (on 3.1): the five meditations and the order of attainment in 3.10 are one order.
  • food honoured → Vows about food — Śaṅkara (on 3.7): food was the first door through which Brahman was known, so it is honoured like a teacher.

Study: Bhṛgu’s inquiry: tapas and the five definitions

Vows about food · 3.7, 3.8, 3.9, 3.10

Do not despise food, do not reject it, make much of it, turn no one away from your door; food and its eater rest in each other.

  • Bhṛgu’s tapas → food honoured — Śaṅkara (on 3.7): food was the first door through which Brahman was known, so it is honoured like a teacher.
  • I am food → ‘I am food’: the song — 3.10 ends the vows and meditations with the knower’s song.

Study: Food: the vows and the meditations

‘I am food’: the song · 3.10

Having passed through the five selves, the knower sits singing: ‘I am food, I am the eater of food, I am the maker of the verse.’

  • Vows about food → I am food — 3.10 ends the vows and meditations with the knower’s song.
  • having reached → The self made of bliss — 3.10.5: passing on to the self made of bliss, he sits singing this sāman.
