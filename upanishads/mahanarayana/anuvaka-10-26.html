Anuvāka 10.26

1 Original Sanskrit

यस्य वैकङ्कत्यग्निहोत्रहवणी भवति प्रत्येवास्याहुतयस्तिष्ठन्त्यथो प्रतिष्ठित्यै ॥ ४४ ॥

Accented text · Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10, the recitation text (MD), as the witness keys it

यस्य॒ वैक॑ङ्कत्यग्निहोत्र॒हव॑णी भवति॒ प्रत्ये॒वास्याहु॑तयस्तिष्ठ॒न्त्यथो॒ प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै

Svara is shown only from this witness and only as it is keyed; it is not proof-read or reconstructed. Its letters agree with the edited text (spelling of nasals aside).

2 IAST transliteration

yasya vaikaṅkatyagnihotrahavaṇī bhavati pratyevāsyāhutayastiṣṭhantyatho pratiṣṭhityai || 44 ||
