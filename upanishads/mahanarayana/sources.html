Three commentaries are used. The anonymous bhāṣya printed with the 80-anuvāka text in the Ānandāśrama pariśiṣṭa comments on this text in this order; its author is unknown and it largely follows Sāyaṇa, so it is cited as anonymous. Sāyaṇa (Vedārthaprakāśa) and Bhaṭṭa Bhāskara (Jñānayajña) comment on 64-anuvāka recensions — Sāyaṇa on the one vishvAsa calls Āndhra, Bhāskara on the Drāviḍa — whose order differs (the five ‘brahma’ mantras sadyojātam … are Sāyaṇa’s 43–47) and which lack the block of homa and japa mantras of anuvākas 51–64 here; they are reached through a computed concordance. A commentary of Raṅgarāmānuja on this Upaniṣad exists but is not in the edition’s sources, and is not cited. The sources are OCR-derived texts of printed books; print matter is removed on import.
