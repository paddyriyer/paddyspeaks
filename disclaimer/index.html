What This Site Is — and Isn’t

Different parts of PaddySpeaks are different kinds of writing. This page says which is which, so you can decide how much weight to put on each.

Last updated 24 September 2026

⚖ Needs legal reviewPlain-language disclaimer, not reviewed by counsel.

Educational material

The technical essays, Interview Studio, design deep-dives and demos explain how things work, as understood at the time of writing. They are for learning. Before relying on a technique in production, verify it against the current documentation of the tools you use. Technology changes faster than essays are revised.

Personal commentary

Many essays are opinion: about work, careers, technology, society, family and growing up. They are one person's view, argued as carefully as possible, and they are labelled by category rather than dressed up as neutral reporting.

Devotional and spiritual interpretation

The sacred-text apps present original texts with transliteration, word meanings and commentary. The texts come from the sources named in each page's Sources & Verification panel. The meanings and the "real-life" or esoteric readings are interpretations: offered respectfully, drawing on traditional commentary where noted, but not authoritative religious guidance. Traditions differ, and your own teacher, lineage and practice come first. Where a translation is known to be under review, the page says so.

Career and job information

Interview guidance, resume advice and study plans are one practitioner's experience and a synthesis of common practice. They are not a guarantee of any hiring outcome. JobSignal reads public job postings from employers' own careers systems and reports what it observed, with dates. A listing can close or change at any time. PaddySpeaks has no relationship with the employers listed and does not endorse them.

Financial commentary

Essays that model house prices, household budgets or relocation costs show their assumptions and sources so you can check them. They are not financial advice. Your own situation needs your own numbers, and for big decisions a qualified adviser.

Health-related writing

Some essays touch on breathing, eating, medical technology and healthcare systems. They are general commentary and not medical advice. Talk to a qualified clinician about your own health.

AI-generated material

  • Skill Check questions are added daily by an automated job that uses Anthropic's Claude to write new SQL, Python, design and dashboarding questions. They are published without individual human review, so treat an answer you disagree with as a question worth checking, and tell us if it is wrong.
  • Community-submitted questions are formatted by the same model before publication.
  • The AI mock interview runs on your own Anthropic key. Its feedback is generated live and may be wrong.
  • The enterprise demos run on synthetic or fictional data. Their numbers describe no real company.

User-submitted content

Testimonials are the words of the people who wrote them, published only after approval. Interview questions shared by readers reflect their own experience of an interview.

Third-party links

Links to other sites are provided for reference. PaddySpeaks does not control those sites and is not responsible for their content or practices.
