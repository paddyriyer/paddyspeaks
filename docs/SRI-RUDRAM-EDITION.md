# Śrī Rudram — the verified study edition

Read this before touching `rudramchamakam/` or `scripts/rudram/`. The brief every
analysis file is written to is `docs/SRI-RUDRAM-BRIEF.md`.

The edition replaced the previous Rudram page on 2026-10-13. That page's text had
been assembled without a recorded source; the audit (on `/rudramchamakam/sources.html`,
generated by `scripts/rudram/audit.py`) found, among other things, whole Sanskrit
lines in Namakam 11 that are in no witness, the Tryambaka mantra (TS 1.8) printed
as part of Namakam 11, recitation prayers set inside Namakam 1, and the Chamakam's
च मे refrain dropped from every item. The order of work since then is fixed:
**verify the Sanskrit first, explain it second, interpret it third, design it last.**

## What exists

| Path | What it is |

Hand-maintained after the import; every change is logged. |

## Commands

```
python3 scripts/rudram/check.py --complete          # the guardrail (Validate Content)
python3 scripts/rudram/check.py --file <analysis>   # one analysis file (add --partial mid-way)
python3 scripts/rudram/collate.py                   # print the W1 × W2 letter differences
python3 scripts/rudram/derive.py                    # then: --check in CI
python3 scripts/rudram/audit.py                     # then: --check in CI
node scripts/rudram/build.mjs                       # then: --check in CI
python3 scripts/platform_build/build.py all         # search index (verses shard), counts
```

After any change to text, corrections or analysis: `check.py`, `derive.py`,
`audit.py`, `build.mjs`, in that order.

## The sources

GitHub was the only source host reachable from the build environment
(sanskritdocuments.org, GRETIL, TITUS were refused). The witnesses all come from
public corpora there:

- **W1** — `github.com/vishvAsa/vedAH_yajuH`, `saMhitA/mUlam/panchAti-kramaH/4/{5,7}.md`:
  the Saṃhitā in its pañcāśat divisions, South Indian Taittirīya orthography (doubled
  sibilants for visarga, ᳵ ᳶ, assimilated nasals, ँ), svara marked. The corpus records it
  as keyed from TITUS and Parankuśācārya's edition. **Base text.**
  W1 and W2 are not wholly independent: both descend in part from TITUS, and at
  Namakam 1.13 both mis-key अयक्ष्मया as अयक्षमया (corrected on the evidence of
  Sāyaṇa, Abhinava Śaṅkara and DCS). Where the commentaries read otherwise, their
  agreement is not proof.
- **W2** — the same corpus, `mUlam/vAkya-kramaH/4/{5,7}.md`: sentence by sentence in
  Weber's orthography, independently keyed. Collated letter by letter (`collate.py`)
  and svara by svara.
- **S** — Sāyaṇa's bhāṣya, `saMhitA/sarva-prastutiH/4/…`: the authority for the
  traditional notes, the arbiter between W1 and W2, and the source of the yajus counts.
- **AŚ** — Abhinava Śaṅkara's *Śrīrudrabhāṣyam* (Vāṇī Vilāsa, Śrīraṅgam, 1962), in the
  same corpus: prints the Namakam text; third witness for the Namakam.
- **DCS** — `github.com/OliverHellwig/sanskrit`: morphological annotation of TS 4.5.1–2.
- Monier-Williams (`github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig`) for meanings; Keith's 1914
  translation (in the vishvAsa corpus) was consulted, never copied.

The two PDFs kept in `rudramchamakam/` are references Paddy saved (a 2018 scan and a
print of kamakoti.org's Chamakam page); neither is a source of this text.

## Rules

- **Never edit a generated page** (`index.html`, `namakam-*.html`, `chamakam-*.html`,
  `sources.html`, `derived.json`, `audit.json`). CI fails on stale output.
- **The text is proven, not trusted.** `check.py` fails unless text.json equals W1 with
  exactly the logged corrections, the collation with W2 yields exactly the classified
  differences, Namakam 1/10/11 have 15/12/11 units and Namakam 2–9 the yajus counts
  Sāyaṇa states (13, 17, 17, 15, 15, 16, 17, 19), and Chamakam 11's numbers are the odd
  series to 33 and the multiples of four to 48.
- **Every change to the Sanskrit is logged** in `corrections.json` with its evidence;
  variants are recorded, not silently chosen.
- **The padaccheda is proven.** For every anuvāka the separated words rejoin by external
  sandhi (`sandhi.py`, which knows the Taittirīya options) into the Saṃhitā; junctions
  outside the rules are declared in `sandhi_exceptions` and shown on the page; an
  unneeded exception fails. The word table lists exactly the padaccheda.
- **Every particle is a word.** च, मे, ते, नः, वः, उत, उ, नमः are never dropped, however
  often they repeat; the Chamakam's च मे is in the Saṃhitā, the padaccheda and the word
  table of every item.
- **IAST is never typed**: `derive.py` generates it with `translit.py`, which the checker
  proves by round trip; IAST carries no accents.
- **Svara is transcribed, not reconstructed.** The marks are W1's. Units where W2 marks a
  syllable differently are flagged "svara under review" and listed on the Sources page.
  Never add or change a mark on one witness's authority.
- **No invented authority.** The traditional note on every unit must name Sāyaṇa, and
  every Sanskrit word or phrase it quotes must occur in his bhāṣya on that anuvāka (or
  be a word of the mantra) — the checker enforces it. Reflections are a separate,
  labelled field. Translations and word meanings may not use the vocabulary of modern
  esoterica (the checker has the list).
- **Supplements are not the text.** ॐ नमो भगवते रुद्राय, the Tryambaka mantra (TS 1.8),
  इडा देवहूः (TS 3.3.2) and the śānti stand on the home page under "Around the
  recitation", each with its source; नमस्ते अस्तु भगवन् … is in none of the witnesses
  and is not reproduced until it can be checked against a printed source.
- **Unit anchors never change**: `#n3-7`, `#c1-2` (part letter, anuvāka, unit).
