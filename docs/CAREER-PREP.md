# Career Prep and the Studio jobs board

Two connected parts of Interview Studio (2026-11-20):

- **`/interview.app/careers/` — Career Prep.** Role-specific interview preparation for
  Product, AI Product, Technical & Program, Data & Analytics and Growth & Strategy roles:
  a curriculum per role, original practice questions, a timed five-round mock interview,
  self-assessed results, and a route to apply. Engineering roles are routed to the Studio's
  existing executable SQL and Python practice instead.
- **`/interview.app/jobs/` — the jobs board.** Open roles across all six career families,
  each linked to Career Prep (with role, company and seniority) and to the employer's own
  posting.

Journey: **Find a role → Explore its interview → Practise → Mock interview → Review → Apply.**

## One taxonomy, as configuration

`interview/careers/roles.json` is the single source of truth for career families,
seniority levels and roles. Everything else reads it: the jobs pipeline, the jobs page,
Career Prep, My Prep.

| Concept | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| CareerFamily | `roles.json` → `families` (6) |
| JobRole | `roles.json` → `roles` (stable `id`, `family`, default `level`, `track`, `focus`, `title_patterns`) |
| JobPosting | `interview.app/jobs/data/jobs.json` (GENERATED by `interview/scripts/build_jobs.py`) |
| InterviewCategory | `interview/careers/categories.json` (with its framework guide) |
| InterviewTrack | `interview/careers/tracks/<id>.json` — one curriculum, shared by related roles |
| InterviewRound | `tracks/<id>.json` → `rounds` (exactly five, each with a question pool) |
| InterviewQuestion | `tracks/<id>.json` → `questions` |
| EvaluationRubric | `interview/careers/rubric.json` (dimensions + four anchors each) |
| PracticeAttempt | the visitor's browser only: `localStorage["ps.careers.v1"]` |

### Adding a role (no UI change)

1. Add the role to `roles.json`: a new stable `id` (never rename one — URLs and stored
   attempts use it), its `family`, default `level`, `prep` (`careers` or `studio`),
   `track` (for `careers`) and optional `focus` categories, a one-line `summary`, and
   `title_patterns`. **Order matters**: titles are matched top to bottom, first match
   wins, so a specific role goes above a general one (AI TPM above Senior TPM above TPM;
   every product specialism above the plain `pm`). Engineering (`studio`) roles carry
   `exclude_patterns` so "Product Manager, Data Engineering" is never an engineer.
2. If the role needs a new interview, add `tracks/<new>.json` (five rounds, every category
   with at least one question, every question in some round).
3. Run `python3 interview/careers/build.py`, `python3 interview/scripts/build_jobs.py`,
   `python3 -m interview.careers.tests.test_careers` (it checks every role's own title
   classifies to itself) and `python3 scripts/platform_build/build.py all`.

A new family is the same: add it to `families`; the selectors on both pages are built from
the data.

## Content rules

- **Three kinds of content, always labelled.** (1) Publicly reported questions come only
  from Interview Intelligence, with sources, and appear only when a report names the role;
  otherwise the page says there are none. (2) Original practice scenarios — every question
  in `tracks/` — are labelled "Original practice scenario — not from any company's
  interviews". (3) Category guides are labelled as study aids, not any employer's rubric.
- **Never attribute a question to an employer, never call one real.** `build.py` fails on
  attribution phrases, and on a question that names a well-known company unless its
  `premise` says it is a hypothetical and *not a reported interview question* (the
  Instagram engagement case is the one such premise, by the owner's prompt).
- **PM and TPM are different jobs.** `build.py` fails if the PM, Technical PM, AI PM and TPM
  tracks share more than a fifth of their categories, or if Technical Product Manager and
  Technical Program Manager use the same track. Each track that has a near neighbour says
  how it differs (`distinct_from`), and the page shows it.
- **Never type a number into a scenario.** Figures inside questions are `vars` substituted
  into `{{…}}` expressions, and tables with `"compute": "rice"` or `"critical_path"` are
  calculated by the build. Counts on the page are derived; the header uses `data-ps-stat`.
- **No grading of free text, no hiring predictions.** Scores are the visitor's own 1–4
  self-assessment against written anchors. `build.py` rejects prediction language.
- **No SQL substituted for a PM interview.** Product and program roles never show SQL; the
  bank link appears on job cards only for `studio` roles.

## The jobs board

`interview/scripts/build_jobs.py` merges sources, classifies every posting with
`interview/careers/classify.py`, and writes `interview.app/jobs/data/jobs.json`
(one job per line). All judgement is Python; `interview.app/js/jobs-filters.js` only
filters, counts and serialises the URL; `interview.app/js/jobs.js` renders.

- **Sources.** JobSignal's verified board (`jobs/data/index.json`, refreshed every four
  hours by `jobsignal-ingest.yml`, staffing firms and `caution` confidence excluded), plus
  the keyless ATS feed and optional Adzuna feed (`refresh-jobs.yml`, Mondays). Duplicates
  across sources are dropped by apply URL and by company + title + location; JobSignal
  wins because it carries salary, workplace and a verified date.
- **Summaries** are verbatim excerpts from the posting (`summarise()`), chosen for talking
  about the role rather than the company, never paraphrased, and never keeping a sentence
  about whom the role reports to.
- **Lifecycle.** `first_seen` is carried forward and never re-stamped. A listing missing
  from a source that delivered this run becomes `expired` for 21 days (shown only when the
  visitor asks, with no Apply button), then is dropped. A listing whose source did not
  deliver this run is carried forward unchanged: a failed fetch never expires real jobs.
- **Sample mode** (`--sample`) is the labelled illustrative preview for when no source is
  connected. Every card says "Sample · not a real opening", and Apply becomes a search.
- **Filters**: career family (also secondary families, e.g. a Group PM for AI appears under
  Product and AI Product), job title, seniority, company, location, remote/hybrid/on-site,
  employment type, salary floor (US-dollar postings only; other currencies are not
  converted), keywords, and closed listings. State lives in the URL; `?company=Name` still
  works.

## Career Prep page

`interview.app/careers/index.html` + `js/app.js` (render, route, store) + `js/engine.js`
(pure: curriculum order, seeded round plans, self-assessment arithmetic). URL:
`?role=&level=&company=&job=&start=` and `#topics|practice|mock|results|apply`.
The jobs page links **Practice interview** to `start=mock` and **View role preparation**
to the topics. A mock in progress survives a reload; My Prep lists completed mocks.

## Guardrails (all no-network, all in Validate Content)

```
python3 interview/careers/build.py --check
python3 -m interview.careers.tests.test_careers
node interview.app/careers/tests/engine.mjs
node interview.app/jobs/tests/filters.mjs
```

The accessibility ratchet (`scripts/a11y/axe-check.mjs`) covers `/interview.app/jobs/`,
`/interview.app/careers/` and a role page.

## Known limitations

- The Studio board is rebuilt weekly (`refresh-jobs.yml`), while JobSignal refreshes every
  four hours, so a listing JobSignal has already closed can show as open here for up to a
  week. Every card shows when it was last checked. A daily rebuild is the obvious next step
  and belongs in its own small PR (it changes a production workflow).
- No live third-party jobs API is connected beyond JobSignal and the keyless ATS boards;
  Adzuna stays dormant until its secrets are set.
- Interview Intelligence has no reports naming product or program roles yet, so the
  "Publicly reported questions" section shows its honest empty state for those roles.
- Company Interview Loops are data-engineering loops; Career Prep links to them only for
  engineering roles.
