Invoking the Gāyatrī गायत्र्यावाहनम्

The Gāyatrī is invited with āyātu varadā and ojo ’si.

1 Viniyoga of āyātu Viniyoga

आयात्वित्यनुवाकस्य वामदेव ऋषिःअनुष्टुप् छन्दःगायत्री देवता

āyātvityanuvākasya vāmadeva ṛṣiḥanuṣṭup chandaḥgāyatrī devatā

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

The other witness

  • Spelling only M: M writes the words unjoined (आयात्विति अनुवाकस्य).
  • Variant reading M: M names the use (आवाहने विनियोगः); S stops at the deity.

2 Āyātu varadā devī: the invitation of the gāyatrī Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.34.52 (TA 10)

आयातु वरदा देवी अक्षरं ब्रह्म सम्मितम्गायत्रीं छन्दसां मातेदं ब्रह्म जुषस्व मेयदह्नात्कुरुते पापं तदह्नात्प्रतिमुच्यतेयद्रात्रियात्कुरुते पापं तद्रात्रियात्प्रतिमुच्यतेसर्ववर्णे महादेवि सन्ध्याविद्ये सरस्वति

āyātu varadā devī akṣaraṃ brahma sammitamgāyatrīṃ chandasāṃ mātedaṃ brahma juṣasva meyadahnātkurute pāpaṃ tadahnātpratimucyateyadrātriyātkurute pāpaṃ tadrātriyātpratimucyatesarvavarṇe mahādevi sandhyāvidye sarasvati

Come, boon-giving goddess, imperishable, the measure of Brahman. Gāyatrī, mother of the metres, accept this sacred word of mine. Whatever wrong is done by day is undone that day; whatever is done by night is undone that night. Goddess of every colour, great goddess, wisdom of the twilights, Sarasvatī!

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.34.52 in the Upaniṣad edition.

3 Ojo ’si and the gāyatrī with its head: the invitation completed and the breath-control mantra Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.35.53 (TA 10)

ओजोऽसि सहोऽसि बलमसि भ्राजोऽसि देवानां धामनामाऽसि विश्वमसि विश्वायुस्सर्वमसि सर्वायुरभिभूरों गायत्रीमावाहयामि सावित्रीमावाहयामि सरस्वतीमावाहयामि

ojo'si saho'si balamasi bhrājo'si devānāṃ dhāmanāmā'si viśvamasi viśvāyussarvamasi sarvāyurabhibhūroṃ gāyatrīmāvāhayāmi sāvitrīmāvāhayāmi sarasvatīmāvāhayāmi

The rite recites part of this unit; the translation is of the whole unit.

You are vigour and conquering power, strength and radiance; the splendour of the gods is your very name; you are the universe and its life, all things and the life of all, the one who prevails. Om — I call here the gāyatrī, the sāvitrī, Sarasvatī, the metres and the seers, and Śrī. The gāyatrī: its metre gāyatrī, its seer Viśvāmitra, its deity Savitṛ; Agni its mouth, Brahmā its head, Viṣṇu its heart, Rudra its crest, the earth its womb; bearing the five breaths, white, of the lineage of Sāṅkhyāyana; twenty-four syllables in three feet; used at the upanayana. Om earth, Om mid-space, Om heaven, Om the great, Om janaḥ, Om tapaḥ, Om the true. Om — let us contemplate the excellent radiance of the god Savitṛ, who impels our minds. Om — the waters, light, essence, the immortal: Brahman; earth, mid-space, heaven — Om.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.35.53 in the Upaniṣad edition.
