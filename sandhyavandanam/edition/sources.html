Sandhyāvandanam

Sources, Variants and Corrections

The witnesses

  • W1 — Base witness: the Taittirīya Saṃhitā in pañcāśat divisions (vishvAsa/vedAH_yajuH, saMhitA/mUlam/panchAti-kramaH; keyed from TITUS and Parankuśācārya) and the Āraṇyaka of muralīdhara (AraNyakam/mUlam/muralIdharaH.md), svara marked (commit b5d616ee16)
  • W2 — Second witness: the vākya-krama Saṃhitā (Weber’s orthography) and the gaṇeśvara Āraṇyaka (same corpus) (commit b5d616ee16)
  • RV — The Ṛgveda parallels (GRETIL, from Aufrecht 1877; Śākala recension, no svara), github.com/sanskrit/raw_etexts (commit 61cbc00bf5)
  • M — M — the Sandhyāvandana prayoga of the Mylapore Veda Adhyayana Sabhā (publisher A. Subbaraman, Mylapore; English by Sundararajan Subramanian), as transcribed in vishvAsa/vedAH_yajuH, taittirIyam/sUtram/ApastambaH/gRhyam/paddhatiH/mailApura-vedAdhyayana-sabhA/sandhyAvandanam.md (commit b5d616ee16)
  • S — S — Sandhyavandanam.tex of github.com/stotrasamhita/vedamantra-book (mantras/), the Smārta Āpastamba Sandhyāvandanam for the three times (commit 097c82834d)
  • Commentaries — Sāyaṇa and Bhaṭṭa Bhāskara on the Taittirīya Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa and Āraṇyaka, as the vishvAsa corpus labels them (witnesses/comm.json).
  • The Mahānārāyaṇa — its units are proven in its own edition.

The vishvAsa corpus (github.com/vishvAsa) records its own sources for the Saṃhitā (TITUS and Parankuśācārya’s edition) and the Āraṇyaka. GRETIL’s Ṛgveda is from Aufrecht’s edition (1877); its file keys RV 5.85.8 wrongly, as noted with that verse. The stotrasamhita vedamantra-book is a LaTeX edition for personal study; it is used here as a witness, and the edition’s text is its own.

Corrections to a base witness

  • yac-cid-dhi.1 त → च: W1 breaks the word at a pañcāśat marker (यत् [45] चिद्धि); the Saṃhitā joins t + c as cc (यच्चिद्धि), as W2 (यत् ॥ चिद् धि, split at its line) and RV 1.25.1 (यच् चिद् धि) show.
  • prayascitta-sayam → ्त: M drops a t: प्रायश्चित्त (prāyaścitta, atonement), as M itself writes it for the morning and midday forms.
  • vin-agnis-ca → ्च: M prints अग्निशेत्य-: the pratīka is अग्निश्च (agniś ca, the anuvāka’s first words), + iti, as in the parallel सूर्यश्चेत्यनुवाकस्य.
  • vin-dadhikravnah व → क: M prints दधिव्रावा (व for क); the deity is Dadhikrāvan, as the mantra itself names him (दधिक्राव्णः).
  • vin-uttame → ्य: M drops the y: गायत्री + उपस्थाने joins as गायत्र्युपस्थाने.
  • vin-mitrasya → ्य: M writes सन्धो- for सन्ध्या + उपस्थान (सन्ध्योपस्थाने), as S writes it.
  • vin-imam-me → ्य: M writes सन्धो- for सन्ध्या + उपस्थान (सन्ध्योपस्थाने), as S writes it.
  • upasthana-sankalpa-sayam → ्य: M writes सन्धो- for सन्ध्या + उपस्थान (सन्ध्योपस्थानम्), as S writes it.
