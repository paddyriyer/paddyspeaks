Sandhyāvandanam · Step 22 of 29

Salutation to all the deities समष्ट्यभिवादनम्

Saluting Sandhyā, Sāvitrī, Gāyatrī and Sarasvatī, the deities together; kāmo ’kārṣīt; and the formal greeting with one’s lineage.

1 Samaṣṭy-abhivādana Formula

सन्ध्यायै नमःसावित्र्यै नमःगायत्र्यै नमःसरस्वत्यै नमःसर्वाभ्यो देवताभ्यो नमो नमः

sandhyāyai namaḥsāvitryai namaḥgāyatryai namaḥsarasvatyai namaḥsarvābhyo devatābhyo namo namaḥ

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

2 Kāmo ’kārṣīt, manyur akārṣīt Formula

कामोऽकार्षी᳚न्मन्युरकार्षी᳚न्नमो॒ नमः

kāmo'kārṣīnmanyurakārṣīnnamo namaḥ

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

An abbreviation of Mahānārāyaṇa 10.61–62 (kāmo ’kārṣīt … ; manyur akārṣīt …), recited as one line.

See Mahānārāyaṇa 10.61.79 and Mahānārāyaṇa 10.62.80.

3 Abhivādana Formula

अभिवादये (त्रयार्षेय) प्रवरान्वित गोत्रः (आपस्तम्ब) सूत्रः यजुःशाखा अध्यायी () शर्मा नामाहम् अस्मि भोः

abhivādaye (trayārṣeya) pravarānvita gotraḥ (āpastamba) sūtraḥ yajuḥśākhā adhyāyī () śarmā nāmāham asmi bhoḥ

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

The other witness

  • Variant reading M: M fills the formula with a worked example (Āṅgīrasa, Bārhaspatya, Bhāradvāja; Bhāradvāja gotra); S leaves the places blank.
  • Spelling only M: M: त्र्यार्षेय; S: त्रयार्षेय.
  • Variant reading M: M’s example gotra (Bhāradvāja); S leaves it blank.
  • Spelling only M: S: यजुःशाखा अध्यायी; M: यजुःशाखाध्यायी (joined).
  • Spelling only M: M writes the words unjoined (नामाहं अस्मि).
