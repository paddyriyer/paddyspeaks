Saluting the directions दिग्देवतावन्दनम्

The four quarters, above and below, the sky and the earth, and the deities.

1 Dig-devatā-vandana Formula

प्राच्यै दिशे नमःदक्षिणायै दिशे नमःप्रतीच्यै दिशे नमःउदीच्यै दिशे नमःऊर्ध्वाय नमःअधराय नमःअन्तरिक्षाय नमःभूम्यै नमःब्रह्मणे नमःविष्णवे नमःमृत्यवे नमः

prācyai diśe namaḥdakṣiṇāyai diśe namaḥpratīcyai diśe namaḥudīcyai diśe namaḥūrdhvāya namaḥadharāya namaḥantarikṣāya namaḥbhūmyai namaḥbrahmaṇe namaḥviṣṇave namaḥmṛtyave namaḥ

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

The other witness

  • Witness error M: M reads दक्षिनायै; S दक्षिणायै.
  • Witness error M: M’s list repeats the four quarters (and reads देशे for दिशे); S gives each once.
  • Variant reading M: S closes with मृत्यवे नमः; M’s list stops at विष्णवे नमः (it then adds शिवाय नमः, यमाय नमः, outside this text).
