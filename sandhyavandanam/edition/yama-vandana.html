1 The names of Yama Verse of the prayoga

यमाय धर्मराजाय मृत्यवे चान्तकाय च वैवस्वताय कालाय सर्वभूतक्षयाय च औदुम्बराय दध्नाय नीलाय परमेष्ठिने वृकोदराय चित्राय चित्रगुप्ताय वै नमः चित्रगुप्ताय वै नम ओं नम इति

yamāya dharmarājāya mṛtyave cāntakāya ca vaivasvatāya kālāya sarvabhūtakṣayāya ca audumbarāya dadhnāya nīlāya parameṣṭhine vṛkodarāya citrāya citraguptāya vai namaḥ citraguptāya vai nama om nama iti

Homage to Yama — King of Dharma, Death, the Ender, son of the Sun, Time, destroyer of all beings; to Audumbara, Dadhna, the dark one, the highest, the wolf-bellied, Citra and Citragupta. To Citragupta, homage; om, homage.

Faithful English translation

To Yama, the King of Dharma, to Death and to the Ender, to the son of Vivasvat, to Time, and to the destroyer of all beings; to Audumbara, to Dadhna, to the dark one, to the highest, to the wolf-bellied, to Citra, to Citragupta indeed, homage. “To Citragupta indeed homage — om, homage.”

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

Fourteen names of Yama, the god of death and of dharma, said facing south, his direction. Several are transparent — Dharmarāja, Mṛtyu (Death), Antaka (the Ender), Vaivasvata (son of the Sun), Kāla (Time) — while others (Audumbara, Dadhna) are traditional names whose sense is not obvious; the list ends with Citragupta, who keeps the record of deeds. The last words are repeated with om namaḥ iti.

In the rite Step yama-vandana, all three services, facing south.

Notes
Linguistic and Textual Analysis

Two anuṣṭubh (śloka) verses with a refrain. The vowels of च औदुम्बराय stand unjoined at the break between the verses. M reads धर्मर्राजाय (corrections.json).

Verification required S writes the verses and the refrain with no danda between them, so नमः चित्रगुप्ताय (verse end, refrain start) stands unjoined inside one line. The padaccheda is two lines (verses | refrain): a danda is needed after the first चित्रगुप्ताय वै नमः (it changes no letter).

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

  • Witness error M: M reads धर्मर्राजाय (a doubled r); S धर्मराजाय.
