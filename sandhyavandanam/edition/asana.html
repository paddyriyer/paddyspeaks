The seat for the japa आसनम्

Before the japa: salutation, dispelling of beings, and the prayer to the Earth for the seat (in the Mylapore prayoga).

1 Before the japa: salutation, dispersal and the seat Verse of the prayoga

आब्रह्म-लोकात् आशेषात् आलोकालोकपर्वतात्ये वसन्ति द्विजा देवाः तेभ्यो नित्यं नमो नमःअपसर्पन्तु ये भूता ये भूता भुवि संस्थिताःये भूता विघ्नकर्तारः ते गच्छन्तु शिवाज्ञयाउग्र-भूत-पिशाचाद्याः ये च वै भूमि-भारगाःएतेषामविरोधेन ब्रह्मकर्म समारभेपृथ्वि त्वया धृता लोकाः देवि त्वं विष्णुना धृतात्वं च धारय मां देवि पवित्रं कुरु चासनम्

ābrahma-lokāt āśeṣāt ālokālokaparvatātye vasanti dvijā devāḥ tebhyo nityaṃ namo namaḥapasarpantu ye bhūtā ye bhūtā bhuvi saṃsthitāḥye bhūtā vighnakartāraḥ te gacchantu śivājñayāugra-bhūta-piśācādyāḥ ye ca vai bhūmi-bhāragāḥeteṣāmavirodhena brahmakarma samārabhepṛthvi tvayā dhṛtā lokāḥ devi tvaṃ viṣṇunā dhṛtātvaṃ ca dhāraya māṃ devi pavitraṃ kuru cāsanam

Homage always to the twice-born and the gods who live anywhere from Brahmā’s heaven down to Śeṣa and out to the mountain at the world’s rim. Let the spirits that dwell on the earth, the makers of obstacles, go away at Śiva’s command; let the fierce ones and the piśācas not stand in the way as I begin this Vedic rite. Earth, you hold up the worlds and Viṣṇu holds you up: hold me too, goddess, and make my seat pure.

Faithful English translation

To the twice-born and the gods who dwell from Brahmā’s world to Śeṣa, to the Lokāloka mountain — to them always homage, homage. Let the beings move away, the beings settled on the earth; the beings that make obstacles, let them go by Śiva’s command. The fierce spirits, piśācas and the rest, who are a burden on the earth — without their opposition I begin the Vedic rite. Earth, by you the worlds are held; goddess, you are held by Viṣṇu; and you, goddess, hold me, and make the seat pure.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

Four traditional verses of the prayoga, said before sitting for the Gāyatrī japa. The first greets every twice-born being and god in the whole extent of the world, from Brahmā’s heaven to Śeṣa below and the Lokāloka range at the edge; the second and third ask the spirits that cause obstacles to withdraw, so that the Vedic rite can begin unopposed; the fourth turns to the Earth, who holds the worlds and is held by Viṣṇu, and asks her to hold the worshipper and purify the seat.

In the rite Step āsana, before the japa, all three services, in the Mylapore prayoga only (S lacks the step).

Forms

  • आब्रह्मलोकात् — ā with the ablative marks a limit: ‘as far as’. The line has three such limits: ā-brahmalokāt, ā-śeṣāt, ā-lokāloka-parvatāt.
  • पृथ्वि — vocative of pṛthvī, a by-form of pṛthivī.
Notes
Linguistic and Textual Analysis

Four anuṣṭubh (śloka) verses. Only M has them.

Verification required M prints the words unjoined where sandhi joins them inside a pāda: आब्रह्म-लोकात् आशेषात् (lokād āśeṣād), द्विजा देवाः तेभ्यो (devās tebhyo), विघ्नकर्तारः ते (-kartāras te), पिशाचाद्याः ये (-ādyā ye), धृता लोकाः देवि (lokā devi). The padaccheda rejoins only into the joined text: proposed text आब्रह्मलोकादाशेषादालोकालोकपर्वतात् … देवास्तेभ्यो … विघ्नकर्तारस्ते … उग्र-भूत-पिशाचाद्या ये … धृता लोका देवि.

Verification required भूमिभारगाः is as M prints; the sense ‘going as a burden on the earth’ is uncertain, and no second witness has the verse.
