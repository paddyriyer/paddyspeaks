The midday Sandhyā माध्याह्निकम्

Every text of the midday service in order: Sanskrit, IAST and readable English. Each step links to its full analysis.

1. Ācamana आचमनम्

Sipping water three times with the three names, then touching the face and body with the twelve names.

Ācamana: the three names Formula

अच्युताय नमःअनन्ताय नमःगोविन्दाय नमः

acyutāya namaḥanantāya namaḥgovindāya namaḥ

Salutation to Acyuta, to Ananta, to Govinda.

Aṅga-vandana: the twelve names Formula

केशव नारायण माधव गोविन्द विष्णो मधुसूदन त्रिविक्रम वामन श्रीधर हृषीकेश पद्मनाभ दामोदर

keśava nārāyaṇa mādhava govinda viṣṇo madhusūdana trivikrama vāmana śrīdhara hṛṣīkeśa padmanābha dāmodara

Keśava, Nārāyaṇa, Mādhava, Govinda, Viṣṇu, Madhusūdana, Trivikrama, Vāmana, Śrīdhara, Hṛṣīkeśa, Padmanābha, Dāmodara.

2. Meditation on the remover of obstacles विघ्नेश्वरध्यानम्

Gaṇeśa is called to mind before the rite begins.

The one clothed in white Verse of the prayoga

शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये

śuklāmbaradharaṃ viṣṇuṃ śaśivarṇaṃ caturbhujam prasannavadanaṃ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye

Think of the one dressed in white, bright as the moon, four-armed and smiling, so that every obstacle may be stilled.

3. Prāṇāyāma प्राणायामः

Breath held while the praṇava, the seven vyāhṛtis, the Gāyatrī and its "head" are recited.

Ojo ’si and the gāyatrī with its head: the invitation completed and the breath-control mantra Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.35.53 (TA 10)

ओं भूःओं भुवःओं सुवःओं महःओं जनःओं तपःओं सत्यम्ओं तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहिधियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्ओमापो ज्योती रसोऽमृतं ब्रह्म भूर्भुवस्सुवरोम्

om bhūḥom bhuvaḥom suvaḥom mahaḥom janaḥom tapaḥom satyamom tatsaviturvareṇyaṃ bhargo devasya dhīmahidhiyo yo naḥ pracodayātomāpo jyotī raso'mṛtaṃ brahma bhūrbhuvassuvarom

The rite recites part of this unit; the translation is of the whole unit.

You are vigour and conquering power, strength and radiance; the splendour of the gods is your very name; you are the universe and its life, all things and the life of all, the one who prevails. Om — I call here the gāyatrī, the sāvitrī, Sarasvatī, the metres and the seers, and Śrī. The gāyatrī: its metre gāyatrī, its seer Viśvāmitra, its deity Savitṛ; Agni its mouth, Brahmā its head, Viṣṇu its heart, Rudra its crest, the earth its womb; bearing the five breaths, white, of the lineage of Sāṅkhyāyana; twenty-four syllables in three feet; used at the upanayana. Om earth, Om mid-space, Om heaven, Om the great, Om janaḥ, Om tapaḥ, Om the true. Om — let us contemplate the excellent radiance of the god Savitṛ, who impels our minds. Om — the waters, light, essence, the immortal: Brahman; earth, mid-space, heaven — Om.

4. Saṅkalpa सङ्कल्पः

The statement of intent: what is done, and for whom.

Saṅkalpa (midday) Formula

ममोपात्त-समस्त-दुरितक्षयद्वारा श्री-परमेश्वर-प्रीत्यर्थं माध्याह्निकं करिष्ये

mamopātta-samasta-duritakṣayadvārā śrī-parameśvara-prītyarthaṃ mādhyāhnikaṃ kariṣye

To remove every fault I have incurred, and for the pleasure of the Supreme Lord, I shall perform the midday rite.

5. Mārjana मार्जनम्

Sprinkling water on the head with the three ṛks to the Waters, then round the self with the vyāhṛtis.

Viniyoga of āpo hi ṣṭhā Viniyoga

आपो हि ष्ठेति मन्त्रस्य, सिन्धुद्वीप ऋषिः, देवी गायत्री छन्दः, आपो देवता, प्रोक्षणे विनियोगः

āpo hi ṣṭheti mantrasya, sindhudvīpa ṛṣiḥ, devī gāyatrī chandaḥ, āpo devatā, prokṣaṇe viniyogaḥ

Āpo hi ṣṭhā: seer Sindhudvīpa, metre gāyatrī, deity the Waters; used for the sprinkling.

To the Waters: you are the bringers of well-being Vedic · TS 4.1.5.1

आपो॒ हि ष्ठा म॑यो॒भुव॒स्ता न॑ ऊ॒र्जे द॑धातनम॒हे रणा॑य॒ चक्ष॑से

यो व॑श्शि॒वत॑मो॒ रस॒स्तस्य॑ भाजयते॒ह नः॑उ॒श॒तीरि॑व मा॒तरः॑

तस्मा॒ अर॑ङ्गमाम वो॒ यस्य॒ क्षया॑य॒ जिन्व॑थआपो॑ ज॒नय॑था च नः

āpo hi ṣṭhā mayobhuvastā na ūrje dadhātanamahe raṇāya cakṣase

yo vaḥśivatamo rasastasya bhājayateha naḥuśatīriva mātaraḥ

tasmā araṅgamāma vo yasya kṣayāya jinvathaāpo janayathā ca naḥ

apo-hi-stha.1 Waters, you are the source of well-being: give us strength, and the joy of seeing clearly.

apo-hi-stha.2 Give us here a share of your most kindly essence, as loving mothers give to their children.

apo-hi-stha.3 May we come fully to that essence of yours, in whose abode you delight; and, Waters, give us life and offspring.

The three vyāhṛtis Formula

ॐ भूर्भुवः॒सुवः॑

om bhūrbhuvaḥsuvaḥ

Om — earth, sky, heaven.

6. Prāśana प्राशनम्

Sipping water with a mantra that offers the faults of the night (morning), of the day (evening), or asks the Waters to purify (midday).

Viniyoga of āpaḥ punantu Viniyoga

आपः पुनन्तु इत्यनुवाकस्य, आप ऋषिः अनुष्टुप् छन्दः, ब्रह्मणस्पतिर्देवता, अपां प्राशने विनियोगः

āpaḥ punantu ityanuvākasya, āpa ṛṣiḥ anuṣṭup chandaḥ, brahmaṇaspatirdevatā, apāṃ prāśane viniyogaḥ

Āpaḥ punantu: seer the Waters, metre anuṣṭubh, deity Brahmaṇaspati; used for sipping water.

Āpaḥ punantu: the water sipped at the midday worship Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.30.48 (TA 10)

आपः पुनन्तु पृथिवीं पृथिवी पूता पुनातु माम्पुनन्तु ब्रह्मणस्पतिर्ब्रह्म पूता पुनातु माम्यदुच्छिष्टमभोज्यं यद्वा दुश्चरितं ममसर्वं पुनन्तु मामापोऽसतां च प्रतिग्रहं स्वाहा

āpaḥ punantu pṛthivīṃ pṛthivī pūtā punātu māmpunantu brahmaṇaspatirbrahma pūtā punātu māmyaducchiṣṭamabhojyaṃ yadvā duścaritaṃ mamasarvaṃ punantu māmāpo'satāṃ ca pratigrahaṃ svāhā

May the waters purify the earth, and the purified earth purify me. May they purify the teacher, the guardian of the Veda, and the purified Veda purify me. Whatever I have eaten that was left over or unfit, whatever I have done amiss, whatever I have taken from the unworthy — of all of it may the waters purify me. Svāhā.

7. Second mārjana पुनर्मार्जनम्

Sprinkling again, now with dadhikrāvṇo and the three ṛks to the Waters.

Viniyoga of dadhikrāvṇo and āpo hi ṣṭhā Viniyoga

दधिक्राव्णः इति मन्त्रस्य, वामदेव ऋषिः, अनुष्टुप् छन्दः, विश्वेदेवा (दधिक्रावा) देवताआपो हिष्ठेति मन्त्रस्य, सिन्धुद्वीप ऋषिः, देवी गायत्री छन्दः, आपो देवता, प्रोक्षणे विनियोगः

dadhikrāvṇaḥ iti mantrasya, vāmadeva ṛṣiḥ, anuṣṭup chandaḥ, viśvedevā (dadhikrāvā) devatāāpo hiṣṭheti mantrasya, sindhudvīpa ṛṣiḥ, devī gāyatrī chandaḥ, āpo devatā, prokṣaṇe viniyogaḥ

Dadhikrāvṇo: seer Vāmadeva, metre anuṣṭubh, deity the All-gods (or Dadhikrāvan). Āpo hi ṣṭhā: seer Sindhudvīpa, metre gāyatrī, deity the Waters; both used for sprinkling.

Dadhikrāvan: may he make our mouths fragrant Vedic · TS 1.5.11.4

द॒धि॒क्राव्ण्णो॑ अकारिषञ्जि॒ष्णोरश्व॑स्य वा॒जिनः॑सु॒र॒भि नो॒ मुखा॑ कर॒त्प्र ण॒ आयूँ॑षि तारिषत्

dadhikrāvṇo akāriṣañjiṣṇoraśvasya vājinaḥsurabhi no mukhā karatpra ṇa āyūṃṣi tāriṣat

I have sung the praise of Dadhikrāvan, the victorious, swift horse: may he make our mouths sweet, and lengthen our lives.

8. Āghrāṇa (aghamarṣaṇa) आघ्राणम्

Water held to the nose with drupadād iva and the three ṛks of creation, then cast away (in the Mylapore prayoga).

Viniyoga of drupadād iva Viniyoga

द्रुपदादिव मन्त्रस्य, (अमुक)ऋषिः, अनुष्टुप् छन्दः, द्रुपदा देवता, आघ्राणे विनियोगः

drupadādiva mantrasya, (amuka)ṛṣiḥ, anuṣṭup chandaḥ, drupadā devatā, āghrāṇe viniyogaḥ

Drupadād iva: seer — (not named), metre anuṣṭubh, deity Drupadā; used for holding the water to the nose.

As from a wooden stock Vedic (composite)

द्रु॒प॒दादि॑व मुञ्चतुद्रु॒प॒दादि॒वेन्मु॑मुचा॒नःस्वि॒न्नः स्वा॒त्वी मला॑दिवपू॒तं प॒वित्रेणे॒वाज्य॑म्आपः॑ शुन्धन्तु॒ मैन॑सः

drupadādiva muñcatudrupadādivenmumucānaḥsvinnaḥ svātvī malādivapūtaṃ pavitreṇevājyamāpaḥ śundhantu mainasaḥ

As a man is freed from the stocks, as one who sweats is freed from dirt by bathing, as butter is made pure by the strainer, so may the Waters cleanse me of sin.

Ambhasy apāre: the one beyond the waters, the gāyatrīs of the gods, and the mantras of the bath Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.1.13 (TA 10)

यदपां क्रूरं यदमेध्यं यदशान्तं तदपगच्छतात्अत्याशनादतीपानाद्यच्च उग्रात्प्रतिग्रहात्तन्नो वरुणो राजा पाणिना ह्यवमर्शतुसोऽहमपापो विरजो निर्मुक्तो मुक्तकिल्बिषःनाकस्य पृष्ठमारुह्य गच्छेद्ब्रह्म सलोकताम्यश्चाप्सु वरुणस्स पुनात्वघमर्षणःइमं मे गङ्गे यमुने सरस्वति शुतुद्रि स्तोमं सचता परुष्णियाअसिक्निया मरुद्वृधे वितस्तयाऽऽर्जीकीये शृणुह्या सुषोमयाऋतं च सत्यं चाभीद्धात्तपसोऽध्यजायतततो रात्रिरजायत ततः समुद्रो अर्णवः

yadapāṃ krūraṃ yadamedhyaṃ yadaśāntaṃ tadapagacchatātatyāśanādatīpānādyacca ugrātpratigrahāttanno varuṇo rājā pāṇinā hyavamarśatuso'hamapāpo virajo nirmukto muktakilbiṣaḥnākasya pṛṣṭhamāruhya gacchedbrahma salokatāmyaścāpsu varuṇassa punātvaghamarṣaṇaḥimaṃ me gaṅge yamune sarasvati śutudri stomaṃ sacatā paruṣṇiyāasikniyā marudvṛdhe vitastayā''rjīkīye śṛṇuhyā suṣomayāṛtaṃ ca satyaṃ cābhīddhāttapaso'dhyajāyatatato rātrirajāyata tataḥ samudro arṇavaḥ

Let whatever in the waters is harmful, unclean or disturbing go away. Whatever wrong comes of eating or drinking too much, or of taking gifts from dangerous people, may King Varuṇa wipe it away with his own hand. Then, sinless, stainless, set free and rid of guilt, I would climb to the summit of heaven and reach the world of Brahman. May Varuṇa, who dwells in the waters, the remover of sin, purify me. [The rivers invoked:] Gaṅgā, Yamunā, Sarasvatī, Śutudrī, Paruṣṇī; Asiknī, Marudvṛdhā, Vitastā, Ārjīkīyā, Suṣomā — come and hear my praise. [The aghamarṣaṇa, said under the water:] From the blazing heat of reflection right order and truth were born; from it the night, from it the billowing ocean.

9. Arghya अर्घ्यप्रदानम्

Water offered to the Sun from cupped hands with the Gāyatrī: three times morning and evening.

Saṅkalpa for the arghya (midday) Formula

माध्याह्निकार्घ्यप्रदानं करिष्ये

mādhyāhnikārghyapradānaṃ kariṣye

I shall offer the midday arghya.

Viniyoga of the arghya Viniyoga

अर्घ्य-प्रदान-मन्त्रस्य, विश्वामित्र ऋषिः, देवी गायत्री छन्दःसविता देवता, अर्घ्यप्रदाने विनियोगः

arghya-pradāna-mantrasya, viśvāmitra ṛṣiḥ, devī gāyatrī chandaḥsavitā devatā, arghyapradāne viniyogaḥ

The arghya mantra (the Gāyatrī): seer Viśvāmitra, metre gāyatrī, deity Savitṛ; used for offering the arghya.

The three vyāhṛtis Formula

ॐ भूर्भुवः॒सुवः॑

om bhūrbhuvaḥsuvaḥ

Om — earth, sky, heaven.

The Gāyatrī (Sāvitrī): may Savitṛ impel our minds Vedic · TS 1.5.6.4

तत्स॑वि॒तुर्वरे॑ण्य॒म्भर्गो॑ दे॒वस्य॑ धीमहिधियो॒ यो नः॑ प्रचो॒दया॑त्

tatsaviturvareṇyambhargo devasya dhīmahidhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt

We meditate on the glorious light of the divine Savitṛ: may he inspire our minds.

11. Aikyānusandhāna ऐक्यानुसन्धानम्

Meditating on the identity of the Sun and Brahman, and of Brahman and oneself.

Aikyānusandhāna: the Sun is Brahman Formula

असावादित्यो ब्रह्मब्रह्मैवाहमस्मि

asāvādityo brahmabrahmaivāhamasmi

That Sun is Brahman. I am Brahman itself.

12. Tarpaṇa देवतर्पणम्

Water offered to the nine grahas and to the twelve names.

Tarpaṇa: the nine grahas Formula

आदित्यं तर्पयामिसोमं तर्पयामिअङ्गारकं तर्पयामिबुधं तर्पयामिबृहस्पतिं तर्पयामिशुक्रं तर्पयामिशनैश्चरं तर्पयामिराहुं तर्पयामिकेतुं तर्पयामि

ādityaṃ tarpayāmisomaṃ tarpayāmiaṅgārakaṃ tarpayāmibudhaṃ tarpayāmibṛhaspatiṃ tarpayāmiśukraṃ tarpayāmiśanaiścaraṃ tarpayāmirāhuṃ tarpayāmiketuṃ tarpayāmi

I offer water to the Sun, the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rāhu and Ketu.

Tarpaṇa: the twelve names Formula

केशवं तर्पयामिनारायणं तर्पयामिमाधवं तर्पयामिगोविन्दं तर्पयामिविष्णुं तर्पयामिमधुसूदनं तर्पयामित्रिविक्रमं तर्पयामिवामनं तर्पयामिश्रीधरं तर्पयामिहृषीकेशं तर्पयामिपद्मनाभं तर्पयामिदामोदरं तर्पयामि

keśavaṃ tarpayāminārāyaṇaṃ tarpayāmimādhavaṃ tarpayāmigovindaṃ tarpayāmiviṣṇuṃ tarpayāmimadhusūdanaṃ tarpayāmitrivikramaṃ tarpayāmivāmanaṃ tarpayāmiśrīdharaṃ tarpayāmihṛṣīkeśaṃ tarpayāmipadmanābhaṃ tarpayāmidāmodaraṃ tarpayāmi

I offer water to Keśava, Nārāyaṇa, Mādhava, Govinda, Viṣṇu, Madhusūdana, Trivikrama, Vāmana, Śrīdhara, Hṛṣīkeśa, Padmanābha and Dāmodara.

13. The seat for the japa आसनम्
