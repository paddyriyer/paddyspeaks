Saluting Hari-Hara हरिहरवन्दनम्

ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma.

‹ Saluting YamaAll 29 stepsProtection from serpents ›

1 Ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma: truth, the supreme, in a person’s form Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.23.41 (TA 10)

ऋतं सत्यं परं ब्रह्म पुरुषं कृष्णपिङ्गलम्ऊर्ध्वरेतं विरूपाक्षं विश्वरूपाय वै नमो नमः

ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma puruṣaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgalamūrdhvaretaṃ virūpākṣaṃ viśvarūpāya vai namo namaḥ

Truth itself, the real, the supreme Brahman; the Person, dark and tawny, chaste, with eyes unlike any other: to him whose form is the whole universe, homage, and again homage.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.23.41 in the Upaniṣad edition.

2 The close of the Harihara-vandana Formula

वि॒श्वरू॑पाय॒ वै नम ओं नम इति

viśvarūpāya vai nama om nama iti

Salutation to the one whose form is all; Om, salutation.

Faithful English translation

To the all-formed one indeed salutation; Om, salutation — thus.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

The closing refrain after ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma (Mahānārāyaṇa 10.23), the salutation to Hari-Hara: it repeats the last words of that verse, viśvarūpāya vai namaḥ, and seals them with Om namaḥ iti. The prayoga ends several of its salutations this way (citraguptāya vai nama om nama iti, pannagebhyo ’bhirakṣatv om nama iti, śrīkeśavaṃ pratigacchaty om nama iti), repeating the end of the verse before Om namaḥ.

In the rite Step harihara-vandana, after ṛtaṃ satyam, in all three services.
