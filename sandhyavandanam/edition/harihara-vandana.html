Sandhyāvandanam · Step 25 of 29

Saluting Hari-Hara हरिहरवन्दनम्

ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma.

‹ Saluting YamaAll 29 stepsProtection from serpents ›

1 Ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma: truth, the supreme, in a person’s form Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.23.41 (TA 10)

ऋतं सत्यं परं ब्रह्म पुरुषं कृष्णपिङ्गलम्ऊर्ध्वरेतं विरूपाक्षं विश्वरूपाय वै नमो नमः

ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma puruṣaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgalamūrdhvaretaṃ virūpākṣaṃ viśvarūpāya vai namo namaḥ

Truth itself, the real, the supreme Brahman; the Person, dark and tawny, chaste, with eyes unlike any other: to him whose form is the whole universe, homage, and again homage.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.23.41 in the Upaniṣad edition.
