Sandhyāvandanam · Step 6 of 29

Prāśana प्राशनम्

Sipping water with a mantra that offers the faults of the night (morning), of the day (evening), or asks the Waters to purify (midday).

‹ MārjanaAll 29 stepsSecond mārjana ›

1 Viniyoga of sūryaś ca Viniyoga Morning

सूर्यश्चेत्यनुवाकस्य अग्निः ऋषिः, गायत्री छन्दः, सूर्यो देवता अपां प्राशने विनियोगः

sūryaścetyanuvākasya agniḥ ṛṣiḥ, gāyatrī chandaḥ, sūryo devatā apāṃ prāśane viniyogaḥ

2 Sūryaś ca mā: the water sipped at the morning worship Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.32.50 (TA 10) Morning

सूर्यश्च मा मन्युश्च मन्युपतयश्च मन्युकृतेभ्यःपापेभ्यो रक्षन्ताम्यद्रात्रिया पापमकार्षम्मनसा वाचा हस्ताभ्याम्पद्भ्यामुदरेण शिश्नारात्रिस्तदवलुम्पतुयत्किञ्च दुरितं मयिइदमहं माममृतयोनौसूर्ये ज्योतिषि जुहोमि स्वाहा

sūryaśca mā manyuśca manyupatayaśca manyukṛtebhyaḥpāpebhyo rakṣantāmyadrātriyā pāpamakārṣammanasā vācā hastābhyāmpadbhyāmudareṇa śiśnārātristadavalumpatuyatkiñca duritaṃ mayiidamahaṃ māmamṛtayonausūrye jyotiṣi juhomi svāhā

May the Sun, and anger, and the powers that rule anger guard me from the wrongs that anger brings. Whatever wrong I did in the night — in thought, in word, with hands or feet, by appetite or desire — may the night that has passed wipe it away. Whatever wrong remains in me, I offer it, and myself with it, into the source of deathlessness, the sun, the light. Svāhā.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.32.50 in the Upaniṣad edition.

3 Viniyoga of āpaḥ punantu Viniyoga Midday

आपः पुनन्तु इत्यनुवाकस्य, आप ऋषिः अनुष्टुप् छन्दः, ब्रह्मणस्पतिर्देवता, अपां प्राशने विनियोगः

āpaḥ punantu ityanuvākasya, āpa ṛṣiḥ anuṣṭup chandaḥ, brahmaṇaspatirdevatā, apāṃ prāśane viniyogaḥ

4 Āpaḥ punantu: the water sipped at the midday worship Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.30.48 (TA 10) Midday

आपः पुनन्तु पृथिवीं पृथिवी पूता पुनातु माम्पुनन्तु ब्रह्मणस्पतिर्ब्रह्म पूता पुनातु माम्यदुच्छिष्टमभोज्यं यद्वा दुश्चरितं ममसर्वं पुनन्तु मामापोऽसतां च प्रतिग्रहं स्वाहा

āpaḥ punantu pṛthivīṃ pṛthivī pūtā punātu māmpunantu brahmaṇaspatirbrahma pūtā punātu māmyaducchiṣṭamabhojyaṃ yadvā duścaritaṃ mamasarvaṃ punantu māmāpo'satāṃ ca pratigrahaṃ svāhā

May the waters purify the earth, and the purified earth purify me. May they purify the teacher, the guardian of the Veda, and the purified Veda purify me. Whatever I have eaten that was left over or unfit, whatever I have done amiss, whatever I have taken from the unworthy — of all of it may the waters purify me. Svāhā.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.30.48 in the Upaniṣad edition.

agniśetyanuvākasya, sūrya ṛṣiḥ, gāyatrī chandaḥ, agnirdevatā, apāṃ prāśane viniyogaḥ

6 Agniś ca mā: the water sipped at the evening worship Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.31.49 (TA 10) Evening

अग्निश्च मा मन्युश्च मन्युपतयश्च मन्युकृतेभ्यःपापेभ्यो रक्षन्ताम्यदह्ना पापमकार्षम्मनसा वाचा हस्ताभ्याम्पद्भ्यामुदरेण शिश्नाअहस्तदवलुम्पतुयत्किञ्च दुरितं मयिइदमहं माममृतयोनौसत्ये ज्योतिषि जुहोमि स्वाहा

agniśca mā manyuśca manyupatayaśca manyukṛtebhyaḥpāpebhyo rakṣantāmyadahnā pāpamakārṣammanasā vācā hastābhyāmpadbhyāmudareṇa śiśnāahastadavalumpatuyatkiñca duritaṃ mayiidamahaṃ māmamṛtayonausatye jyotiṣi juhomi svāhā

May Fire, and anger, and the powers that rule anger guard me from the wrongs that anger brings. Whatever wrong I did today — in thought, in word, with hands or feet, by appetite or desire — may the passing day wipe it away. Whatever wrong remains in me, I offer it, and myself with it, into the source of deathlessness, the true, the light. Svāhā.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.31.49 in the Upaniṣad edition.
