Faithful English translation

The morning Sandhyā-Gāyatrī upasthāna I shall perform.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

The saṅkalpa, the statement of intent, before the upasthāna, the closing worship standing before the deity. The witness prints the opening of the saṅkalpa (mamopātta … prītyartham, ‘by way of destroying all the faults I have incurred, for the pleasure of the Supreme Lord’) once, and then only this closing part for each time of day; the opening is said before it. The verbs are first person: the worshipper speaks for himself, and the formula is the same for every performer.

In the rite Morning service, step gayatri-upasthana, after the japa; before uttame śikhare.

Notes
Traditional Interpretation

A saṅkalpa is said with the breath held after the prāṇāyāma, or immediately after it; it names the act, the time, and the purpose. The Smārta form offers the act for the pleasure of the Supreme Lord (Parameśvara); Vaiṣṇava prayogas use other wording, which is outside this edition.

Linguistic and Textual Analysis

S writes the phrase as separate words (प्रातः सन्ध्या गायत्री उपस्थानं); in sense it is one compound, ‘the morning-sandhyā-Gāyatrī-upasthāna’. The vowels of gāyatrī and upasthānam are left unjoined in the witness.

Traditional Interpretation

M has a different wording, प्रातःसन्धोपस्थानं करिष्ये (corrections.json).

4 Viniyoga of uttame śikhare Viniyoga

उत्तमेत्यनुवाकस्य, वामदेव ऋषिः, अनुष्टुप् छन्दः, गायत्री देवता, गायत्र्युपस्थाने विनियोगः

Faithful English translation

Of the anuvāka “uttame”: Vāmadeva the seer, anuṣṭubh the metre, Gāyatrī the deity, the use in the upasthāna of the Gāyatrī.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

A viniyoga is the statement said before a mantra of its seer (ṛṣi), its metre (chandas), its deity (devatā) and the use (viniyoga) it is put to. Here: uttame śikhare devī (Mahānārāyaṇa 10.36), with which the Gāyatrī, invited before the japa, is asked to return to her mountain-top. The commas of the witness mark the pauses between the parts; a word before a comma keeps its pausal form.

In the rite Step gayatri-upasthana, before uttame śikhare, in all three services (M only).

5 Uttame śikhare: the farewell of the gāyatrī Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.36.54 (TA 10)

उत्तमे शिखरे जाते भूम्यां पर्वतमूर्धनिब्राह्मणेभ्योऽभ्यनुज्ञाता गच्छ देवि यथासुखम्स्तुतो मया वरदा वेदमाता प्रचोदयन्ती पवने द्विजाताआयुः पृथिव्यां द्रविणं ब्रह्मवर्चसं मह्यं दत्त्वा प्रजातुं ब्रह्मलोकम्

uttame śikhare jāte bhūmyāṃ parvatamūrdhanibrāhmaṇebhyo'bhyanujñātā gaccha devi yathāsukhamstuto mayā varadā vedamātā pracodayantī pavane dvijātāāyuḥ pṛthivyāṃ draviṇaṃ brahmavarcasaṃ mahyaṃ dattvā prajātuṃ brahmalokam

To the highest peak, on the summit of the mountain that rises from the earth — go, goddess, with the leave of those who worship you, wherever you are at ease. Praised by me, boon-giver, mother of the Vedas, who moves all beings as the wind does — give me life, wealth and the lustre of learning, and go to the world of Brahmā.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.36.54 in the Upaniṣad edition.
