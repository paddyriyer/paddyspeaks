Sandhyāvandanam · Step 20 of 29

Taking leave of the Gāyatrī गायत्र्युपस्थानम्

The goddess is asked to return to her place with uttame śikhare.

5 Uttame śikhare: the farewell of the gāyatrī Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.36.54 (TA 10)

उत्तमे शिखरे जाते भूम्यां पर्वतमूर्धनिब्राह्मणेभ्योऽभ्यनुज्ञाता गच्छ देवि यथासुखम्स्तुतो मया वरदा वेदमाता प्रचोदयन्ती पवने द्विजाताआयुः पृथिव्यां द्रविणं ब्रह्मवर्चसं मह्यं दत्त्वा प्रजातुं ब्रह्मलोकम्

uttame śikhare jāte bhūmyāṃ parvatamūrdhanibrāhmaṇebhyo'bhyanujñātā gaccha devi yathāsukhamstuto mayā varadā vedamātā pracodayantī pavane dvijātāāyuḥ pṛthivyāṃ draviṇaṃ brahmavarcasaṃ mahyaṃ dattvā prajātuṃ brahmalokam

To the highest peak, on the summit of the mountain that rises from the earth — go, goddess, with the leave of those who worship you, wherever you are at ease. Praised by me, boon-giver, mother of the Vedas, who moves all beings as the wind does — give me life, wealth and the lustre of learning, and go to the world of Brahmā.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.36.54 in the Upaniṣad edition.
