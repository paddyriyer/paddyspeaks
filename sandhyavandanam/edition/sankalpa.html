Saṅkalpa सङ्कल्पः

The statement of intent: what is done, and for whom.

1 Saṅkalpa (morning) Formula Morning

ममोपात्त-समस्त-दुरितक्षयद्वारा श्री-परमेश्वर-प्रीत्यर्थं प्रातः सन्ध्यामुपासिष्ये

mamopātta-samasta-duritakṣayadvārā śrī-parameśvara-prītyarthaṃ prātaḥ sandhyāmupāsiṣye

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

The other witness

  • Spelling only M: M writes the words unjoined (सन्ध्यां उपासिष्ये); S joins them (सन्ध्यामुपासिष्ये).

2 Saṅkalpa (midday) Formula Midday

ममोपात्त-समस्त-दुरितक्षयद्वारा श्री-परमेश्वर-प्रीत्यर्थं माध्याह्निकं करिष्ये

mamopātta-samasta-duritakṣayadvārā śrī-parameśvara-prītyarthaṃ mādhyāhnikaṃ kariṣye

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); the other witness does not have this text.

3 Saṅkalpa (evening) Formula Evening

ममोपात्त-समस्त-दुरितक्षयद्वारा श्री-परमेश्वर-प्रीत्यर्थं सायं सन्ध्यामुपासिष्ये

mamopātta-samasta-duritakṣayadvārā śrī-parameśvara-prītyarthaṃ sāyaṃ sandhyāmupāsiṣye

The analysis of this text is in preparation.

Source and witnesses
