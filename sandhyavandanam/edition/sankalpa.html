1 Saṅkalpa (morning) Formula Morning

ममोपात्त-समस्त-दुरितक्षयद्वारा श्री-परमेश्वर-प्रीत्यर्थं प्रातः सन्ध्यामुपासिष्ये

mamopātta-samasta-duritakṣayadvārā śrī-parameśvara-prītyarthaṃ prātaḥ sandhyāmupāsiṣye

To remove every fault I have incurred, and for the pleasure of the Supreme Lord, I shall perform the morning Sandhyā.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

The saṅkalpa, the statement of intent, for the morning service. It names the means — the removal of all the faults one has incurred — and the purpose, the pleasure of the Supreme Lord, and then the act itself. The verbs are first person: the worshipper speaks for himself, and the formula is the same for every performer.

In the rite Morning service, step sankalpa, after the prāṇāyāma.

Notes
Traditional Interpretation

A saṅkalpa is said with the breath held after the prāṇāyāma, or immediately after it; it names the act, the time, and the purpose. The Smārta form offers the act for the pleasure of the Supreme Lord (Parameśvara); Vaiṣṇava prayogas use other wording, which is outside this edition.

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

The other witness

  • Spelling only M: M writes the words unjoined (सन्ध्यां उपासिष्ये); S joins them (सन्ध्यामुपासिष्ये).
