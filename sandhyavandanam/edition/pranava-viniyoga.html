Sandhyāvandanam · Step 15 of 29

The seers, metres and deities of the prāṇāyāma प्रणवादिविनियोगः

The ṛṣi, chandas and devatā of the praṇava, the seven vyāhṛtis, the Gāyatrī and its head; then the prāṇāyāma.

1 Viniyoga of the prāṇāyāma Viniyoga

प्रणवस्य ऋषिर्ब्रह्मादेवी गायत्री छन्दःपरमात्मा देवताभूरादि-सप्त-व्याहृतीनाम् अत्रि-भृगु-कुत्स-वसिष्ठ-गौतम-काश्यप-अङ्गिरस ऋषयःगायत्री-उष्णिक्-अनुष्टुप्-बृहती-पङ्क्ति-त्रिष्टुप्-जगत्यः छन्दांसिअग्नि-वायु-अर्क-वागीश-वरुण-इन्द्र-विश्वदेवा देवताःप्राणायामे विनियोगः

praṇavasya ṛṣirbrahmādevī gāyatrī chandaḥparamātmā devatābhūrādi-sapta-vyāhṛtīnām atri-bhṛgu-kutsa-vasiṣṭha-gautama-kāśyapa-aṅgirasa ṛṣayaḥgāyatrī-uṣṇik-anuṣṭup-bṛhatī-paṅkti-triṣṭup-jagatyaḥ chandāṃsiagni-vāyu-arka-vāgīśa-varuṇa-indra-viśvadevā devatāḥprāṇāyāme viniyogaḥ

For the prāṇāyāma: the praṇava — seer Brahmā, metre gāyatrī, deity the supreme Self; the seven vyāhṛtis — seven seers, seven metres and seven deities, one for each.

Faithful English translation

Of the praṇava, Brahmā the seer, divine gāyatrī the metre, the supreme Self the deity. Of the seven vyāhṛtis beginning with bhūḥ: Atri, Bhṛgu, Kutsa, Vasiṣṭha, Gautama, Kāśyapa, Aṅgiras the seers; gāyatrī, uṣṇih, anuṣṭubh, bṛhatī, paṅkti, triṣṭubh, jagatī the metres; Agni, Vāyu, Arka, Vāgīśa, Varuṇa, Indra, the All-gods the deities. The use in the prāṇāyāma.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

A viniyoga is the statement said before a mantra of its seer (ṛṣi), its metre (chandas), its deity (devatā) and the use (viniyoga) it is put to. Here: the viniyoga of the whole prāṇāyāma mantra (Mahānārāyaṇa 10.35) — Om, the seven vyāhṛtis, the Gāyatrī and its ‘head’. S gives the praṇava and the vyāhṛtis; M adds the seer, metre and deity of the Gāyatrī and of its head (corrections.json). The seven lists run in parallel: the first seer, metre and deity belong to bhūḥ, the second to bhuvaḥ, and so on to satyam.

In the rite Step pranava-viniyoga, before the prāṇāyāma that opens the japa, in all three services.

2 Ojo ’si and the gāyatrī with its head: the invitation completed and the breath-control mantra Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.35.53 (TA 10)

ओं भूःओं भुवःओं सुवःओं महःओं जनःओं तपःओं सत्यम्ओं तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहिधियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्ओमापो ज्योती रसोऽमृतं ब्रह्म भूर्भुवस्सुवरोम्

om bhūḥom bhuvaḥom suvaḥom mahaḥom janaḥom tapaḥom satyamom tatsaviturvareṇyaṃ bhargo devasya dhīmahidhiyo yo naḥ pracodayātomāpo jyotī raso'mṛtaṃ brahma bhūrbhuvassuvarom

The rite recites part of this unit; the translation is of the whole unit.

You are vigour and conquering power, strength and radiance; the splendour of the gods is your very name; you are the universe and its life, all things and the life of all, the one who prevails. Om — I call here the gāyatrī, the sāvitrī, Sarasvatī, the metres and the seers, and Śrī. The gāyatrī: its metre gāyatrī, its seer Viśvāmitra, its deity Savitṛ; Agni its mouth, Brahmā its head, Viṣṇu its heart, Rudra its crest, the earth its womb; bearing the five breaths, white, of the lineage of Sāṅkhyāyana; twenty-four syllables in three feet; used at the upanayana. Om earth, Om mid-space, Om heaven, Om the great, Om janaḥ, Om tapaḥ, Om the true. Om — let us contemplate the excellent radiance of the god Savitṛ, who impels our minds. Om — the waters, light, essence, the immortal: Brahman; earth, mid-space, heaven — Om.

This is a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10), where its text is proven and analysed in full: padaccheda, word by word, translation, the three commentaries. Mahānārāyaṇa 10.35.53 in the Upaniṣad edition.
