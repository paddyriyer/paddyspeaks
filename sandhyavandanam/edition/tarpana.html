Tarpaṇa देवतर्पणम्

Faithful English translation

Āditya I satisfy. Soma I satisfy. Aṅgāraka I satisfy. Budha I satisfy. Bṛhaspati I satisfy. Śukra I satisfy. Śanaiścara I satisfy. Rāhu I satisfy. Ketu I satisfy.

What it says and does

The nine grahas, the ‘seizers’ — the Sun, the Moon, the five visible planets, and the two nodes of the Moon’s path, Rāhu and Ketu — each receive water with ‘I satisfy (you)’. A tarpaṇa is an offering of water that ‘satisfies’ its recipient. The Smārta prayogas of the Āpastamba school offer it to the nine grahas and then to the twelve names of Viṣṇu; some Vaiṣṇava prayogas give only the twelve.

In the rite Step tarpana, after the aikyānusandhāna and an ācamana, in all three services; water is poured from the hand for each name.

Notes
Direct Textual Meaning

Each line is the name in the accusative followed by tarpayāmi, ‘I satisfy’; the formula is the same for every recipient.

Academic History

The witnesses differ only in M’s misprint अङ्गाकरं for अङ्गारकं (corrections.json).

Source and witnesses

Base witness: S (stotrasamhita); collated with M.

The other witness

  • Witness error M: M reads अङ्गाकरं, a transposition; S and the graha’s name have अङ्गारकम् (Aṅgāraka, Mars).
  • Witness error M: The other half of M’s transposition अङ्गाकरं for अङ्गारकं.

What it says and does

The same twelve names of Viṣṇu that were said at the ācamana (anga-vandana), now in the accusative, each receiving water with ‘I satisfy’. With the nine grahas before them they make the tarpaṇa of the Sandhyāvandanam, after which the ācamana closes the first part of the rite.

In the rite Step tarpana, after the nine grahas, in all three services; then the ācamana that ends the first part (pūrvabhāga) of the rite.
