Stop guessing who has your data.We find it. You fix it.

Your personal information is out there — on data-broker sites, people-search pages and forgotten corners of the internet. This finds those exposures, verifies they are really you rather than someone who shares your name, and walks each one through to removal. You stay in control.

Your details are stored only in this browser. There is no account and no database — everything is held in this tab's local storage, and you can erase it at any time.

One exception, stated plainly: if you use Scan for me, your search terms — which include your name, and whatever address and phone you entered — are sent to this site's own server so it can run the searches your browser is not allowed to run. They are used for that request and discarded: nothing is logged, nothing is stored, nothing is cached. If you would rather that never happened, use Paste results instead — it does the same job and sends nothing.
