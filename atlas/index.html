How the Atlas works

No AI model is involved in anything you see here. The results come from a search index built from the site's own content every time the site changes. The connections come from four places, and each suggestion says which:

  • Concepts: links chosen by hand between an idea and the pages that explain or apply it. Each is checked on every build to point at something real.
  • Real links: when one page links to another in its own text.
  • Series: consecutive parts of a multi-part essay.
  • Shared tags: essays that share at least two explicit topic tags.

Live job listings are never copied into the Atlas. It hands role searches to JobSignal, which checks every role against the employer's own careers page.
