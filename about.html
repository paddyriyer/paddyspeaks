The connecting idea

The Gita opens on a battlefield it calls dharmakshetra, the field of dharma — and kurukshetra, the field of action. Nobody on it gets to stay out. The question is only how to act well inside it.

Technology changes every year. The questions underneath it change very slowly. AI can write the pipeline, draft the email and rank the candidates; it cannot carry the consequences of the choice. The person who ships it still does.

Architecture is mostly that: choosing, under uncertainty, what to build and what to refuse, and living with the result. The old texts spend most of their pages on exactly that problem. I don’t read them for predictions about AI. I read them because they are very good on judgment.

Why PaddySpeaks exists

The other question

Most technology writing asks: what should we build next? PaddySpeaks also asks: what is all this building doing to us?

AI is rewriting work, creativity and, more quietly, meaning. Most of the conversation is about what to build. I am more interested in why we build — and how to build without losing ourselves in the process. The site runs on four convictions:

  • Technology without judgment is incomplete.
  • Knowledge that is never applied becomes decoration.
  • Tradition without inquiry becomes ritual alone.
  • Building and reflecting belong in the same week, not in different careers.

Nothing here asks you to sign up or to pay. That is the engineer’s craft in service of the philosopher’s give without expecting.
