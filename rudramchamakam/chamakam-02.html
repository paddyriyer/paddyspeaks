चमकम् · Chamakam · Anuvāka 2

द्वितीयोऽनुवाकःPre-eminence, increase, truth and what is to come

Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.7.2 · 5 sense-groups for study · 114 words

✲

The stream continues with the goods of standing and growth: pre-eminence and lordship, spirit and wrath, victory, greatness and extent; truth and faith, wealth and control, play and joy; what is born and what is to be born, what has been and what will be; good roads, prosperity, fitness and understanding. Sāyaṇa reads many of the pairs as present and future — offspring born and to be born, wealth gained and to be gained — and supplies kalpatām, ‘may it be ready’, to each.

Sandhi & sandhi-viccheda

  • ज्यैष्ठ्यञ्च jyaiṣṭhyañca → ज्यैष्ठ्यम् + च

    consonant sandhi (final m)

    final m before a consonant becomes anusvāra (P. 8.3.23), written as the nasal of the following consonant's class (P. 8.4.59): m + c → ñc

  • म आधिपत्यञ्च ma ādhipatyañca → मे + आधिपत्यम्

    vowel sandhi (ayādi, y dropped)

    final e before a vowel other than a becomes ay (P. 6.1.78) and the y is dropped (P. 8.3.19), leaving the hiatus: me ādhipatyam → ma ādhipatyam

  • मन्युश्च manyuśca → मन्युः + च

    visarga sandhi

    visarga before c becomes ś (P. 8.3.34, 8.4.40)

  • मेऽमश्च me'maśca → मे + अमः

    vowel sandhi (pūrvarūpa)

    final e before a: the a is elided into the e (pūrvarūpa, eṅaḥ padāntād ati, P. 6.1.109), marked by avagraha in the text

Vyākaraṇa

  • ज्यैष्ठ्यम् jyaiṣṭhyam

    Abstract noun (taddhita) from jyeṣṭha ‘eldest, best’: ‘the state of being foremost’ (MW: = jyeṣṭha-tā).

  • आधिपत्यम् ādhipatyam

    Abstract noun from adhipati ‘overlord’, with vṛddhi of the first syllable (MW cites P. 5.1.124).

  • अम्भः ambhaḥ

    ambhas, n. s-stem, nom. sg. ambhaḥ.

Translation

LiteralPre-eminence and for me, lordship and for me, spirit and for me, wrath and for me, force and for me, water and for me [may they be ready].

In readable EnglishMay pre-eminence be mine, and lordship; may spirit be mine, and wrath, and force; and water.

Reading: corrections.json, C2.1: the second witness (W2) reads भामस् च; classified a W2 error, since visarga before c can only be ś. The text (W1) reads भामश्च.

Interpretation: ama: Monier-Williams gives ‘impetuosity, violence, strength, power’; Sāyaṇa ‘immeasurability’. ambhas: Sāyaṇa ‘water’; MW also gives ‘power, fruitfulness’ (VS, AV), which Keith’s ‘impetuosity’ reflects. The word table gives both senses.

Svara: The two witnesses differ on one accent here: W1 marks the svarita on the second syllable of भाम॑श्च, W2 has no mark. The unit is flagged ‘svara under review’; W1’s mark is kept.

Uncertain: Sāyaṇa’s sentence distinguishing manyu and bhāma, «एको मानसोऽक्षमादिलिङ्गजन्यो बाह्यः», seems to have lost a word; it is not said which of the two is the inward and which the outward anger.

Interpretation

Traditional interpretation · after Sāyaṇa

Sāyaṇa: jyaiṣṭhya is being the most excellent, ādhipatya mastery (स्वामित्वम्). Manyu and bhāma both name kinds of anger: «मन्युभामाशब्दौ क्रोधावान्तरविशेषवाचिनौ»; one is inward (मानसः), the other outward (बाह्यः), shown by signs such as impatience. Ama he takes as immeasurability: «अमोऽप्रमेयत्वं», that opponents cannot take one’s measure. Ambhas is water, cool and sweet: «शैत्यमाधुर्योपेतमुदकम्».

Vyākaraṇa

  • जेमा jemā

    jeman, mahiman, variman, prathiman and varṣman are masculine abstracts in -man; the nom. sg. ends in -ā. MW gives jeman ‘victoriousness’ (citing VS 18.4, where the Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā has its version of this litany, and TS), variman as the abstract of uru ‘wide’, prathiman of pṛthu ‘broad’.

  • द्राघुया drāghuyā

    drāghuyā has the Vedic ending -uyā of adverbs like dhṛṣṇuyā (from dhṛṣṇu), apparently from a u-stem related to dīrgha ‘long’ (compare drāghīyas ‘longer’). The word is not in Monier-Williams; here it stands as an item of the list, ‘length’.

  • वृद्धम् vṛddham

    Past participle of √vṛdh used as a neuter noun, beside the action noun vṛddhi.

Translation

LiteralVictoriousness and for me, greatness and for me, breadth and for me, extent and for me, height and for me, length and for me, what has grown and for me, growth and for me [may they be ready].

In readable EnglishMay victory be mine, and greatness, breadth and extent, height and length; may increase be mine, and growth.

Uncertain: drāghuyā: the stem and case are not certain (no headword in Monier-Williams); the sense ‘length’ follows the root and Sāyaṇa’s दीर्घत्वम्.

Interpretation: variman: Sāyaṇa derives it from ‘choosing’ (वरणीयत्वं); MW gives ‘expanse’ (the abstract of uru), which the sequence with prathiman supports. varṣman: Sāyaṇa ‘the bodies of sons and grandsons’; MW ‘height, top’ and ‘body’.

Reading: The printed bhāṣya’s lemma reads गृहिमा where the mantra has महिमा; the gloss (महत्त्वं) shows that महिमा is meant. Its gloss on prathiman also has a misprint (मृह-).

Interpretation

Traditional interpretation · after Sāyaṇa

Sāyaṇa reads these abstracts as the goods of a householder who prevails. Jeman is the capacity to win (जयसामर्थ्यम्); mahiman the wealth that victory brings («महत्त्वं जयसंपादितधनादिसंपत्तिः»); variman being worth choosing and honouring («वरणीयत्वं पूज्यत्वम्»); prathiman the extent of house and fields; varṣman the bodies of sons, grandsons and so on («पुत्रपौत्रादिशरीराणि»); drāghuyā their length, an unbroken line of descent («दीर्घत्वमविच्छिन्ना संततिः»). Vṛddha is abundant food and wealth («प्रभूतमन्नं»), vṛddhi eminence through qualities such as learning.

Bhāṣya

ऋद्धं वर्धितं धनादिकमनुष्ठितकर्मफलं वा ।

Bhāṣya

ऋद्धिरनुष्ठास्यमानसत्रफम् ।

Bhāṣya

क्लृप्तं समर्थं स्यकार्यक्षमद्रव्यम् ।

Bhāṣya

कॢप्तिः स्वकीयसामर्थ्यम् ।

Bhāṣya

मतिः पदार्थमात्रनिश्चयः ।

Bhāṣya

सुमतिदुर्घटराजकार्यादिनिश्चयः ॥

इति श्रीमत्सायणाचार्यविरचिते माधवीये वेदार्थप्रकाशे कृष्णयजुर्वेदीयतैत्तिरीयसंहिताभाष्ये चतुर्थकाण्डे सप्तमप्रपाठके द्वितीयोऽनुवाकः ॥

२ ॥
