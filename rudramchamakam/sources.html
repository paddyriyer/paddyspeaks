How the text was established

  1. Base text. W1, anuvāka by anuvāka: Namakam = TS 4.5.1–11, Chamakam = TS 4.7.1–11 (4.7.1 opens with the verse agnāviṣṇū sajoṣasā, which Sāyaṇa treats as the caturgṛhīta offering before the vasordhārā itself).
  2. Collation. W1 and W2 reduced to one phonemic skeleton (visarga ≡ doubled sibilant, any nasal before a consonant ≡ one nasal, jihvāmūlīya/upadhmānīya ≡ visarga …) and compared letter by letter. Every surviving difference is classified below; a new one fails the build.
  3. Arbitration. Where W1 and W2 differ in a letter, Sāyaṇa’s gloss and Abhinava Śaṅkara’s printed text decide; in every such case they support W1, and W2’s reading is a keying error or a convention of its line divisions. The two transcriptions are not wholly independent — both descend in part from TITUS, and at Namakam 1.13 they share a mis-keying — so agreement between them is never taken as proof on its own where the commentaries disagree.
  4. Corrections. 4 places where W1 itself is mis-keyed were corrected: in Chamakam 10 three, each supported by W2 and by W1’s own spelling elsewhere; in Namakam 1.13 one word that both transcriptions mis-key (अयक्षमया for अयक्ष्मया), corrected on the evidence of Sāyaṇa, Abhinava Śaṅkara and DCS. No other letter of W1 has been changed.
  5. Units. Namakam 1, 10 and 11 by Sāyaṇa’s ṛks (in 11 the refrain of ṛk 1 is supplied to ṛks 2–9 by anuṣaṅga, as he says; the Saṃhitā gives it twice, and so does this edition); Namakam 2–9 one yajus to each namaskāra, matching the yajus count Sāyaṇa states for each anuvāka (13, 17, 17, 15, 15, 16, 17, 19); the Chamakam, which Sāyaṇa reads as a single mantra of eleven anuvākas, in short sense-groups for study only — the Saṃhitā of each anuvāka is also given whole and unbroken.
  6. Svara. Shown as W1 marks it. W2’s marks were compared syllable by syllable: 39 syllables in 24 units differ and are flagged on their units. No accent was supplied or changed on the strength of one witness alone, except where W1 had dropped a mark W2 has and the pattern requires (Chamakam 10.3).
  7. Padaccheda. A learner’s word division, not the traditional pada-pāṭha (which none of the witnesses gives). For every anuvāka the separated words are rejoined by the rules of external sandhi and must spell the Saṃhitā exactly (scripts/rudram/check.py); junctions outside the general rules are declared and listed on their units.
  8. IAST. Generated from the Devanagari, never typed, and proven by round trip.
  9. Interpretation. The traditional note on every unit reports Sāyaṇa; every Sanskrit phrase it quotes is verified against his bhāṣya on that anuvāka. Reflections are optional, separate and labelled modern. The check rejects the vocabulary of modern esoterica in any translation or word meaning.

Open questions for scholarly review

  • Namakam 1.5 — Uncertain: sumanāḥ is a masculine/feminine nom. sg. (the neuter would be sumanaḥ, joining as *sumano ’sat), yet the subject in the sentence is the neuter jagat. Sāyaṇa refers it to the world (सौमनस्योपेतं); DCS tags it masculine. The translation follows Sāyaṇa; the agreement is not explained here.
  • Namakam 1.6 — Uncertain: yātudhānyaḥ is taken as acc. pl. f. (object of jambhayan, with sarvāḥ), as DCS also tags it; the ending -yaḥ, where -īḥ is the expected accusative of such a stem, is not explained here.
  • Namakam 1.7 — Uncertain: īmahe: MW lists it (with ava) as an intensive form of i; DCS lemmatises it under ī. The sense with ava, ‘we deprecate, appease [anger]’, is secure; the formation is not settled here.
  • Namakam 1.12 — Uncertain: niṣaṅgathi is rare and its sense is not secure. Sāyaṇa: arrow-holder (बाणाधारः), or that which can draw the sword; MW, from the lexicons: ‘embracing, an embrace; a bowman; a charioteer; the shoulder’. The translation leaves the word as niṣaṅgathi in the literal and gives ‘sheath’ in the readable.
  • Namakam 1.13 — Reading: corrections.json, N1.13 (orthographic): W1 writes परि॑ ब्भुज, the doubled bh of the Taittirīya recitation; W2 a single bh. The word is bhuja either way; the junction is declared as recitational doubling.
  • Namakam 1.13 — Reading: Both witnesses spell the instrumental अ॑यक्ष॒मया॒ (ayakṣamayā), which is not a word. The word is ayakṣmayā: Sāyaṇa «अयक्ष्मयाऽनुपद्रवकारिण्या», DCS ayakṣmayā, and the same stem as अय॒क्ष्मँ in ṛk 5. The accent mark written after क्ष looks like the anudātta of य॒ (a-ya-kṣmá-yā) displaced, with the virāma of ष् lost (Unicode क्ष + ॒ + म). The padaccheda gives अयक्ष्मया; text.json is not changed, so the editor should log a correction (proposed: अ॑य॒क्ष्मया॒) before this unit can rejoin.
  • Namakam 2.6 — Uncertain: No comment of Sāyaṇa on this yajus survives in the witness (sayana.json, N2: «अथ (अत्र टीकाभागः न लब्धः??)»), and the next comment is headed «अथ षष्ठं यजुराह— नमो रुद्रायेति», though it is the seventh yajus. The unit count (13) is Sāyaṇa’s own and is not affected.
  • Namakam 2.7 — Svara: W1 marks the syllable tā of ātatāvine anudātta (आतता॒विने॒); W2 leaves it unmarked (रु॒द्राया॑तताविने॒). The text follows W1.
  • Namakam 2.9 — Uncertain: The first member of sthapati is uncertain (Monier-Williams: ‘place-lord’, with other derivations recorded), so no samāsa analysis is given; Sāyaṇa glosses only the whole word.
  • Namakam 3.1 — Uncertain: No comment of Sāyaṇa on this yajus survives in the witness (sayana.json, N3); the word meanings rest on the grammar and Monier-Williams. āvyādhinī is a rare word (Monier-Williams: f. pl. ‘a band of robbers’).
  • Namakam 3.3 — Reading: corrections.json, N3.3: W2 reads एषुधिमते; classified as a W2 error. The stem is iṣudhi- ‘quiver’ (Namakam 1.15, Namakam 7), and the text keeps इषुधिमते.
  • Namakam 3.4 — Uncertain: No comment of Sāyaṇa on this yajus survives in the witness (sayana.json, N3); the meanings rest on the grammar and Monier-Williams (√vañc ‘to go crookedly, sneak along’; pari-√vañc ‘to sneak about’).
  • Namakam 3.7 — Reading: corrections.json, N3.7: W2 reads ञक्त- for नक्त-; classified as a W2 error (naktam ‘by night’ + carat-; ñ cannot open the word).
  • Namakam 3.7 — Svara: W1 marks the last syllable of asimadbhyaḥ anudātta (असि॒मद्भ्यो॒), the mark before an udātta: naktam carries its own accent and stands as a word. W2 marks it svarita (असि॒मद्भ्यो॑ ञक्तं॒ चर॑द्भ्यः), which leaves the first syllable of naktam unaccented, as it would be in a compound naktaṃ-carat. The text and the padaccheda follow W1, with Sāyaṇa («नक्तं चरन्तः»).
  • Namakam 4.1 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa’s gloss on the women’s forms is printed as स्रीमूर्तय, evidently for strīmūrtayaḥ ‘female forms’, and the participle as विवध्यन्त्यः for the mantra’s vividhyantyaḥ; the reading of the mantra is not in doubt.
  • Namakam 4.2 — Svara: W1 accents तृँह॒तीभ्य॑श्च (anudātta on ha, svarita on bhyaś); W2 prints the word with no marks (तृँहतीभ्यश्). The edition keeps W1’s accents; the unit is under svara review.
  • Namakam 4.2 — Uncertain: The form of ugaṇa is not secure: Sāyaṇa explains u- as ut ‘highest’ (उत्कृष्टगणरूपाः), Monier-Williams suggests a corruption of ud-gaṇa or uru-gaṇa. The samāsa entry follows Sāyaṇa.
  • Namakam 4.5 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa’s comment on this yajus is missing from the bhāṣya as printed; the meanings here rest on the dictionaries.
  • Namakam 4.7 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa’s comment on this yajus is missing from the bhāṣya as printed; the meanings here rest on the dictionaries.
  • Namakam 4.11 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa gives no gloss; kṣattṛ has several senses (carver, door-keeper, charioteer). Beside saṅgrahītṛ ‘charioteer’ the translation takes ‘attendant, door-keeper’.
  • Namakam 4.14 — Reading: puñjiṣṭa (text, Sāyaṇa) against puñjiṣṭha (Monier-Williams, from the Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā). The text is not altered; the analysis of the word as a compound is left open.
  • Namakam 4.14 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa’s gloss is printed धातकाः; the sense ‘killers’ (ghātaka) is inferred from the context and from his gloss on niṣāda in the next sentence.
  • Namakam 5.6 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa does not analyse śipiviṣṭa here; he identifies the bearer of the name. The samāsa entry follows Monier-Williams (‘pervaded by rays’), and the sense of śipi is not secure; the translation keeps the name.
  • Namakam 5.10 — Uncertain: saṃvṛdhvan has no entry in Monier-Williams. The analysis sam + √vṛdh + -van is the editor’s; Sāyaṇa gives the sense (vardhitaḥ, ‘made to grow’) but not the formation.
  • Namakam 5.13 — Uncertain: The base word Sāyaṇa names is printed शीम; whether he wrote śībha or śīma cannot be settled from the bhāṣya given.
  • Namakam 6.2 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa’s witness reads सहर्तुं in the gloss on aparaja; the sense ‘to withdraw [the world]’ assumes संहर्तुं, which the context requires. The misprint is in the commentary, not the mantra.
  • Namakam 6.3 — Uncertain: Sāyaṇa does not analyse apagalbha. Monier-Williams gives apa-galbha, ‘wanting in boldness’ (galbha ‘bold’); the prādi analysis ‘gone from boldness’ given here is the editor’s, not Sāyaṇa’s.
