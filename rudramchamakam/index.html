श्रीरुद्रम्Śrī Rudram · Namakam and Chamakam

The Rudra hymn of the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda, in the Taittirīya Saṃhitā’s own text: every word separated and explained, with Sāyaṇa’s commentary and the sources the text was checked against.

✲

The Namakam is Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.5 and the Chamakam 4.7.1–11. In the ritual they are spoken at the building of the fire altar: the Namakam as the Śatarudrīya offering to Rudra, the Chamakam as the vasordhārā, the unbroken stream of ghee, when every item of the list is asked to be “ready for me”.

This edition gives the Saṃhitā as the Taittirīya tradition writes it, with its svara, in 222 units: Sāyaṇa’s ṛks, one yajus to each namaskāra, and in the Chamakam (which is one continuous mantra) short groups for study. Each unit is set out the same way: the Saṃhitā, the separated words, the IAST, the meaning of every word, the sandhi, compounds and grammar, a literal translation, and Sāyaṇa’s interpretation — with any modern reflection labelled as such.

The text was verified before it was explained: see Sources, verification & audit for the witnesses, every correction, and the audit of the previous version of this page.

Around the recitation

These are often recited with Śrī Rudram but are not part of the Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.5 or 4.7, so they stand here, apart from the numbered anuvākas, each with its source.

Invocation · before the Namakam

ॐ नमो भगवते रुद्रायॐ नमो भगवते रुद्राय

oṃ namo bhagavate rudrāya

Om. Homage to the blessed Rudra.

Recited before the Namakam, and in the Mahānyāsa (T. M. Nārāyaṇaśāstrī’s edition, Śāradāvilāsa, Kumbakonam, as transcribed in the vishvAsa corpus: taittirIyam/sUtram/ApastambaH/gRhyam/paddhatiH/rudra-prashna-mahAnyAsaH). Not part of the Taittirīya Saṃhitā.

नमस्ते अस्तु भगवन् … · in some recitations, within the Namakam

A later prayer of praise (‘namaste astu bhagavan viśveśvarāya …’) that some traditions recite with the Namakam. It is not in the Taittirīya Saṃhitā; the previous page printed it inside Anuvāka 1. None of the witnesses used for this edition contains it, so its text is not reproduced here until it can be checked against a printed source.

Tryambaka mantra · often recited with the Rudram

त्र्य॑म्बकय्ँयजामहे सुग॒न्धिम्पु॑ष्टि॒वर्ध॑नम् ॥ उ॒र्वा॒रु॒कमि॑व॒ बन्ध॑नान्मृ॒त्योर्मु॑क्षीय॒ माऽमृता॑त् ॥त्र्यम्बकय्ँयजामहे सुगन्धिम्पुष्टिवर्धनम् ॥ उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय माऽमृतात् ॥

tryambakay̐yajāmahe sugandhimpuṣṭivardhanam || urvārukamiva bandhanānmṛtyormukṣīya mā'mṛtāt ||

We worship the three-eyed one, fragrant, increaser of prosperity. Like a cucumber from its stalk, may I be freed from death — not from immortality.

Taittirīya Saṃhitā, prapāṭhaka 1.8 (W1, the same edition as the base text). Not part of TS 4.5.11, where the previous page printed it.

इडा देवहूः … · after the Chamakam, in many recitations

इडा॑ देव॒हूर्मनु॑र्यज्ञ॒नीर्बृह॒स्पति॑रुक्थाम॒दानि॑ शँसिष॒द्विश्वे॑ दे॒वाः [3] सू॒क्त॒वाच॒ᳶ पृथि॑वि मात॒र्मा मा॑ हिँसी॒र्मधु॑ मनिष्ये॒ मधु॑ जनिष्ये॒ मधु॑ वक्ष्यामि॒ मधु॑ वदिष्यामि॒ मधु॑मतीन्दे॒वेभ्यो॒ वाच॑मुद्यासँ शुश्रू॒षेण्या॑म्मनु॒ष्ये॑भ्य॒स्तम्मा॑ दे॒वा अ॑वन्तु शो॒भायै॑ पि॒तरोऽनु॑ मदन्तु ॥इडा देवहूर्मनुर्यज्ञनीर्बृहस्पतिरुक्थामदानि शँसिषद्विश्वे देवाः [3] सूक्तवाचᳶ पृथिवि मातर्मा मा हिँसीर्मधु मनिष्ये मधु जनिष्ये मधु वक्ष्यामि मधु वदिष्यामि मधुमतीन्देवेभ्यो वाचमुद्यासँ शुश्रूषेण्याम्मनुष्येभ्यस्तम्मा देवा अवन्तु शोभायै पितरोऽनु मदन्तु ॥

iḍā devahūrmanuryajñanīrbṛhaspatirukthāmadāni śam̐siṣadviśve devāḥ sūktavācaḫ pṛthivi mātarmā mā him̐sīrmadhu maniṣye madhu janiṣye madhu vakṣyāmi madhu vadiṣyāmi madhumatīndevebhyo vācamudyāsam̐ śuśrūṣeṇyāmmanuṣyebhyastammā devā avantu śobhāyai pitaro'nu madantu ||

Iḍā who calls the gods, Manu who leads the sacrifice, Bṛhaspati shall recite the hymns of praise; the All-gods are the speakers of the hymns. Mother Earth, do not harm me. Sweet shall I think, sweet shall I bring forth, sweet shall I bear, sweet shall I speak; may I utter honeyed speech to the gods, worth hearing to men. May the gods protect me for splendour; may the fathers rejoice after me.

Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.3.2 (W1, the same edition as the base text). Not part of TS 4.7.

Śānti · at the close

ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिःॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः

oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ

Om. Peace, peace, peace.

The customary close of a Vedic recitation. Not part of TS 4.5 or 4.7.
