Telugu · Bhakti · c. 1620–1688

The Keertanas of Bhadrachala Ramadasu

A revenue officer who spent the state’s money on a temple, went to prison for twelve years, and came out having written some of the most-sung verse in the Telugu language. Each song its own page: Telugu and transliteration, word meanings, ragam and talam, and an esoteric reading.
