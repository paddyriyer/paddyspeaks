What changes on the site

The changelog records meaningful changes: sacred-text additions and corrected verses, Interview Studio features, JobSignal improvements, new demos, privacy and security work. Follow it at /changelog.xml. Factual corrections are also listed on the corrections page.

Job alerts

JobSignal alerts save a search in your browser and show you what is new each time you come back. They live only in your browser: there is no account, and nothing is emailed.

Interview Studio updates

New Skill Check questions are added every weekday. What’s New lists the latest questions and community submissions.

Email

There is no email newsletter yet, so there is nowhere to leave your address. That is deliberate: a sign-up form that led nowhere would be worse than none. If one is added, it will be announced in the changelog, it will be double opt-in, and it will be covered by the privacy policy before it collects a single address.
