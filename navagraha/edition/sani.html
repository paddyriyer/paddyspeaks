Navagraha Sūktam · Step 7 of 10

Śanaiścara (Saturn) शनैश्चरः

The mantra of Śanaiścara, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.
