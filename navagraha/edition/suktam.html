1. Sūrya सूर्यः

The mantra of Sūrya, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Savitṛ comes on his golden chariot Vedic · TS 3.4.11.2

आ स॒त्येन॒ रज॑सा॒ वर्त॑मानो निवे॒शय॑न्न॒मृत॒म्मर्त्य॑ञ्चहि॒र॒ण्यये॑न सवि॒ता रथे॒ना दे॒वो या॑ति॒ भुव॑ना वि॒पश्य॑न्

ā satyena rajasā vartamāno niveśayannamṛtammartyañcahiraṇyayena savitā rathenā devo yāti bhuvanā vipaśyan

Moving through the abiding sky, setting the immortal and the mortal each in its place, Savitṛ the god comes on his golden chariot, watching over every world.

We choose Agni as messenger Vedic · TB 3.5.2

अ॒ग्निं दू॒तं वृ॑णीमहेहोता॑रं वि॒श्ववे॑दसम्अ॒स्य य॒ज्ञस्य॑ सु॒क्रतु॑म्

agniṃ dūtaṃ vṛṇīmahehotāraṃ viśvavedasamasya yajñasya sukratum

We choose Agni to be our messenger and to call the gods: he knows all things, and he is skilled in the work of this sacrifice.

The lord of the beasts lets this one go Vedic · TS 3.1.4.1

येषा॒मीशे॑ प॒शु॒पतिः॑ पशू॒नाञ्चतु॑ष्पदामु॒त च॑ द्वि॒पदा॑म्निष्क्री॑तो॒ऽयय्ँ य॒ज्ञिय॑म्भा॒गमे॑तु रा॒यस्पोषा॒ यज॑मानस्य सन्तु

yeṣāmīśe paśupatiḥ paśūnāñcatuṣpadāmuta ca dvipadāmniṣkrīto'yaṃyajñiyambhāgametu rāyaspoṣā yajamānasya santu

This beast belongs to Paśupati, lord of all creatures with four feet and with two; now it is released from him — let it go to its place in the sacrifice, and let the sacrificer’s wealth increase.

adhidevatā pratyadhidevatā sahitāya ādityāya namaḥ

Homage to Āditya and to the deities who attend him.

2. Aṅgāraka (Mars) अङ्गारकः

The mantra of Aṅgāraka, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Agni, the head of heaven Vedic · TS 4.4.4.1

अ॒ग्निर्मू॒र्धा दि॒वः क॒कुत्पतिः॑ पृथि॒व्या अ॒यम्अ॒पाँ रेताँ॑सि जिन्वति

agnirmūrdhā divaḥ kakutpatiḥ pṛthivyā ayamapāṃ retāṃsi jinvati

Agni is the summit of heaven and the lord of the earth; it is he who quickens the seed in the waters.

Syonā pṛthivi: be kindly, Earth Vedic · Mahānārāyaṇa 10.1.10 (TA 10)

स्योना पृथिवि भवा नृक्षरा निवेशनीयच्छा नः शर्म सप्रथाः

syonā pṛthivi bhavā nṛkṣarā niveśanīyacchā naḥ śarma saprathāḥ

The rite recites part of this unit; the translation is of the whole unit.

Brahman, born first in the east and longed for by all, opened up the shining worlds from their bounds, and laid open the deep quarters near at hand, the dwellings of this world, the source of what is and of what is not. Be kind to us, Earth; you bring forth people and give them a place to live; give us shelter, wide one. [Over the earth taken for the bath:] I call here Śrī, known by her fragrance, unshakeable, ever-nourished, rich in cattle, mistress of all beings. May fortune come to me; may misfortune leave me. The gods, led by Viṣṇu, won these worlds with the metres, never to be won back. May great Indra, thunderbolt in hand, full like the sixteen-digit moon, give us shelter.

With the lord of the field as a friend Vedic · TS 1.1.14

क्षेत्र॑स्य॒ पति॑ना व॒यँ हि॒तेने॑व जयामसिगामश्व॑म्पोषयि॒त्न्वा स नः॑ मृ॒डा॒ती॒दृशे॑

kṣetrasya patinā vayaṃ hiteneva jayāmasigāmaśvampoṣayitnvā sa naḥ mṛḍātīdṛśe

With the Lord of the Field as our friend we win cattle and horses and food that makes us thrive; may he be kind to us in all of this.

adhidevatā pratyadhidevatā sahitāya aṅgārakāya namaḥ

3. Śukra (Venus) शुक्रः

The mantra of Śukra, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Bring the offering to the bright one Vedic · TB 2.8.2

प्र व॑श्शु॒क्राय॑ भा॒नवे॑ भरध्वम्ह॒व्यम्म॒तिञ्चा॒ग्नये॒ सुपू॑तम्यो दैव्या॑नि॒ मानु॑षा ज॒नूँषि॑अ॒न्तर्विश्वा॑नि वि॒द्मना॒ जिगा॑ति

pra vaḥśukrāya bhānave bharadhvamhavyammatiñcāgnaye supūtamyo daivyāni mānuṣā janūṃṣiantarviśvāni vidmanā jigāti

Offer to Agni, bright and radiant, your purified offering and your prayer — to him who passes knowingly among all the generations of gods and men.

Indrāṇī, whose husband does not age Vedic · TS 1.7.13.1

इ॒न्द्रा॒णीमा॒सु नारि॑षु सु॒पत्नी॑म॒हम॑श्रवम्न ह्य॒स्या अप॒रञ्च॒न ज॒रसा॑ मर॑ते॒ पतिः॑

indrāṇīmāsu nāriṣu supatnīmahamaśravamna hyasyā aparañcana jarasā marate patiḥ

Of all women, I have heard, Indrāṇī is the most fortunate in her husband, for he never grows old and dies.

We call Indra for you, above all peoples Vedic · TS 1.6.12

इन्द्रव्ँ॑वो वि॒श्वत॒स्परि॒ हवा॑महे॒ जने॑भ्यःअ॒स्माक॑मस्तु॒ केव॑लः

indraṃvo viśvataspari havāmahe janebhyaḥasmākamastu kevalaḥ

We call Indra for you, above every people: may he be ours, and ours alone.

4. Soma (the Moon) सोमः

The mantra of Soma, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Swell, Soma Vedic · TS 4.2.7.4

आ प्या॑यस्व॒ समे॑तु ते वि॒श्वत॑स्सोम॒ वृष्णि॑यम्भवा॒ वाज॑स्य सङ्ग॒थे

ā pyāyasva sametu te viśvataḥsoma vṛṣṇiyambhavā vājasya saṅgathe

Grow full, Soma; let strength flow into you from every side, and be with us where strength is gathered.

Soma told me: in the waters are all remedies Vedic · TB 2.5.8

अ॒प्सु मे॒ सोमो॑ अब्रवीत्अ॒न्तर्विश्वा॑नि भेष॒जाअ॒ग्निञ्च॑ वि॒श्वश॑म्भुवम्आप॑श्च वि॒श्वभे॑षजीः

apsu me somo abravītantarviśvāni bheṣajāagniñca viśvaśambhuvamāpaśca viśvabheṣajīḥ

Soma has told me that within the waters lie all remedies — and Agni, who blesses all — and that the waters are a remedy for all things.

Gaurī lowed, shaping the waters Vedic · TB 2.4.6

गौ॒री मि॑माय सलि॒लानि॒ तक्ष॑तीएक॑पदी द्वि॒पदी॒ सा चतु॑ष्पदीअ॒ष्टाप॑दी॒ नव॑पदी बभू॒वुषी॑स॒हस्रा॑क्षरा पर॒मे व्यो॑मन्

gaurī mimāya salilāni takṣatīekapadī dvipadī sā catuṣpadīaṣṭāpadī navapadī babhūvuṣīsahasrākṣarā parame vyoman

The bright cow lowed and shaped the waters. She became one-footed, two-footed, four-footed, eight-footed, nine-footed — a thousand syllables in the highest heaven.

5. Budha (Mercury) बुधः

The mantra of Budha, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Wake, Agni, and keep watch over him Vedic · TS 4.7.13.5

उद्बु॑ध्यस्वाग्ने॒ प्रति॑ जागृह्येनमिष्टापू॒र्ते सँसृ॑जेथाम॒यञ्च॑पुन॑ः कृ॒ण्वँस्त्वा॑ पि॒तरय्ँ॒युवा॑नम॒न्वाताँ॑सी॒त्त्वयि॒ तन्तु॑मे॒तम्

udbudhyasvāgne prati jāgṛhyenamiṣṭāpūrte saṃsṛjethāmayañcapunaḥ kṛṇvaṃstvā pitaraṃyuvānamanvātāṃsīttvayi tantumetam

Wake, Agni, and keep watch over him; together with him bring about what is offered and what is given. Making you young and a father again, he has drawn this thread of the rite through you.

Viṣṇu strode out this world Vedic · TS 1.2.13.1

इ॒दव्ँविष्णु॒र्वि च॑क्रमे त्रे॒धा नि द॑धे प॒दम्समू॑ढमस्य पाँ॒सु॒रे

idaṃviṣṇurvi cakrame tredhā ni dadhe padamsamūḍhamasya pāṃsure

Viṣṇu strode out across this world; three times he set down his foot, and all of it is held in the dust of his footprint.

You are Viṣṇu’s brow Vedic · TS 1.2.13.3

विष्णो॑ र॒राट॑मसि॒ विष्णोः॑ पृ॒ष्ठम॑सि॒ विष्णो॒श्श्ञप्त्रे॑ स्थो॒ विष्णो॒स्स्यूर॑सि॒ विष्णो॑र्ध्रु॒वम॑सि वैष्ण॒वम॑सि॒ विष्ण॑वे त्वा

viṣṇo rarāṭamasi viṣṇoḥ pṛṣṭhamasi viṣṇoḥśnaptre stho viṣṇoḥsyūrasi viṣṇordhruvamasi vaiṣṇavamasi viṣṇave tvā

You are Viṣṇu’s brow, you are Viṣṇu’s back, you two are Viṣṇu’s corners, you are Viṣṇu’s seam, you are Viṣṇu’s fixed knot; you belong to Viṣṇu — to Viṣṇu, you.

6. Bṛhaspati (Jupiter) बृहस्पतिः

The mantra of Bṛhaspati, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Bṛhaspati, give us that bright wealth Vedic · TS 1.8.22.2

बृह॑स्पते॒ अति॒ यद॒र्यो अर्हा॑द्द्यु॒मद्वि॒भाति॒ क्रतु॑म॒ज्जने॑षुयद्दी॒दय॒च्छव॑सा ऋ॒त॒प्र॒जा॒त॒ तद॒स्मासु॒ द्रवि॑णन्धेहि चि॒त्रम्

bṛhaspate ati yadaryo arhāddyumadvibhāti kratumajjaneṣuyaddīdayacchavasā ṛtaprajāta tadasmāsu draviṇandhehi citram

Bṛhaspati, born of the sacred order: the wealth that a noble man most deserves, that shines with splendour and power among men, that blazes with strength — that bright wealth, give it to us.

Indra with the Maruts, drink the soma here Vedic · TS 1.4.18.1

इन्द्र॑ मरुत्व इ॒ह पा॑हि॒ सोमय्ँ॒यथा॑ शार्या॒ते अपि॑बस्सु॒तस्य॑तव॒ प्रणी॑ती॒ तव॑ शूर॒ शर्म॒न्ना वि॑वासन्ति क॒वय॑स्सुय॒ज्ञाः

indra marutva iha pāhi somaṃyathā śāryāte apibaḥsutasyatava praṇītī tava śūra śarmannā vivāsanti kavayaḥsuyajñāḥ

Indra, with your Maruts, drink the soma here, as you drank the pressed soma at the sacrifice of Śaryāti’s son. Under your guidance and in your shelter, hero, the wise who sacrifice well come to serve you.

Brahman, born first in the east Vedic · TS 4.2.8.2

ब्रह्म॑ जज्ञा॒नम्प्र॑थ॒मम्पु॒रस्ता॒द्वि सी॑म॒तस्सु॒रुचो॑ वे॒न आ॑वःस बु॒ध्निया॑ उप॒मा अ॑स्य वि॒ष्ठास्स॒तश्च॒ योनि॒मस॑तश्च॒ विवः॑

brahma jajñānamprathamampurastādvi sīmataḥsuruco vena āvaḥsa budhniyā upamā asya viṣṭhāḥsataśca yonimasataśca vivaḥ

Brahman was born first, in the east; the Bright One opened the shining rays from the horizon. He laid bare the deep forms of this world and their places — he uncovered the womb of what is and of what is not.

7. Śanaiścara (Saturn) शनैश्चरः

The mantra of Śanaiścara, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

May the divine Waters be a blessing Vedic · TB 1.2.1

शन्नो॑ दे॒वीर॒भिष्ट॑येआपो॑ भवन्तु पी॒तये॑शय्ँयोर॒भि स्र॑वन्तु नः

śanno devīrabhiṣṭayeāpo bhavantu pītayeśaṃyorabhi sravantu naḥ

May the divine Waters be a blessing to us, for help and for drinking; may they flow to us with welfare and health.

Prajāpati, none but you encompasses all these Vedic · TS 1.8.14.2

प्रजा॑पते॒ न त्वदे॒तान्य॒न्यो विश्वा॑ जा॒तानि॒ परि॒ ता ब॑भूवयत्का॑मास्ते जुहु॒मस्तन्नो॑ अस्तु व॒यँ स्या॑म॒ पत॑यो रयी॒णाम्

prajāpate na tvadetānyanyo viśvā jātāni pari tā babhūvayatkāmāste juhumastanno astu vayaṃ syāma patayo rayīṇām

Prajāpati, none other than you encompasses all that has been born. What we desire as we offer to you, may it be ours; may we be lords of riches.

Yama, come and sit on the strewn grass Vedic · TS 2.6.12.6

इ॒मय्ँय॑म प्रस्त॒रमा हि सीदाङ्गि॑रोभिः पि॒तृभि॑स्सव्ँविदा॒नःआ त्वा॒ मन्त्राः॑ कविश॒स्ता व॑हन्त्वे॒ना रा॑जन्‌ह॒विषा॑ मादयस्व

imaṃyama prastaramā hi sīdāṅgirobhiḥ pitṛbhiḥsaṃvidānaḥā tvā mantrāḥ kaviśastā vahantvenā rājanhaviṣā mādayasva

Come, Yama, sit on this grass we have spread, together with the Aṅgirases, the Fathers. Let the words the poets recite bring you to us; take delight, O king, in this offering.

adhidevatā pratyadhidevatā sahitāya śanaiścarāya namaḥ

8. Rāhu राहुः

The mantra of Rāhu, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

With what help will the bright one come to us? Vedic · TS 4.2.11

कया॑ नश्चि॒त्र आ भु॑वदू॒ती स॒दावृ॑ध॒स्सखा॑कया॒ शचि॑ष्ठया वृ॒ता

kayā naścitra ā bhuvadūtī sadāvṛdhaḥsakhākayā śaciṣṭhayā vṛtā

With what help will he come to us, the bright one, our friend who is always growing — with what mighty power?

The dappled bull has stepped forth Vedic · TS 1.5.3.1

आऽयङ्गौः पृश्ञि॑रक्रमी॒दस॑नन्मा॒तर॒म्पुनः॑पि॒तर॑ञ्च प्र॒यन्त्सुवः॑

ā'yaṅgauḥ pṛśnirakramīdasananmātarampunaḥpitarañca prayantsuvaḥ

The bright, dappled bull has come striding out: he has reached his mother, the Earth, once more, and goes on to his father, the shining Sky.

I loose the noose that Nirṛti bound Vedic · TS 4.2.5.3

यत्ते॑ दे॒वी निर्ऋ॑तिरा ब॒बन्ध॒ दाम॑ ग्री॒वास्व॑विच॒र्त्यम्इ॒दन्ते॒ तद्वि ष्या॒म्यायु॑षो॒ न मध्या॒दथा॑ जी॒वः पि॒तुम॑द्धि॒ प्रमु॑क्तः

yatte devī nirṛtirā babandha dāma grīvāsvavicartyamidante tadvi ṣyāmyāyuṣo na madhyādathā jīvaḥ pitumaddhi pramuktaḥ

The noose that cannot be loosed, that the goddess Nirṛti tied about your neck — I untie it for you. Now, freed and alive, eat your food.

Homage to Rāhu Formula

9. Ketu केतुः

The mantra of Ketu, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

Making a banner for the bannerless Vedic · TS 7.4.20.1

के॒तुङ्कृ॒ण्वन्न॑के॒तवे॒ पेशो॑ मर्या अपे॒शसे॑समु॒षद्भि॑रजायथाः

ketuṅkṛṇvannaketave peśo maryā apeśasesamuṣadbhirajāyathāḥ

You give a sign to what has none and form to what is formless, O men: you were born with the dawns.

Soma passes beyond the purifying filter Vedic · TS 3.4.11.1

ब्र॒ह्मा दे॒वाना॑म्पद॒वीः क॑वी॒नामृषि॒र्विप्रा॑णाम्महि॒षो मृ॒गाणा॑म्श्ये॒नो गृ॑ध्राणाँ॒ स्वधि॑ति॒र्वना॑नाँ॒ सोमः॑ प॒वित्र॒मत्ये॑ति॒ रेभ॑न्

brahmā devānāmpadavīḥ kavīnāmṛṣirviprāṇāmmahiṣo mṛgāṇāmśyeno gṛdhrāṇāṃ svadhitirvanānāṃ somaḥ pavitramatyeti rebhan

Among the gods the priest, among poets the path-finder, among the inspired the seer, the buffalo among beasts, the falcon among hunting birds, the axe among the trees — Soma runs on through the filter, singing.

Bright one, give us bright wealth Vedic · RV 6.6.7

स चित्र चित्रं चितयन्तमस्मे चित्रक्षत्र चित्रतमं वयोधाम्चन्द्रं रयिं पुरुवीरं बृहन्तं चन्द्र चन्द्राभिर्गृणते युवस्व

sa citra citraṃ citayantamasme citrakṣatra citratamaṃ vayodhāmcandraṃ rayiṃ puruvīraṃ bṛhantaṃ candra candrābhirgṛṇate yuvasva

Bright god, bright in your rule, bring us wealth that shines and is seen, the brightest, full of strength; gleaming wealth, rich in sons and great — O shining one, give it to the one who praises you with shining hymns.

10. Close उपसंहारः

The nine are saluted together, and the recitation ends with śānti.

Homage to the nine Formula

ॐ आदित्यादि नवग्रहदेव॑ताभ्यो॒ नमो॒ नमः॑

om ādityādi navagrahadevatābhyo namo namaḥ

Om. Homage, homage to the nine grahas, with the Sun the first of them.

Om. Peace, peace, peace.
