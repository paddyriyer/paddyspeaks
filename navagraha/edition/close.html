1 Homage to the nine Formula

ॐ आदित्यादि नवग्रहदेव॑ताभ्यो॒ नमो॒ नमः॑

om ādityādi navagrahadevatābhyo namo namaḥ

Om. Homage, homage to the nine grahas, with the Sun the first of them.

Faithful English translation

Om. To the deities, the nine grahas beginning with Āditya, homage, homage.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

The salutation that closes the sūktam: after each graha has been honoured in turn, the nine are saluted together as deities, with Āditya, the Sun, named first. The words name no other deity and add nothing to the nine salutations before them; their work is to gather the recitation into one act.

In the rite Step 10 (close), first formula, before the śānti.

2 Peace, peace, peace Formula

Om. Peace, peace, peace.

Word for word; words supplied for English are in [brackets].

What it says and does

The close of the recitation: Om, and peace said three times. The formula is the common ending of a Vedic recitation, and nothing in the witness gives it a meaning special to the grahas; the edition says no more than the words do — peace, three times over.

In the rite Step 10 (close), the last words of the sūktam.
