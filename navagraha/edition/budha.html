Budha (Mercury) बुधः

The mantra of Budha, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

The Ṛgveda (Śākala recension, GRETIL, no svara)

इदं विष्णुर्वि चक्रमे त्रेधा नि दधे पदम् । समूऌहमस्य पांसुरे — RV 1.22.17

Corrections to the base witness

  • → े: In the Saṃhitā the ṛk runs on into the next (पाँसुर इरावती, -e before i becoming -a); the sūktam recites it alone, so its last word stands in its pausal form, पाँसुरे (as in RV 1.22.17).

3 budha-3 Vedic · TS 1.2.13.3

विष्णो॑ र॒राट॑मसि॒ विष्णोः॑ पृ॒ष्ठम॑सि॒ विष्णो॒श्श्ञप्त्रे॑ स्थो॒ विष्णो॒स्स्यूर॑सि॒ विष्णो॑र्ध्रु॒वम॑सि वैष्ण॒वम॑सि॒ विष्ण॑वे त्वा

viṣṇo rarāṭamasi viṣṇoḥ pṛṣṭhamasi viṣṇoḥśnaptre stho viṣṇoḥsyūrasi viṣṇordhruvamasi vaiṣṇavamasi viṣṇave tvā

Cut from TS 1.2.13.3 (base witness W1; collated with W2) by scripts/prayoga/import.py.

The other witness

  • Witness error W2: W2 reads श्न्यप्त्रे; W1 has श्ञप्त्रे, the Taittirīya spelling of śnaptre (the two corners of the cart’s frame).
  • Spelling only W2: W2 writes the word-final -as of sthas before v unjoined (स्थस् विष्णोः), Weber’s practice; joined it is स्थो विष्णोः.
