Navagraha Sūktam · Step 9 of 10

Ketu केतुः

The mantra of Ketu, then two more for the deities who attend it (the witness names the adhidevatā and pratyadhidevatā together, not which mantra is whose), then the salutation.

1 ketu Vedic · TS 7.4.20.1

के॒तुङ्कृ॒ण्वन्न॑के॒तवे॒ पेशो॑ मर्या अपे॒शसे॑समु॒षद्भि॑रजायथाः

ketuṅkṛṇvannaketave peśo maryā apeśasesamuṣadbhirajāyathāḥ
