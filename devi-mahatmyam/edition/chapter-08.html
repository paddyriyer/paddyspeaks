रक्तबीजवधः

raktabījavadhaḥ

The Slaying of Raktabīja · Text only — analysis in preparation

Recited before the chapter (liturgical, from the Gita Press pāṭha)

These lines belong to the recitation, not to the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa text, and are kept apart from the numbered mantras. They are given here in Sanskrit and IAST only.

Dhyāna

ॐ अरुणां करुणातरङ्गिताक्षीं
धृतपाशाङ्कुशबाणचापहस्ताम्।
अणिमादिभिरावृतां मयूखै-
रहमित्येव विभावये भवानीम्॥

ॐ aruṇāṃ karuṇātaraṅgitākṣīṃ
dhṛtapāśāṅkuśabāṇacāpahastām|
aṇimādibhirāvṛtāṃ mayūkhai-
rahamityeva vibhāvaye bhavānīm||
