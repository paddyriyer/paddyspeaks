niśumbhavadhaḥ

The Slaying of Niśumbha · Text only — analysis in preparation

Recited before the chapter (liturgical, from the Gita Press pāṭha)

These lines belong to the recitation, not to the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa text, and are kept apart from the numbered mantras. They are given here in Sanskrit and IAST only.

Dhyāna

ॐ बन्धूककाञ्चननिभं रुचिराक्षमालां
पाशाङ्कुशौ च वरदां निजबाहुदण्डैः।
बिभ्राणमिन्दुशकलाभरणं त्रिनेत्र-
मर्धाम्बिकेशमनिशं वपुराश्रयामि॥

ॐ bandhūkakāñcananibhaṃ rucirākṣamālāṃ
pāśāṅkuśau ca varadāṃ nijabāhudaṇḍaiḥ|
bibhrāṇaminduśakalābharaṇaṃ trinetra-
mardhāmbikeśamaniśaṃ vapurāśrayāmi||
