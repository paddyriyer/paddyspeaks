How to read this edition

Every verse is set out in the same seven parts:

  1. The original śloka in Devanagari, exactly as recited, with its Gita Press mantra number.
  2. Padaccheda — the verse again with every word separated (नमः | अस्तु for नमोऽस्तु). Compounds stay whole here; they are taken apart in part 5.
  3. IAST — the recited line, and the separated words.
  4. Word by word — each separated word with its meaning in the verse and its grammar.
  5. Vyākaraṇa — the sandhi of the verse with Pāṇini's rule, its compounds with their vigraha, notable forms, and the anvaya (prose order).
  6. Translation — a literal one that follows the grammar, and a readable one.
  7. Commentary, every paragraph labelled: Direct textual meaning Traditional interpretation Comparative Śākta / Śrīvidyā Modern reflection.

Proven, not trusted. The separated words are not just typed: the build rejoins them by the rules of sandhi and fails unless they spell the recited verse exactly. The transliteration is generated from the Devanagari, so the two cannot differ. Each chapter's counts of uvāca lines, half-ślokas and ślokas match its own colophon. Where the digital text was wrong it was corrected and logged; where other witnesses read differently, the Gita Press reading is kept and the difference recorded (Source & notes).
