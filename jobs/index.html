What the badge actually means

“Posted” tells you what a website claims. “Verified” tells you what we checked. A role is marked verified when the employer’s own applicant tracking system still lists the requisition — the same feed their careers page is built from. When a role disappears from that feed twice in a row, we close it and move it to the archive instead of leaving a dead listing up. We never tell you a job is fake. We show you the dates, the requisition number, where the apply button goes, and how many times this role has been posted before. You decide.
